---
title: The Fox X-Men films
oneLine: Twenty years of films about mutants, made by a different studio under a separate licence, with no connection to the shared run until the multiverse opened.
---

For two decades these were simply a different set of films about different characters, with their own continuity and their own contradictions. Watching them requires nothing from the connected run and gives nothing back to it, until very late, when the multiverse made both true at once.
