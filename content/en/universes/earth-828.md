---
title: The retro-futurist Earth
oneLine: A 1960s that never ended, where the Fantastic Four are four years into being famous and the rest of the shared story has not happened at all.
---

This is a Marvel Studios film in the same run as the others, set in a universe that is not the one the run takes place in. The choice was made for a reason that is easy to check and hard to argue with: putting the team on their own Earth means nobody has to explain where they were when half of the connected universe disappeared.

What you get instead is a version of the 1960s that kept going, with the technology people in 1964 expected to have by now. Nothing here requires anything from the shared continuity, which makes it one of the easiest entries in the catalogue to start from.
