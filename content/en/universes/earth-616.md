---
title: The main continuity
oneLine: The connected run of films and series made by Marvel Studios, where almost everything happens and everything else is measured against it.
---

This is the one people mean when they say the shared universe. It began in 2008 with a single film that had no guarantee of a second, and it now carries the majority of the catalogue. If a title belongs here, it can assume you have seen the ones before it, and later ones will assume you have seen it.

:::detail{title="616 or 199999, and why this catalogue says 616"}
Both numbers are real and the disagreement is worth knowing about.

*The Official Handbook of the Marvel Universe A to Z* gave the film universe
the designation **Earth-199999**, assigned from the comics side before the
films had said anything about it. Sony still uses it: in *Spider-Man: Across
the Spider-Verse*, Miguel O'Hara complains about "Doctor Strange and the little
nerd back on Earth-199999", meaning this one.

The films call themselves **Earth-616**, and they have done it five times over
eleven years. The term is on Erik Selvig's blackboard in *Thor: The Dark
World*. Mysterio says the main continuity is Earth-616 in *Spider-Man: Far From
Home*. Ant-Man's van sits in a storage bay labelled 616 in *Endgame*. In
*Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*, Christine Palmer of Earth-838
tells Strange that the Illuminati named his universe Earth-616. And in
*Deadpool & Wolverine*, Wade Wilson travels from Earth-10005 to Earth-616.

This catalogue is about the films, so it uses the name the films use. It is
worth adding that one of those five is Mysterio, who spends that entire film
lying, and another is a character from a different Earth reporting what her
Illuminati called it. Neither is quite the same as the franchise stating its
own address.
:::
