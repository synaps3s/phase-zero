---
title: Outside any shared story
oneLine: Marvel films that belong to no continuity at all, made before or beside everything else.
---

Blade, the older Fantastic Four films, Ghost Rider, Elektra, the Punisher films. They are included here and marked as separate rather than left out, because their characters keep re-entering the connected run and because a guide that only covers the connected films cannot explain where any of this came from.
