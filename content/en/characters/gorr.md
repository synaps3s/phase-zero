Gorr is the last of his people and the last prophet of their god. His daughter
dies of thirst in a desert while he prays. When he finally reaches Rapu he
finds a god who is comfortable, well fed, and openly amused that anyone
believed the prayers were being heard.

The Necrosword is lying nearby. It is a weapon that kills gods and consumes the
person holding it, and Gorr picks it up in the one moment of his life when that
trade looks reasonable. Christian Bale plays the whole thing at a whisper,
which is why the character survives a film that is otherwise in a very good
mood.

:::spoiler{title="The wish"}
He gets to Eternity, where any wish is granted, having spent the film promising
to end every god there is. Thor and Jane Foster reach him first and free him
from the sword. He wishes for his daughter back instead, and dies having seen
her once.
:::
