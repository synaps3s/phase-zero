Yondu is hired by Ego to collect his children from across the galaxy. He does
it many times. Then he collects Peter Quill, works out what happens to the ones
he delivers, and stops, which gets him thrown out of the Ravager clans and
leaves him raising a boy he is not equipped to raise.

Michael Rooker plays him as somebody who has decided that being loved would be
worse than being useful. The second film gives him the line the whole character
was built for, and it is not a long line.

:::spoiler{title="Vol. 2"}
He dies saving Quill from Ego, giving him the only spacesuit. The funeral is
attended by every Ravager clan that exiled him, which is the film telling you
what they thought of the decision he made decades earlier.
:::
