Adrian Toomes has the best motive of any villain in these films because it is
paperwork. His company is hired to clear the alien wreckage out of New York
after the Battle of New York, he takes on debt to do the job properly, and then
the Department of Damage Control is created and the work is taken from him. He
keeps a truckload of Chitauri technology and goes into business for himself.

Michael Keaton plays him as a working man with a crew to feed, which makes the
car scene late in the film one of the most frightening things in the series. He
is not threatening a superhero. He is a father, driving his daughter's date to
the dance, working out who is in the seat behind him.

:::detail{title="The cameo nobody has explained"}
Keaton appears as Toomes in *Morbius*, a Sony film outside the Marvel Studios
continuity, in a mid-credits scene where he arrives in that universe and
proposes teaming up. No later film has followed it up, and no source here
explains how the two universes are meant to connect. It is listed because it
happened, not because it makes sense.
:::
