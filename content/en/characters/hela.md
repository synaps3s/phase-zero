Hela is Odin's first child and his executioner. The two facts are the same
fact. Asgard's golden age was conquered, realm by realm, by the pair of them,
and when her ambition ran past his use for it he imprisoned her and had the
murals painted over.

Her return is not an invasion. It is an inheritance. She walks into the throne
room, breaks Thor's hammer with one hand, and is legally correct about all of
it, which is the most uncomfortable thing about her. What she destroys in the
process includes the Warriors Three and, in the end, a good part of the idea
that Asgard had of itself.

Cate Blanchett plays her enjoying every minute, and the film is smart enough to
let the joke and the horror share a scene.

:::spoiler{title="How Ragnarok ends"}
She cannot be beaten while Asgard stands, because Asgard is what feeds her. So
Thor stops trying to save the place and lets the prophecy run: Surtur destroys
the realm and Hela with it, and the Asgardians leave on a ship. The film's
answer is that Asgard was never the ground.
:::
