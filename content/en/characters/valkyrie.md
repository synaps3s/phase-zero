Valkyrie is the only member of her order left alive. The rest died in a single
engagement against Hela, and the film shows it once, in slow motion, and then
never lets her talk about it. When we meet her she is calling herself Scrapper
142, selling captured fighters on Sakaar, and drinking.

Tessa Thompson plays the recovery without ever making it a lesson. By *Endgame*
she is running a refugee town in Norway, and by *Love and Thunder* she has the
worst part of the job, which is the meetings.
