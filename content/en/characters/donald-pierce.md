Donald Pierce is the cybernetically enhanced leader of the Reavers and the
head of security for Alkali-Transigen, the company that bred the children
in *Logan*. Boyd Holbrook plays him. He is the one who tracks X-23, which
makes him the face of the pursuit for most of the film.

:::spoiler{title="The children"}
He is killed by the other children from the program, the ones Laura was
trying to reach, who turn their powers on him together.
:::
