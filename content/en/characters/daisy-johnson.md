Daisy Johnson was born in China to Calvin Johnson and his Inhuman wife Jiaying,
and was taken by S.H.I.E.L.D. agents as an infant and raised as an orphan by
nuns. Under the name Skye she became a hacktivist who worked against
organisations like S.H.I.E.L.D., which is how Coulson finds her and recruits
her, and has Ward and then May train her into a field agent.

She is the reason to watch *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* at all. Seven seasons is
long enough to actually change somebody, and Chloe Bennet's arc from a girl
living in a van to Quake is the longest continuous character journey in this
catalogue, film or television.

:::spoiler{title="What she finds"}
The search for her parents ends with the Inhumans, with Terrigenesis, and with
a mother who would rather have a weapon than a daughter. Her powers cost her
the family she spent her life looking for.
:::
