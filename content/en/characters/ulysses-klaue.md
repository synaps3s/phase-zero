Ulysses Klaue is the character both of his films use as a key: he is the only
outsider who has stolen from Wakanda and lived, which makes him proof that the
country's whole security policy has already failed once.

Andy Serkis plays him as a man having a wonderful time, which is rare in these
films and is why the Busan sequence works. His arm is a Wakandan mining tool he
turned into a weapon, which is the joke of the character in one object.
