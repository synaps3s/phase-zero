Frigga is the reason Loki can do what he does: the illusions are hers, and she
taught them to the adopted son rather than to the heir. That single fact does
more work than most of what these films say out loud about that family.

Her scene in *Endgame* is the best writing any Asgardian gets. Thor arrives
from the future in pieces, and she recognises him, and rather than being
comforted she does the comforting, and refuses to be told how she dies. Rene
Russo plays it without a single raised voice.

:::spoiler{title="How it ends"}
She is killed in 2013 by Algrim, defending Jane Foster in the palace. Thor's
visit in *Endgame* happens on the day she dies, and she works that out before
he says it.
:::
