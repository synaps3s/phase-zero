Harvey Elder, called the Mole Man, is a former enemy turned ally of the
Fantastic Four and the human ruler of Subterranea, an underground society whose
people call themselves Moloids. Paul Walter Hauser plays him.

He matters in *First Steps* out of proportion to his screen time because he is
the film's proof of method. The Fantastic Four are not written as people who
win fights. They are written as people who talk to whoever is on the other
side, and the Mole Man is who that is tested on first.

:::detail{title="Two projects"}
Wikipedia records him in two: the film and the one-shot comic
*Fantastic Four: First Steps*, both from 2025.
