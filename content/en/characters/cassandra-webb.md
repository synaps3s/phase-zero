Madame Web is the least liked entry on this shelf, and the premise is not the
reason. A paramedic with no interest in any of this begins seeing the immediate
future, which is a small and specific power, and the film is about her trying to
use something that unreliable to keep three strangers alive.

Dakota Johnson plays it with a flatness that has been read as both the film's
worst problem and its only defence. It is listed here because it is part of the
record.
