Yelena Belova spent three years in Ohio as a child, in a house with two
handlers playing her parents and an older girl playing her sister, and was the
only one of the four who thought it was real. Everything about the character
comes from that: the humour, the directness, and the complete refusal to let
anybody be sentimental about it in front of her.

She is also the only person in this catalogue who talks about the Red Room as
an ongoing problem rather than as a backstory. Natasha Romanoff escaped it.
Yelena went back for everyone else.
