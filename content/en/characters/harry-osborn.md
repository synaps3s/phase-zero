Harry is the trilogy's slowest fuse and its best structural idea. He is Peter's
friend, Norman's disappointment, and eventually the person holding the same
equipment his father used. James Franco plays the resentment as something that
was there long before the mask.

The third film has to carry too much, but the part it gets right is this one:
the reconciliation happens too late and costs too much, which is how the
character was always going to end.
