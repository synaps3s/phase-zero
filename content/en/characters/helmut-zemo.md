Helmut Zemo is the most effective villain in these films and he never throws a
punch. He is a Sokovian baron and a colonel of an elite commando unit, and his
family is killed in the battle the Avengers fight against Ultron in Sokovia.
He does not blame Ultron.

What he does instead is study them. He finds the record of Hydra's Winter
Soldier programme, he frames Bucky Barnes for a bombing in Vienna to get the
location out of the authorities, and then he arranges for Tony Stark to watch
footage of Barnes killing Stark's parents while Steve Rogers is standing in the
room. Everything after that happens by itself.

Daniel Brühl plays him quietly, which is what makes the last scene land. He is
asked whether he really thought he could kill them. He says he never wanted
to.

:::detail{title="A villain the sequel had to keep"}
By the end of *Civil War* Zemo has succeeded completely and is in custody,
which is normally where a Marvel villain stops. *The Falcon and the Winter
Soldier* takes him out of the cell instead and makes him a source, because the
question the series is asking, what a super soldier is for, is the one question
he has thought about longer than anyone.
:::
