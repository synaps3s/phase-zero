Ronan is a fanatic whose family died in the Kree-Nova war, which the films
mention and then decline to explore. What he is for, in practice, is scale: he
is the thing standing between the Guardians and each other, and the reason four
people who cannot stand one another end up holding hands.

He is also the catalogue's first demonstration of what an Infinity Stone does to
somebody unprepared, which is the lesson *Infinity War* spends three hours
collecting on.
