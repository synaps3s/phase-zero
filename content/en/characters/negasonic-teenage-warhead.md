Negasonic Teenage Warhead is a young mutant who can generate atomic bursts
around her body, and a member of the X-Men. She spends most of her first film
refusing to look up from her phone, which is a joke that works because Colossus
keeps trying to make her care.

She matters here for a reason that has nothing to do with the fighting. She and
Yukio are together across two films, treated as ordinary and never explained,
which was not common in a superhero film of that size in 2018.

:::detail{title="Where the name comes from"}
It is taken from a Monster Magnet song, and the films know it is absurd. The
character in the comics is a different person with different powers, and the
film versions keep only the name and the attitude.
:::
