Peter Porker is a talking animal Spider-Man from a cartoon-based
anthropomorphic universe, who was once a spider that bit a pig. John Mulaney
voices him.

The reason he works is that the film lets his rules come with him. He pulls a
mallet out of the air in a world where that should not be possible, and the
film does not explain it, because explaining it would be admitting the other
Spider-People are the real ones.

:::detail{title="Which Earth this is"}
The films never say the number out loud. The Marvel Database files this
version under Earth-8311B, with a suffix it uses to keep the film apart from the
comics' Earth-8311, and describes the page as covering Earth-8311 as the films
depict it. The number is shown here without the suffix, which is the wiki's
own bookkeeping rather than anything on screen.
:::
