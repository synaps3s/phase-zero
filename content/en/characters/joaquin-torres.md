Joaquin Torres is a member of the United States Air Force and Sam Wilson's
best friend, born in Mexico and raised in Miami from the age of six. Danny
Ramirez plays him. He lived through the five years of the Blip and is the one
who tells Wilson what he missed. In *The Falcon and the Winter Soldier* he
brings Wilson intelligence on the Flag Smashers, meets Bucky Barnes, and, when
Wilson takes on the mantle of Captain America, is given the Falcon's wingsuit
and the name that goes with it.

*Brave New World* puts him in the air. He and Wilson intercept an illegal sale
of adamantium in Oaxaca, and as a reward Wilson introduces him to Isaiah
Bradley. When President Ross is attacked at the White House, the two of them
investigate a hidden site in West Virginia called Camp Echo One, and discover
that Samuel Sterns had used mind control on Bradley. At Celestial Island in the
Indian Ocean they stop two rogue pilots from firing on the Japanese fleet and
starting a war.

:::spoiler{title="The cost, and the invitation"}
He is badly injured at Celestial Island. While he is recovering, Wilson asks
him to join the Avengers.
:::
