Miles Morales is an intelligent and rebellious teenager of African-American and
Puerto Rican descent who gets spider-like abilities and becomes the Spider-Man
of Earth-1610 after his reality's Peter Parker dies.

What separates him from every other Spider-Man in this catalogue is the order
of events. The others become Spider-Man and then find out what it costs. Miles
sees the cost first, in the street, and takes the job anyway.

:::detail{title="Where these films sit"}
The Spider-Verse films are animated, made by Sony Pictures Animation, and this
catalogue files them in their own band. They reach into the other continuities
constantly and none of the others reach back, which is why they are recorded as
unbound rather than connected.
:::
