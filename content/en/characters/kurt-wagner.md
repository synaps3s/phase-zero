Kurt Wagner is a German mutant with blue skin, a prehensile tail, demonic
features and the ability to teleport. The opening of *X2* is one of the best
sequences the series ever produced: he goes through the White House in a series
of bamfs, and it takes the film another twenty minutes to explain that he did
not want to.

Storm and Jean find him afterwards in a church, which is where he was always
going to be. He is a believer who has been told his whole life that he looks
like the opposite, and Alan Cumming plays that as gentleness rather than
self-pity.

:::detail{title="Two Kurts, and a third appearance"}
Alan Cumming played him in *X2* and Kodi Smit-McPhee in the prequel films, in
which he is much younger. Cumming is set to reprise the role in
*Avengers: Doomsday*.
:::
