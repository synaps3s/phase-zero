Kate Bishop watched the Battle of New York from the window of a building that
was coming apart around her, saw a man on a roof with a bow, and decided that
day what she was going to be. Everything about the character since has been her
being extremely good at it and nobody wanting her to do it.

She is written as the opposite of her mentor in every respect that matters. He
is quiet, tired, and would like to be finished. She is loud, twenty-two and
cannot believe her luck, and the series is largely about him deciding whether
to let her stay.
