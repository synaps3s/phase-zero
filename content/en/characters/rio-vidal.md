Rio Vidal is the name Death uses when she is passing as a green witch, and
Aubrey Plaza plays her in *Agatha All Along*. Wikipedia's article on the
character records that she is one of the four cosmic entities who created
the Infinity Stones, alongside Eternity, Infinity and Entropy, and that her
identity was never announced before the series: Plaza was cast in an unnamed
role, the truth was telegraphed through the episodes, and it was not
confirmed until the seventh, "Death's Hand in Mine". The reader who sent
this list called her the green witch who is later revealed to be Lady
Death, which is the shape of it.

Some time before 1750 Death, as Rio, entered a relationship with Agatha
Harkness, whom she met while collecting the dead from one of Agatha's
killing sprees. When Agatha's son Nicholas was six, Death took him, and the
showrunner Jac Schaeffer has said the writers talked at length about
whether Rio was his other parent before deciding it was irrelevant to the
story. Agatha used the Darkhold to hide from her afterwards.

:::spoiler{title="The Road, and the garden"}
Death tracks Agatha to Westview in 2026, appears inside Wanda's spell as an
FBI agent called Rio Vidal, and tells the Salem Seven where to find her.
When the coven summons a green witch to the Road, Death crawls out of Sharon
Davis's grave and walks it with them, incognito, through the second and
third trials. She stays behind in the third to guide Alice to the
afterlife. When Agatha helps Billy leave the Road without surrendering to
her, Death confronts her in the garden in Westview; Agatha chooses to
sacrifice herself, kisses her, and dies of it. Death sheds Rio's appearance
to show Billy her true form before telling him to go.
:::

:::detail{title="Statues, and a rumour"}
The same article records that Death has been depicted in-universe before
this: a stylised relief at the start of *Guardians of the Galaxy*, and a
statue at the climax of *Thor: Love and Thunder*. Those are images of her,
not appearances, so they are not in her list. It also records that the
Russo brothers explained in 2018 why they had kept her out of *Infinity
War* and *Endgame*, and that it has been rumoured she will appear again.
:::
