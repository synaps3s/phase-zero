Darren Cross was Hank Pym's protégé, and *Ant-Man* is about what happens when
the protégé works out what the mentor was hiding. In 2015 he tries to recreate
the Pym particle formula, the thing that lets a person shrink, and to sell it
at a black-market auction. Scott Lang, Pym and Pym's daughter Hope van Dyne
stop him, which stings twice over, because Hope was Cross's own protégée and
the person he had hoped would be more than that. Corey Stoll plays him.

He puts on the Yellowjacket suit for the final fight and loses it in the worst
way available: shrunk unevenly to subatomic size and dropped into the Quantum
Realm.

:::spoiler{title="M.O.D.O.K."}
*Quantumania* finds him there, mutated and cybernetically rebuilt
into a creature that is mostly head. The name is an acronym, Mechanized
Organism Designed Only for Killing, and he works for Kang the Conqueror until
he does not. Before the end he turns on Kang and makes his peace with Lang, Pym
and Hope, and dies having done so.
:::
