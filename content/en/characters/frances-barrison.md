Frances Barrison, called Shriek, is Cletus Kasady's lover and can produce sonic
screams. Andy Serkis described her as a damaged soul who has spent her life in
isolation, with a dark side to her. Olumide Olorunfemi plays her as a child.

The film's best idea is that her power and Carnage's are incompatible: sound
hurts symbiotes, so the love story is built to destroy itself. It is a neater
piece of construction than the rest of the film manages.

:::detail{title="One appearance"}
She appears only in *Venom: Let There Be Carnage*, in the Sony universe rather
than the connected one.
:::
