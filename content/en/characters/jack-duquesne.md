Jacques Duquesne is a wealthy socialite and a highly skilled fencer. With his
uncle Armand III he attends the black market auction of items stolen from the
Avengers compound in 2024 and takes Ronin's retractable sword. His fiancée
Eleanor Bishop frames him for his uncle's murder at Wilson Fisk's request, and
he later helps Kate Bishop against the Tracksuit Mafia.

*Hawkeye* spends five episodes inviting you to suspect him and then reveals
that he is exactly what he looks like: a slightly ridiculous man who is very
good with a sword and fond of his fiancée's daughter.
