Drake is the name the film uses; the vampires who dig him up in the Syrian
Desert at the start of *Blade: Trinity* believe the tomb is Dracula's, and
the Marvel Database files him under that name for this universe. Dominic
Purcell plays him. He is the first of the vampires, and the point of reviving
him is that he can survive in sunlight, which the Talos siblings hope to use
to cure the rest of their kind.

He does not think much of them. He tells Blade that humans and vampires alike
are inferior in his eyes and that he intends to wipe both out, and he goes
out of his way to isolate Blade from the Nightstalkers because he wants to
test him. A pure blood source is also exactly what the Nightstalkers need to
make their virus, Daystar, work against the whole species.

:::spoiler{title="The end of the trilogy"}
Blade and Drake fight and Drake wins, until Abigail Whistler fires the
Daystar arrow. Drake catches it, and the distraction is enough for Blade to
stab him with it, which completes the virus and releases it, killing the
vampires around them. Dying, Drake praises Blade for fighting honourably,
warns him that his need for blood will get him in the end, and calls him the
future of the vampire race. With the last of his power he shape-shifts into
Blade so that Blade can walk out. The FBI recovers the body; on the autopsy
table it turns back into Drake.
:::
