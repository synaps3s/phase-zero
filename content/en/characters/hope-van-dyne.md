Hope van Dyne is the daughter of Hank Pym and Janet van Dyne, a senior board
member of her father's company, and the most qualified person in the room for
the entire first film. She is also not allowed to do the job, for reasons that
turn out to be about her father's grief rather than about her.

The second film corrects it by putting her name in the title, and the character
improves immediately. She is better at the work than Scott Lang, she knows it,
and the films stop pretending that is a source of tension and let it be a fact.

:::detail{title="A suit that was already hers"}
The Wasp identity comes from her mother. Janet van Dyne wore it first, went
into the Quantum Realm on a mission in the 1980s and did not come back for
thirty years, and Hank Pym's refusal to let his daughter near a suit is a
direct consequence.

That makes the handover in the second film something other than a promotion. It
is an inheritance that had been withheld.
:::
