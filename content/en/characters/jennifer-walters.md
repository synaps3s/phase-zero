Jennifer Walters is Bruce Banner's cousin, and the joke the series is built on
is that she gets the same condition and it is simply less of a problem for her.
She keeps her mind, she keeps her job, and what she loses is the ability to be
taken seriously as a lawyer rather than as an attraction.

Tatiana Maslany plays her talking directly to the audience, which the series
uses for more than comedy: it lets her point at the parts of her own show that
are unfair to her. It is a legal comedy that keeps being interrupted by being a
Marvel series, and by the end it says so out loud.
