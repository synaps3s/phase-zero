Ben Urich is an investigative journalist for the *New York Bulletin*,
struggling with the lack of interest in the crime pieces that made him
successful when he was young, and with a sick wife he can barely afford to
keep in hospital or move to a home. Vondie Curtis-Hall plays him in the
first season of *Daredevil*. He agrees to work with Karen Page on her
investigation into Wilson Fisk, which is the story of the season.

:::spoiler{title="The apartment"}
Fisk learns that Urich met his mother while investigating him, breaks into
his apartment and strangles him. The reader who sent this list described
him as the journalist who got killed by the man he was trying to expose,
which is exactly it. Karen takes up the work he was doing.
:::

:::detail{title="Why he dies"}
Wikipedia records that the decision to kill Urich was Marvel's, made before
the showrunner Steven DeKnight joined. The point was to have Fisk do
something truly terrible late in the season, when the audience had begun
to sympathise with him, and to signal that being beloved in the comics
kept nobody safe. In DeKnight's account, Urich commits the one unforgivable
sin in Fisk's mind: he went to Fisk's mother.
:::
