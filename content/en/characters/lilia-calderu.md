Lilia Calderu is a 450-year-old Sicilian witch and a member of Agatha
Harkness's coven. Patti LuPone plays her, and Chloe Camp plays her young.
Wikipedia's article on Death records that Death, in non-human form,
appeared to Lilia when she was a young woman in the 1500s. The reader who
sent this list described her as the premonition witch who hid Billy's true
identity from everyone, which is the kind of thing a divination witch
knows and does not say.

:::spoiler{title="The fourth trial"}
After Agatha, Jennifer Kale and Billy Maximoff escape the fourth trial,
Lilia decides to stay behind as the Salem Seven close in. She flips one of
the cards, the entire room turns upside down, and the Seven are impaled,
and presumably she is too.
:::
