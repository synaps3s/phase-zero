Quentin Beck is the first character in these films to weaponise the audience.
He is an illusions specialist who worked at Stark Industries, and he arrives
in a world that has just watched half its population return and would very much
like a straightforward hero. So he provides one, complete with an origin from
another universe, and creates the monsters he is seen defeating.

What makes him land is that the film gives him a crew: engineers and designers
Stark also discarded, standing in a warehouse admiring their own work. It is
the most convincing depiction of resentment in the series.

:::spoiler{title="What he does on the way out"}
Beck dies, but he has already arranged the rest. His edited footage frames
Spider-Man for the attack in London and reveals Peter Parker's name to the
world, which is the thing the next film has to spend its entire running time
undoing.
:::
