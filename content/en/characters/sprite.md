Sprite can project lifelike illusions, and she is stuck in a permanent
childlike state, appearing as a young girl despite being thousands of years
old. She often uses her power to appear as an adult woman called Sandra.

Hers is the cruellest arrangement in the film and the one it is most careful
with. She has an adult's mind and seven millennia of experience and nobody has
ever treated her as anything but a child, and she cannot be touched, loved or
taken seriously.

:::spoiler{title="What Sersi does for her"}
At the end Sersi uses what is left of the Uni-Mind to make Sprite human, which
ends the permanent childhood and starts the clock. It is offered as a kindness
and the film knows it is also a death sentence.
:::
