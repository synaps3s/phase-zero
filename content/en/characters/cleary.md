P. Cleary is an agent of the Department of Damage Control, and Arian Moayed
plays him in three projects. In *Spider-Man: No Way Home* he leads the
interrogations of Peter Parker and his friends and family once Mysterio has
outed Peter as Spider-Man. By *Ms. Marvel* he has forgotten Parker ever
existed, courtesy of Stephen Strange's spell, and is taking an interest in
an incident at AvengerCon in New Jersey involving Kamala Khan; he sends
Agent Sadie Deever, and after the incident and a confrontation with Khan he
removes her from the mission and from her position.

In *Wonder Man* he hires Trevor Slattery to spy on the actor Simon Williams,
who is suspected of having superpowers, on the grounds that Hollywood has a
Doorman Clause forbidding people with powers from major roles. When
Slattery fails, Cleary imprisons him, until he learns Williams has freed
him; ionic energy was detected at the blast site, and Cleary suggests
Williams could be a valuable resource.
