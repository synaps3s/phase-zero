Kaecilius broke from the Ancient One's teaching after a loss, and what he wants
is not power but the end of death. Dormammu offers eternal life, and Kaecilius
and his zealots take the offer without reading what eternal means in the Dark
Dimension.

Mads Mikkelsen plays him with more grief than menace, which makes the ending
land: he gets what he was promised.

:::spoiler{title="What he was promised"}
When Strange defeats Dormammu with the time loop, the Dark Dimension pulls back
and takes Kaecilius and his followers with it. They become Mindless Ones. He
does live forever.
:::
