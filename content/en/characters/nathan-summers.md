Cable is the straight man the second *Deadpool* film needed. Josh Brolin plays
him with no jokes at all, which is why the film can keep making them. He has
come back from a future where a boy called Russell burns everything, and he
intends to prevent it in the simplest available way.

The film's decision to give him a photograph, and to make the whole thing about
a family he cannot get back, is the reason the character is not just a
mechanism.
