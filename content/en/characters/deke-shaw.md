Deke Shaw is described by his own series as the ultimate survivor and a roguish
scavenger, living on the space station Lighthouse in the year 2091, sharp and
quick and able to get you anything for a price. Jeff Ward plays him.

He is the clearest sign of what *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* became in its later
seasons: a show that had stopped waiting for the films to give it permission
and started running time travel, alternate timelines and a 1930s period season
on its own authority.

:::spoiler{title="How he gets here"}
During the final fight with the Kree Kasius, Enoch and Deke give their lives to
send Coulson's team back to their own time. Deke arrives in the present
regardless, which the series treats as a joke and then takes seriously.
:::
