Alexei is a comic character carrying the film's saddest idea. He was the Soviet
Union's super soldier, then he was placed in Ohio with a fake wife and two
assigned children for a three-year intelligence operation, and those three years
were the best of his life. He talks about them constantly. The two women he
talks to were the children, and for them it was not a cover story, it was
childhood.

David Harbour plays the comedy at full volume, which is the correct decision,
because it means the moment where he is asked what he thinks he was to them
arrives without any warning at all.
