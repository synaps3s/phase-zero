Pietro Maximoff is Wanda's twin brother, played by Aaron Taylor-Johnson, with
Gabriel Gurevich as the boy he was. The two of them are introduced
as Hydra assets who volunteered to be experimented on with the Mind Stone, and
came out of it changed: Wanda with her powers, Pietro with speed. They hate
Tony Stark, whose bombs killed their parents, and that is why they start the
film on Ultron's side.

He is in the series for one film, and the reception was lukewarm, with the
common complaint that neither he nor his power was developed enough, either
against the comics or against the version Fox had put on screen a year
earlier. This catalogue files that one separately, under his other name, Peter
Maximoff.

:::spoiler{title="Sokovia"}
In the final fight with Ultron he dies saving Clint Barton and a Sokovian boy,
which is the film's one real cost and the thing Wanda carries into everything
after it.
:::

:::detail{title="The other Pietro"}
In *WandaVision* a Pietro turns up at Wanda's door, played by Evan Peters, who
had played Fox's Quicksilver. For a few episodes it looked like the two film
series had just joined hands. They had not: he is Ralph Bohner, a resident of
Westview forced by Agatha Harkness to impersonate Pietro to get close to Wanda
inside her invented town. Wikipedia counts the series among the character's
appearances on that basis, and so does this entry, with the actor's note saying
what he was actually doing.
:::
