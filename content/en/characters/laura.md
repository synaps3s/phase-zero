Laura is Wolverine's daughter, created by Zander Rice at Alkali-Transigen from
his DNA implanted into a woman. She has two claws in each hand and one in each
foot, an advanced healing factor, and, for the first half of *Logan*, almost no
English at all: she is fluent in Spanish and simply does not speak to people who
have not earned it.

Dafne Keen's performance is the reason *Logan* works. The film hands its
emotional weight to a child who is mostly silent, and it holds.

:::spoiler{title="Logan, and afterwards"}
Transigen schedules the children for termination and the nurses help them
escape towards Eden. Gabriela puts Laura in Logan's care, and Xavier is killed
by X-24 on the road. When X-24 fatally wounds Logan, Laura shoots him with an
adamantium bullet, buries Logan, and crosses into Canada with the others.

In *Deadpool & Wolverine* she has been pruned into the Void, where she fights
alongside Elektra, Blade and Gambit against Cassandra Nova. She kills Juggernaut
and Psylocke, and is freed when Nova falls.
:::
