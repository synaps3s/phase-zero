Colossus exists in the *Deadpool* films to be sincere at somebody who cannot
handle sincerity. He is patient, Russian, enormous, and genuinely trying to make
Wade Wilson join the X-Men, and the joke works because he never gives up and
never gets angry.

Three actors have played him across the run, which is easy to miss because he is
computer generated from *Deadpool* onwards. Stefan Kapičić is the voice most
people mean.
