James Rhodes has the least comfortable position in the Iron Man films and the
one the story never quite resolves. He is Stark's best friend and he is also
the officer the United States Air Force has assigned to Stark Industries, and
those two jobs give opposite answers to almost every question the films ask.

He is the one who flies into Afghanistan to find Stark rather than sending
somebody. He is also the one who eventually decides the armour cannot belong to
one man, takes a suit off the floor of the workshop, and hands it to the
military. The films treat both as loyalty, which is the interesting part.

By *Captain America: Civil War* he is the loudest voice for oversight, and the
film costs him more for it than anyone else pays for anything. He is hit by his
own side, by accident, in a scene about a disagreement between friends.

:::detail{title="Two actors and one first film"}
Terrence Howard played Rhodes in *Iron Man* in 2008. Don Cheadle has played him
in everything since. This catalogue records the current portrayer, which means
the entry's first appearance is the one film that actor is not in.

The article has a second inconsistency worth knowing about: its own summary
says Rhodes has appeared in seven films, and its list of appearances names
eight, including a mid-credits scene in *Captain Marvel*. This catalogue lists
the eight the article documents.
:::
