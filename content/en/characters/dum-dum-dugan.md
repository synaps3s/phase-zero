Timothy "Dum Dum" Dugan is a member and leader of the Howling Commandos, who
fought alongside Steve Rogers, Bucky Barnes and Peggy Carter during the
Second World War. Neal McDonough plays him, in four projects that between
them stretch across the film and television sides of the early MCU: *The
First Avenger*, the Marvel One-Shot *Agent Carter*, *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*
and the *Agent Carter* series. For the first film McDonough wore the
character's signature bowler hat and, he said, grew the moustache himself.
