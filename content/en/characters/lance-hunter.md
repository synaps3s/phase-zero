Lance Hunter is an SAS lieutenant turned mercenary who joins the rebuilt
S.H.I.E.L.D. at Coulson's request, on a recommendation from his ex-wife Bobbi
Morse. Despite a difficult relationship with her he becomes a full-time agent
and risks his life to get her back when she is taken. Nick Blood plays him.

He is the show's way of having somebody in the room who thinks the whole
operation is absurd, and it needs one.

:::detail{title="Where he goes"}
After an incident in Russia the two of them leave the agency. A spin-off built
around Hunter and Morse was developed and never ordered, which is why their
story ends where it does.
:::
