Nick Fury is the connective tissue of the whole thing, and the films are honest
that this is a job rather than a virtue. He recruits, he withholds, he shows up
uninvited, and every relationship he has is partly operational.

His origin is not a tragedy. In 1995 he is a mid-level agent doing paperwork
when an alien war lands on his desk, and by the end of that week he has met
somebody who can fly through a spaceship and has decided the planet needs more
of them. The Initiative is named after her call sign, which he chooses while
she is still in the room.

He is also the character the story is most willing to make wrong. Project
Insight is his. So is the assumption that the agency he built could not have
been hollowed out from the inside, and it takes the Winter Soldier putting him
through a wall to prove otherwise.

:::detail{title="Born on the fourth of July"}
The article gives his date of birth as 4 July 1950, in Huntsville, Alabama.
Steve Rogers was born on 4 July 1918.

There is no in-story significance to this and no character mentions it. It is
the kind of coincidence a catalogue notices and a film would be wise to leave
alone.
:::
