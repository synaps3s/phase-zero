Dane Whitman is a human who teaches history at the Natural History Museum in
London and is seeing Sersi. He spends the film as the person things are
explained to, which is a job somebody has to do in a story with ten immortals
in it.

:::spoiler{title="The last scene"}
After the credits he opens a case containing the Ebony Blade, an heirloom with
his family name on it, and a voice off screen asks whether he is ready for it.
The voice is Blade. Neither of them has appeared since.
:::
