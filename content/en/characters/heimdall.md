Heimdall is the sentry, and the character the Thor films use whenever they need
somebody to be right early. He sees across all Nine Realms, hears everything in
them, and is therefore the first to know when something is wrong and the last
to be listened to about it.

Idris Elba plays him in six films across seven years, mostly in short scenes.
The best of them is in *Ragnarok*, where the guardian of the bridge is a
fugitive leading refugees through tunnels, which is the same job with the
grandeur taken off.
