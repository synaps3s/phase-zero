Melina Vostokoff is the mother in the fake family that Natasha Romanoff and
Yelena Belova grew up in, and a mother to them in more than name. Rachel Weisz
plays her. She is a seasoned spy who trained in
the Red Room as a Black Widow herself, and a scientist whose research into
methods of mind control is done for General Dreykov.

*Black Widow* is about what the Red Room did to the girls it took, and Melina
is on both sides of that: she helped build the machine, and she is a mother to
two of the people it was used on. When the family comes back together she helps
Natasha rescue the Widows and destroy the Red Room, which is the choice the
film has been waiting for her to make.

:::detail{title="The other Melina"}
The animated *What If...?* has a version of her who took over the Red Room
after Natasha killed its previous leader, and who runs a brainwashed Steve
Rogers as her weapon against Natasha and Captain Carter. Weisz voices her in
the second season and Kari Wahlgren in the third; Wahlgren also voices a
version in *Marvel Zombies*. This catalogue does not count animated variants
for live-action characters, so those are not in her list.
:::
