Chase Stein is a high school lacrosse player who is often dismissed as a dumb
jock and who shows real brilliance in engineering. Gregg Sulkin plays him.

The most uncomfortable thread in *Runaways* is his. His father is violent, his
father is also the person whose work he most wants to be worthy of, and the
series refuses to make that simple in either direction.
