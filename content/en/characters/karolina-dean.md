Karolina Dean is a human and alien hybrid, burdened by a religious upbringing
and wanting to follow her own desires instead. Her mother Leslie is one of the
leaders of the cult-like Church of Gibborim and a member of Pride; her father
Frank is a former teen star whose career has stalled.

Her story is the one *Runaways* does best. She is not fighting a monster, she
is working out how much of what she was raised on was true, and the answer is
complicated rather than clean.
