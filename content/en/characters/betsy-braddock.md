Betsy Braddock appears in *X-Men: Apocalypse* as Caliban's bodyguard, with
psionic powers she projects as a sword. Apocalypse recruits her, gives her more
power, and makes her Pestilence, one of the four Horsemen. When he is defeated
she leaves, which is the whole of what the film gives her.

She is the best illustration of a problem with *Apocalypse*: it is full of
people who exist to stand behind the villain, and it is not curious about any
of them.

:::detail{title="Psylocke is three different people here"}
The films use the name for three separate characters. A character credited as
Psylocke appears in *The Last Stand*, played by Meiling Melançon, is never
called Psylocke on screen, teleports through shadows, belongs to the Omegas and
is killed by the Phoenix. Betsy Braddock, played by Olivia Munn, is the one in
*Apocalypse*. A third, played by Ayesha Hussain, appears in the Void in
*Deadpool & Wolverine* as one of Cassandra Nova's gang, wields a psionic whip
and is killed by X-23.

This entry is the second of the three. The other two are not the same person
and are not recorded as her appearances.
:::
