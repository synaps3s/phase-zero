James Proudstar is a mutant with enhanced strength, speed and senses, and one
of the surviving mutants in the future. Booboo Stewart plays him.

Nobody in the future sequences is given a backstory, which is deliberate: they
are the last ones, they have been doing this for years, and the film treats
their competence as the only introduction they need.
