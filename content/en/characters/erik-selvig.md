Erik Selvig is an astrophysicist, born in Stockholm in 1951 according to
Wikipedia's article on him, who grew up hearing the legends of Asgard and
became a professor at Culver University, where he met Bruce Banner and took
Jane Foster, the daughter of a colleague, under his wing. Stellan Skarsgård
plays him in five films. The reader who sent this list called him the man
who became an expert in Asgardian mythos, which is where he started.

In *Thor* he is in New Mexico with Jane and Darcy Lewis when a powerless
Thor appears out of a storm, and Nick Fury recruits him afterwards to study
the Tesseract, unaware that he is already under Loki's influence. In *The
Avengers* Loki takes him fully, and Selvig builds the device that opens the
portal over Stark Tower; knocked free of the control, he tells Natasha
Romanoff that Loki's sceptre can shut it. A year later the aftermath has
given him a mental breakdown, which is the state Thor finds him in for *The
Dark World*, where his teleportation devices are what send Malekith and his
army back. By *Age of Ultron* he has recovered, takes Thor to the Water of
Sights, and joins the Avengers compound afterwards.

:::detail{title="Mentioned, and not counted"}
The article on him uses *Endgame* and *Far From Home* for what happened to
him offscreen: he is among those Thanos dusts and is brought back in 2023,
and he later hosts a television special on Einstein-Rosen bridges. Its lead
lists only five films for Skarsgård, and those five are his list here. In
*Love and Thunder* Jane consults him about her cancer treatment and he tells
her it is not working. An alternate version appeared in the animated
*Marvel Zombies*, which this catalogue does not count for live-action
people.
:::
