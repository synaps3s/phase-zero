Alice Wu-Gulliver is an ex-police officer and security guard, and Ali Ahn
plays her in *Agatha All Along*, with black hair and red highlights. Her
mother, Lorna Wu, played by Elizabeth Anweis, was a famous singer and witch
before dying in a hotel fire on tour; the reader who sent this list
remembered her as the blood witch whose mother sang a cover of "The
Witches' Road". Alice joins Agatha Harkness's coven to walk the Road
itself, in the hope of learning what happened to her mother.

:::spoiler{title="The third trial"}
In the third trial Agatha is possessed by the ghost of her mother, Evanora,
and Alice intervenes to exorcise her. In the process a possessed Agatha
absorbs Alice's magic, and Alice dies. Her soul is claimed by Rio Vidal,
who is Death, and who tells her she died trying to save Harkness.
:::
