Ben Grimm is the cost of the accident. The other three can pass in the
street; he cannot, and the film puts him in a neighbourhood that knows him
rather than in isolation, which is a kinder choice than the character usually
gets.

Ebon Moss-Bachrach plays him warm and unbothered on the surface, which makes the
moments where it slips the most affecting thing in the film.

:::detail{title="A separate Earth"}
These four live on Earth-828, not on the Earth the rest of the Marvel Studios
run takes place on. That was decided before the film was written, so that a team
this capable would not need an excuse for having been absent from everything
else.
:::
