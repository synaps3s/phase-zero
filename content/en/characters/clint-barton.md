Clint Barton has no powers, no armour and no serum, and the films are largely
uninterested in pretending that is remarkable. What they are interested in is
that he keeps going home. The Avengers find out about the farm in the middle of
the worst crisis they have had, and the film stops for twenty minutes so
everyone can sit in his kitchen, which is the most unusual thing
*Avengers: Age of Ultron* does.

His introduction is the best joke in his character. He is on a crane in New
Mexico with a bow, watching a man in a crater, asking his handler for permission
to shoot, and he does not appear again for a year.

What the story eventually does to him is take the farm away. Everything that
follows, including a costume and a name he never explains, is a man behaving
exactly as badly as somebody with nothing left would.
