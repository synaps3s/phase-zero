Okoye is the character who makes Wakanda feel like a country rather than a set.
She is the general of the Dora Milaje, the royal guard, and her loyalty is
constitutional: she serves the throne, and the film puts her in the one
situation where that is unbearable.

When Erik Killmonger wins the challenge and becomes king, he becomes king
legitimately. Okoye's oath does not have an exception for this. What she does
next is the most interesting decision anyone makes in *Black Panther*, and the
film makes her say it out loud rather than letting her off with a look.

She is also, straightforwardly, the best fighter in these films who has no
enhancements at all.
