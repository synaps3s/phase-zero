Kang is a plural character, and that is the whole idea. Jonathan Majors plays a
being who exists in variants across the multiverse, and the version stranded in
the Quantum Realm was put there by the others because even they found him
excessive.

The film that introduces him properly is a small one, and the choice is
deliberate: he explains himself to Janet van Dyne over a kettle, and what he
explains is that he has burned timelines the way other people cancel plans. His
last scene is a warning about how many more of him there are.

:::detail{title="Where the variants appear"}
The character is counted here in a single project, *Ant-Man and the Wasp:
Quantumania*. Variants of him appear in the series *Loki*, where He Who Remains
is one, and in that same film. The catalogue keeps variants separate from the
character, which is exactly the distinction the story is built on.
:::
