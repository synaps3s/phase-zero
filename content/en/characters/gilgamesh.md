Gilgamesh is the strongest Eternal, able to project an exoskeleton of cosmic
energy around himself. He has a deep connection with Thena, and after the
Eternals split up in 1521 he stays as her guardian across the centuries.

Don Lee plays him warm and unbothered, and the film gives him the domestic
scenes rather than the fights, which is why the one thing that happens to him
lands.

:::spoiler{title="At Druig's"}
He is killed at Druig's settlement by Kro, a Deviant that has been evolving,
who absorbs his powers and his memories with him.
:::
