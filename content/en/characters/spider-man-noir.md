A Spider-Man from a monochromatic universe set in the 1930s, voiced by Nicolas
Cage, who based the performance on the hard-boiled films of the period. He
speaks entirely in the register of a pulp narrator and never breaks it.

He is a one-joke character and the joke is very good: he is rendered in
greyscale on a screen full of colour, which means the animation itself is doing
the work rather than the dialogue.
