Ikaris can fly and project cosmic energy beams from his eyes, and has
superhuman strength and durability. He and Sersi were together for thousands of
years before he left her.

Richard Madden plays him as the reliable one, which the film needs, because
Ikaris is the character the story turns on and it cannot afford to signal it.

:::spoiler{title="What he does"}
When he discovers that Ajak means to stop the Emergence, he feeds her to the
Deviants and then rejoins the others on Earth as though nothing has happened.
He has known what the Eternals are for the whole time, and he did what he was
made to do.
:::
