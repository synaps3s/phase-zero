Everett K. Ross is a CIA operative, and Martin Freeman plays him in four
projects. In 2016 he is tasked with regulating members of the Avengers, and
later with tracking down Ulysses Klaue, which is how he ends up in Busan
taking a bullet for Nakia. He is taken to Wakanda for treatment, and after
healing he helps prevent vibranium weapons from leaving the country as Erik
Killmonger had planned. The reader who sent this list called him the
secretary who has helped the Wakandans, which is the role he settles into.

In 2025 he secretly collaborates with Shuri and Okoye despite Wakanda's
political conflict with the United States, and is arrested for it by his
ex-wife and now boss, Valentina Allegra de Fontaine. Okoye frees him.

:::spoiler{title="Secret Invasion"}
In 2026 he is captured by Skrulls and held in Russia, and freed by G'iah.
:::
