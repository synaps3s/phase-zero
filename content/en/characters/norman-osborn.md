Willem Dafoe's Green Goblin is the first great villain of the modern superhero
film and, twenty years later, still the most frightening. The construction is
simple: Norman Osborn takes his own unfinished serum because the board is about
to remove him, and it gives him a second personality that is better at his job
than he is.

*No Way Home* brings him back and does the one thing that could justify it. He
is the only one of the returning villains who is not looking for a cure, and the
film lets him take everything from a teenager who was trying to help him.
