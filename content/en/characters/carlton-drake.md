Carlton Drake is a genius inventor and the head of the Life Foundation, running
experiments on the symbiotes. Riz Ahmed plays him as a man trying to save the
future of humanity, which is how the film wants him read: he believes Earth is
finished and that merging with the symbiotes is the way off it.

Riot is the symbiote that takes him, and it is the one with a plan, which makes
Drake less a villain than a vehicle. That is the film's structural problem with
him and the reason he is the least remembered part of it.

:::detail{title="One appearance"}
Drake appears only in *Venom*. This catalogue files the Sony Venom films in
their own universe, separate from the connected Marvel Cinematic Universe.
:::
