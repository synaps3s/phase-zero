Groot has three words and the films get an extraordinary amount out of them. The
joke is that only Rocket understands him. The better joke is that the audience
starts to as well, somewhere in the first film, without anything having been
explained.

He is also the run's clearest piece of structural design. Each film gives him a
different age, so the same character is a bodyguard, then a toddler, then a
teenager, then an adult, across nine years of releases. Nobody else in this
catalogue is allowed to grow up.

:::detail{title="Whether it is the same Groot"}
The original Groot dies at the end of the first film. What is left is a
sapling, and the reference article is direct about what that means: James Gunn
has described the new Groot as the original's son, and Rocket's own entry lists
Groot as his adopted son.

So by the article's own framing, films two through six are a different
individual from film one. Its summary nonetheless counts six films for one
character, and this catalogue follows the summary. It is exactly the same
question the entry for Gamora has to answer, and it is answered here the same
way, which is: record what the source counts and say plainly where the source
is in two minds.
:::
