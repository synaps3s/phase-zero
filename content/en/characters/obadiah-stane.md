Obadiah Stane is the mentor who was already the enemy. He took over Stark
Industries after Howard died, kept it running through Tony's absence, and hired
the Ten Rings to kill him when Tony finally started paying attention to what the
company was selling.

Jeff Bridges plays him warm, which is the whole trick. He is the only person in
the film who is kind to Tony without visibly wanting anything, right up to the
moment he takes the arc reactor out of his chest and explains why.
