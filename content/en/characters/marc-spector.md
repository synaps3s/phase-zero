Moon Knight is the only entry in this catalogue that begins from inside the
condition rather than outside it. Marc Spector is an American mercenary with
dissociative identity disorder, developed from childhood abuse after his younger
brother drowned and his mother blamed him. Steven Grant, who works in a London
gift shop and thinks he simply sleeps badly, is one of his alters. Jake Lockley
is another, and the series takes its time telling anyone.

Oscar Isaac plays all of them, and the series is at its best when it stays
inside the confusion instead of explaining it. Khonshu, the god Marc serves, is
a demanding and unreliable employer, which is a good description of the deal the
character has made.
