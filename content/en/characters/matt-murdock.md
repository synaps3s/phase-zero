Matt Murdock is the most Catholic character in this catalogue and the one whose
guilt is most load-bearing. He is a lawyer who believes in the law and a man
who goes out every night because he has decided the law is not enough, and the
series he comes from never lets him settle that.

The performance is the thing that carried the character across a change of
studio. He is the only person here who was introduced by one company, sat in
limbo for years while nobody would say whether he counted, and came back for
another one with the same actor and the same continuity intact.
