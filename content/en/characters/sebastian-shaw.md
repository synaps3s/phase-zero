Sebastian Shaw absorbs and redirects energy and stays young while doing it. He
is first shown as Klaus Schmidt, a Nazi scientist interested in what young Erik
Lehnsherr can do with metal, and he shoots Erik's mother in front of him to
force the power out. Everything Magneto becomes starts in that room.

Later he runs the Hellfire Club and works to trigger a nuclear war, on the
theory that mutants will inherit whatever is left. He manipulates military
officials on both sides into placing warheads, and pushes the Cuban Missile
Crisis towards the ending he wants.

:::spoiler{title="The coin"}
Magneto lifts Shaw's submarine out of the sea, takes off the helmet that was
keeping Xavier out of his head, and holds him still while Xavier freezes him.
Then he pushes a Nazi coin slowly through his skull, and takes over the Hellfire
Club himself. It is the moment the film stops pretending Erik is going to end up
on the other side.
:::
