Drax is the one running joke in these films that is also a character. He takes
everything literally, which starts as a gag about metaphor and turns into
something else once you notice he is the only member of the group who says what
he means at all times.

His family were killed by Ronan on Thanos's orders. He introduces himself with
this, in full, to strangers. Dave Bautista plays him with a stillness that makes
the loud parts land, and the friendship with Mantis in the second film is the
best thing either character gets.
