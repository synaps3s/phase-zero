The two Amazing Spider-Man films give Gwen Stacy a great deal more to do than
this material usually gives the person the hero is in love with. She is
scientifically literate, she is ahead of Peter Parker for most of both films,
and her decisions are the ones the plots turn on.

That is also what makes what happens to her land the way it does. She is not in
the room because somebody took her there.
