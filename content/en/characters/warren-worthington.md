Warren Worthington III is a wealthy mutant with large feathered wings, and
the Fox films tell his story twice, with different actors, in what turn out to
be different timelines. Ben Foster plays him in *The Last Stand*, with Cayden
Boyd as the child, and Ben Hardy in *Apocalypse*.

*The Last Stand* opens with a boy shaving the feathers off his growing wings
before his father walks in. As an adult he is strapped to a table to be given
the mutant cure, breaks free, and throws himself from a window to fly to the
Xavier Institute. In the battle at Alcatraz he saves his father from being
thrown from a building.

In *Apocalypse* he is a young cage fighter whose wings are badly damaged in a
bout with Nightcrawler, which sends him into substance abuse. Apocalypse finds
him there, gives him metal wings and the name Archangel, and makes him the
Horseman of Death. He fights Nightcrawler again in Egypt and is beaten when he
crashes inside the X-Men's jet.

:::detail{title="Cut, and cut again"}
Wikipedia records that versions of Angel were written for the first two X-Men
films and cut both times. It also records that promotional material said he
would be killed by Sentinels in the future, a death no film shows.
:::
