Lucien arrives at a hospital in Greece as a ten-year-old with the same rare
blood disease as Michael Morbius, who renames him Milo, the name he gives
every boy who shares that bed. They grow up together, Milo wealthy and
Michael brilliant, and Matt Smith plays the adult Milo in *Morbius*, with
Joseph Esson as the boy. Smith was originally announced as a different comic
character, Loxias Crown, before the part was rewritten into a mirror of
Michael himself.

It is Milo's money that puts Michael's cure on a mercenary ship in
international waters. When the cure works, and turns Michael into something
that kills for blood, Michael refuses to give it to him.

:::spoiler{title="What he does with it"}
He takes it anyway, kills a nurse with it and lets Michael take the blame,
and when Michael understands, urges him to embrace what they are. He kills
Emil Nicholas, the man who raised them both, and mortally wounds Martine.
Michael restrains him with a swarm of bats and injects an antibody he had
built to stop him, and Milo dies.
:::
