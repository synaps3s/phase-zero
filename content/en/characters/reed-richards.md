Reed Richards is written here as a man whose first instinct is always to
calculate, including about people, which is the trait the film keeps testing.
His inventions have pushed his Earth's technology decades past ours: flying
cars, a space programme, a robot in the house.

Pedro Pascal plays him tired rather than arrogant. The film's best decision is
to make him a father before it makes him a hero, so that the offer Galactus
brings lands on a specific person rather than on a principle.

:::detail{title="A separate Earth"}
These four live on Earth-828, not on the Earth the rest of the Marvel Studios
run takes place on. That was decided before the film was written, so that a team
this capable would not need an excuse for having been absent from everything
else.
:::
