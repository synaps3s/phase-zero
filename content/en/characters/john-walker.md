John Walker is the series arguing with itself. On paper he is the correct
choice: three Medals of Honor, a decorated captain in the 75th Ranger Regiment,
tested at MIT and above average in everything. What he does not have is the
thing Steve Rogers had, and the show is honest enough to admit that nobody can
say precisely what that was.

Wyatt Russell plays him as a man who can feel the gap and cannot name it. Then
his partner Lemar Hoskins is killed in front of him, and he kills a man with the
shield in a public square in daylight, and the series has its image.
