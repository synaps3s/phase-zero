Peggy Carter is the spine of the pre-Avengers half of this story. She is a
British officer attached to the Strategic Scientific Reserve, she selects Steve
Rogers for the serum programme, and after the war she does the part nobody made
a film about for seventy years: she builds S.H.I.E.L.D. and runs it.

Hayley Atwell got a one-shot, then a series, then a place in almost every
flashback the franchise needed, which is unusual for a character introduced as a
love interest. *Agent Carter* is worth the detour: it is a 1946 spy show about a
woman whose colleagues answer her phone for her.
