Peter Maximoff is the answer to a question the films had not asked: what does
speed actually look like. The Pentagon kitchen sequence in *Days of Future Past*
stops the world, puts a song on, and lets him take his time, and it is the most
copied thing in the series.

*Apocalypse* runs the same idea across an exploding mansion and adds the reason
he is there: Magneto is his father, and he has not worked out how to say it.
