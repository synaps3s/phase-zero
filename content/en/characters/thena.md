Thena is a fierce warrior who can form any weapon out of cosmic energy. She
suffers from Mahd Wy'ry, which the film treats as the accumulated weight of too
many lifetimes of memory, and Gilgamesh becomes her guardian over the centuries
because of it.

Angelina Jolie plays the fighting as beautiful and the illness as frightening,
and the film is unusual in this catalogue for letting a character be genuinely
unwell without curing her by the end.

:::detail{title="What Mahd Wy'ry is for"}
The Eternals do not age and their memories do not stop. The condition is what
the film uses to ask whether living forever is a gift, and it is the one
question *Eternals* answers clearly.
:::
