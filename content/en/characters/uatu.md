Every episode of *What If...?* opens with the same voice explaining that in
this universe, one thing happened differently. The voice belongs to the
Watcher, played by Jeffrey Wright, a member of a race whose entire purpose is
to observe the multiverse and never step into it. He is the frame the series
is built in, and for a while he is only that.

The interesting part is that the frame does not hold. A Doctor Strange who has
lost everything hears him and begs for help, and the Watcher refuses. Then an
Ultron who has finished with his own universe hears him too, and wants the rest.
The rule that was the whole of his character turns out to be the thing he has to
give up, and the rest of the series is about what a being like that does once
he has done it.

Wright plays him warm rather than grand. The writers compared him to somebody
watching a video of a rat carrying a slice of pizza: interested, moved even,
and with no intention of joining in. That distance is what makes the moments
he crosses it land.

:::spoiler{title="The trial"}
By the third season his interference has a record: information handed to
Strange Supreme so he could rebuild his universe, people pulled out of their
own stories before they died, a Captain Carter drawn into his affairs. The
other Watchers, the Eminence, the Incarnate and the Executioner, put him on
trial for it, and the season is the trial and the escape. His name, Uatu, is
finally used here, in a flashback to when he was recruited.
:::

:::detail{title="Where else he turns up, and why some of it is not counted"}
The Watchers as a race appear first in *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2*, in the
Stan Lee cameo, where Lee is their informant; this Watcher is not one of them
on screen, so that film is not in his list. He has silent cameos in
*X-Men '97*, *Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man* and, in the reading
Marvel's animation head has acknowledged, a pattern of broken glass in
*Daredevil: Born Again*. The first is not counted because the series' own
producers said it is not necessarily the same version of him; the other two
because the sources name the series and not the season, and this catalogue
is filed by season. The two animated series where he is named as Uatu, *Eyes
of Wakanda* and *Marvel Zombies*, are counted.
:::
