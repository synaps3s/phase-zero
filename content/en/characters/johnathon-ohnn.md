Johnathon Ohnn is a former Alchemax scientist turned supervillain after an
accident, whose body is now covered in interdimensional portals that let him
travel through space and between universes. Jason Schwartzman voices him.

He was not originally meant to be the main antagonist. Phil Lord and
Christopher Miller dismissed the idea when the producer Avi Arad suggested him,
and changed their minds once they saw what the power could do on screen.

:::spoiler{title="Where he comes from"}
The accident is the collider at the end of the first film, which means Miles
made him. The film's whole thesis is about consequences arriving late, and the
villain is one of them.
:::
