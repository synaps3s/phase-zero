Hunter B-15 is an agent of the Time Variance Authority and later its new
leader, a brainwashed variant who remembers her family and befriends Loki.
Wunmi Mosaku plays her. The second-season episode "Science/Fiction" reveals
that she is a variant of Verity Willis, a paediatrician in New York in 2012.
The reader who sent this list described her as a hunter who later became
the head of the TVA, which is the arc in one line.

:::detail{title="Three, one of them a comic"}
Wikipedia counts her in three projects: *Loki*, *Deadpool & Wolverine*, and
the comic book series *TVA*, which its editors note is written by a
second-season *Loki* writer and set after *Deadpool & Wolverine* with the
same characters. This catalogue lists films and series, so the comic is not
in her list.
:::
