Angel Salvadore has insect-like wings and can spit acid, and she defects from
the X-Men to the Hellfire Club. Zoe Kravitz plays her.

The defection is the moment *First Class* is making its actual argument. Shaw
offers her something the CIA never does: he tells her she does not have to
hide. The film knows he is a monster and knows the offer is real, and it does
not resolve that.
