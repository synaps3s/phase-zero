Andrew Garfield's Peter is the funniest of the three on screen and the one whose
films around him work least well. The first is a solid origin with a much
better performance in it than it needs; the second is overloaded with setup for
films that never happened.

What redeems the run entirely is *No Way Home*, which finds the exact thing this
version is for. He has spent a decade as the Spider-Man who was not there in
time, and the film gives him the catch he missed, and Garfield plays it as the
only thing that has ever mattered to him.
