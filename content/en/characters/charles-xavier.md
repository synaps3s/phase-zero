Xavier's position is that mutants and humans can share a world, and every film
in the series tests it. He is not written as naive: he knows exactly what the
other side of the argument sounds like, because he is best friends with it.

The most interesting thing the films do with him is refuse to make him good.
He edits memories, he keeps secrets from people who had a right to them, and in
his last film the consequences of that are not abstract.

:::detail{title="The count in the source is wrong"}
The reference article says he appears in eleven live-action films, and then
names thirteen in the same paragraph. The eleven is exactly the Fox run; the
two Marvel Studios appearances are not counted.

This catalogue follows the named list rather than the number.
:::
