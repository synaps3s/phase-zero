Hogun, called Hogun the Grim, is a member of the Warriors Three and a grim
Vanir warrior from Vanaheim. Tadanobu Asano plays him across all three *Thor*
films.

He is the one detail that suggests Asgard is an empire rather than a country:
the Warriors Three include a man from a realm Asgard conquered, and nobody in
the films thinks that is worth a line of dialogue.
