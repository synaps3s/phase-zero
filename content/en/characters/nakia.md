Nakia is the argument the first film is having. She is a War Dog, Wakanda's
intelligence service, and years of watching what happens outside the border have
convinced her that a country this rich hiding this hard is a moral failure.

The film gives her position to two people: her, reasonably, and N'Jadaka,
violently. What T'Challa eventually does at the United Nations is her plan. She
just did not get to be the one who broke something first.
