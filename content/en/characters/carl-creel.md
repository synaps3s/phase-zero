Carl Creel can take on the properties of whatever he touches: steel, stone,
whatever is nearest. Brian Patrick Wade plays him, cast in August 2014. He
arrives in *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* as a Hydra operative, recruited by John
Garrett and brainwashed by Whitehall, and he is the series' first proper
demonstration of what a comics villain looks like on a television budget.

He does not stay a villain. Glenn Talbot hires him as a bodyguard, and he
quits when Talbot is injured and hospitalised. Later General Hale hires him to
hunt S.H.I.E.L.D., and he turns on her when he finds out she is holding Talbot
prisoner, and helps Talbot and Coulson escape. Along the way he forms a mental
link with gravitonium and discovers that the minds of Franklin Hall and Ian
Quinn, two people it absorbed, are still inside it.

:::spoiler{title="How he ends"}
While he is recovering in hospital, Talbot, by then carrying gravitonium of his
own, visits him and absorbs him into himself.
:::
