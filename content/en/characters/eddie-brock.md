Eddie Brock is a bad journalist and a worse boyfriend, and the films are
cheerfully clear about both. What makes him work is that he is not corrupted by
what happens to him. He was already like this. The symbiote just gives him
somebody to argue with out loud.

The trilogy is a relationship story with a body-horror premise, and the second
film is the one that admits it. Everything good about the run comes from the
fact that two performances are coming out of one actor.
