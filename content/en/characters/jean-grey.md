Jean Grey is played by two actors across two runs of films, and both times the
story is the same: her power is greater than the team's, Charles Xavier decided
when she was a child to wall part of it off, and he did not tell her. The Fox
films tried this twice, in *The Last Stand* and again in *Dark Phoenix*, and
neither attempt is loved.

What survives both attempts is the relationship with Logan, which the films
handle better than the cosmic material. Famke Janssen and Hugh Jackman are the
reason *The Wolverine* has anything to grieve about.
