Kamala Khan is the only person in this catalogue who was a fan of the others
first. She has posters, she has opinions, she has written the fiction, and when
she meets Carol Danvers the scene plays the way it would actually play, which
is badly and at speed.

The series she comes from is the warmest thing here and its real subject is
inheritance: the bangle leads backwards through her family to the Partition of
India, and the story treats that as the thing worth understanding rather than
the powers.

:::detail{title="The word the films avoid"}
The reference article describes the version of Kamala in these films as a
Pakistani-American mutant. That word carried a contractual history in this
material for two decades, and its use here is the point at which the two halves
of the catalogue formally stopped being separate.
:::
