Willis Stryker is a powerful arms dealer, Luke Cage's half-brother, and the
man who framed him for the crime that sent him to Seagate Prison. Erik LaRay
Harvey plays him in the first season of *Luke Cage*, with Jared Kemp as the
teenage Willis. The casting was never officially announced before the series
came out, and Harvey agreed to do no publicity so as not to ruin the twist
of Stryker being the season's main villain.

Harvey chose not to read the comics, so as not to cloud his judgement, and
relied on the showrunner Cheo Hodari Coker for the 2016 version of the man,
including the half-brother backstory. Coker still brought as much of the
comics' Diamondback as he could, adapting the costume into armour that lets
Stryker match Luke's strength. The reader who sent this list said he was the
first to truly get through Luke's skin, which is what the armour is for.

:::spoiler{title="Pop's"}
The showdown is outside Pop's barbershop, with Stryker in the exo-suit.
Luke wins, Stryker is arrested, and Luke later tells Jessica Jones he had
him sent to the Raft.
:::
