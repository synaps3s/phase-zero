Valentina is a structural character: she exists to connect films that were made
years apart. Julia Louis-Dreyfus plays her as someone who is enjoying herself
enormously and telling the truth about roughly half of what she says, which
makes her hard to place and easy to watch.

By *Thunderbolts* the shape is clear. She has been recruiting damaged people
individually, off the books, for years, and the film is what happens when they
all meet each other and compare notes.
