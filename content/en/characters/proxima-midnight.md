Proxima Midnight is Thanos's adopted daughter, voiced and faced by Carrie Coon
and motion captured by Monique Ganderton. She joins his search for the Infinity
Stones and takes part in the attack on the Statesman, helping kill the
Asgardians aboard to get the Space Stone. With Corvus Glaive she attacks Wanda
Maximoff and Vision for the Mind Stone, and Steve Rogers, Natasha Romanoff and
Sam Wilson drive them off.

She is a useful reminder that Gamora and Nebula are not the only adopted
children in that family, and that the others made a different choice about it.
