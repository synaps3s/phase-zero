Danny Ketch is a young child caught up in a monstrous conspiracy who ends up
in the care of Johnny Blaze, and Fergus Riordan plays him in *Ghost Rider:
Spirit of Vengeance*. The plot is a chase: Roarke's forces want him, a monk
named Moreau sends the Ghost Rider after him, and his mother Nadya runs with
him until her former boyfriend Ray Carrigan catches them. The reader who
sent this list suspected he might have demonic powers of his own, and the
film agrees.

:::spoiler{title="Whose son he is"}
Nadya reveals that Danny is Roarke's son, and Roarke is Mephistopheles in
disguise. The ritual at the end is meant to transfer the aging Devil's mind
into the boy's body. Before that, Danny is the one who stops the Rider from
turning the Penance Stare on his mother, and after Johnny has been
exorcised, Danny returns the Ghost Rider to him and grants it the ability to
stay in Rider form in daylight. When it is over Johnny revives him with the
blue flame of Zarathos.
:::

:::detail{title="The name on the database"}
The Marvel Database files him as Daniel "Danny" Ketch of Earth-121347, with
"Anti-Christ" as an alias, and describes him as a human and demon hybrid,
which is the film's plot in two words.
:::
