Titania is a social media influencer with incredible strength and an
obsession with She-Hulk, whom she decides is her rival. Jameela Jamil plays
her, and her description of the character is better than any summary: annoying
and weird, "all narcissism and ego", somebody who "almost doesn't need to use
her strength; she could just annoy you to death". She believes she is the
strongest woman in the world until She-Hulk humiliates her in public, and the
grudge does the rest.

The head writer, Jessica Gao, wanted to modernise the comics character and
give her "a little more complexity", which turned out to mean social media and
brand maintenance. Jamil gave her what she called a very Los Angeles influencer
accent, trained in jiu-jitsu, kickboxing and kung fu for the part, and drew on
the comics backstory, which the series never shows, for the insecurity
underneath.

:::detail{title="Why she is barely in the early episodes"}
The writers kept her out of the way at first on purpose, to preserve from the
comics the sense that she is a gnat forever buzzing around She-Hulk rather than
somebody trying to destroy her, and to let the audience get to know everyone
else before she came back.
:::
