Phillip J. Coulson is a high ranking S.H.I.E.L.D. agent and Nick Fury's longest
serving partner, and for the first four years of these films he is the
connective tissue: the man who arrives after the thing has happened and asks
for a statement. He grew up idolising Captain America and Peggy Carter, and
joined the agency Carter founded.

Clark Gregg plays him as unbothered, which is why the audience became attached
to him without anyone planning it. He is the first character in this catalogue
who was invented for the films rather than adapted from the page, and he has
since been written into the comics because of it.

:::spoiler{title="The helicarrier, and everything after"}
Loki stabs him through the heart during the attack on the helicarrier, and Fury
uses his death, and a set of bloodied trading cards, to make the Avengers into
a team. Fury then has him resurrected with the blood of a dead Kree, which is
where *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* begins: seven seasons of a man working out what
was done to him, running the agency, losing a hand, making a deal with a demon
to briefly become the Ghost Rider, dying again in Tahiti, and coming back one
more time as a Life Model Decoy.
:::

:::detail{title="How much of this the films acknowledge"}
Very little. *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* is built on the films and the films never
refer back to it, which is the central problem of the Marvel Television era and
the reason this catalogue marks that tier separately.
:::
