Pavitr Prabhakar is the Spider-Man of Earth-50101, voiced by Karan Soni, living
in a city the film calls Mumbattan.

He is in the film to make a structural point. He is six months into the job,
adored, dating, and untouched by any of it, which is exactly what the Spider
Society says is not allowed to last.

:::spoiler{title="The canon event"}
Miles saves Pavitr's police captain, who was supposed to die. That is the moment
the film stops being an adventure: the argument is no longer theoretical,
because Miles has already broken the rule once and nothing bad has happened yet.
:::
