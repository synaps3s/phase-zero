Grant Douglas Ward is the son of politicians, abused as a child by his parents
and his older brother Christian. After trying to kill Christian by burning
their house down, he meets John Garrett, a Hydra double agent inside
S.H.I.E.L.D., who trains him into a skilled agent. He is assigned to Coulson's
team as its muscle and wetwork specialist. Brett Dalton plays him.

*Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* spends most of a season being a competent, unexciting
procedural, and then *Captain America: The Winter Soldier* reveals Hydra inside
S.H.I.E.L.D. and the series has to answer it. Ward is the answer, and it is the
only time the television tier and the films ever moved together properly.
