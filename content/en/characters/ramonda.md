Ramonda is the character *Wakanda Forever* is actually about. She has lost
T'Chaka and then T'Challa, she is running a country that everybody now knows is
sitting on vibranium, and the film gives her the two scenes that carry it: the
address to the United Nations, and the one where she tells Shuri that grief is
not a plan.

Angela Bassett won an Academy Award for supporting actress for it, the first
performance in these films to win one.
