Hugh Jackman played this character for twenty-four years, which is longer than
anyone has played anyone else in this catalogue, and the run is unusual in that
it improved. The early films use him as the audience's way in. The last one
uses him as an old man dying of the thing that kept him alive.

What holds it together is that he never wants to be there. Every film in the
series begins by finding him somewhere else, alone, and giving him a reason he
cannot refuse.

:::detail{title="Nine films, or ten"}
The reference articles disagree with themselves. One says he has appeared in
nine films, meaning the Fox run, and mentions *Deadpool & Wolverine*
separately. Another says ten and lists all ten.

This catalogue records ten, because that article enumerates them and the other
does not.
:::
