Deacon Frost is an upstart vampire with great ambitions and influence, the
primary enemy of the first *Blade* and a man who wants to conquer the human
race. Stephen Dorff plays him. The club Blade raids in the opening minutes is
his.

What sets him apart from the vampires around him is that he was made, not
born. The pure-blood elders consider Frost and his kind socially inferior,
and when he tries to push them into a war with humans, they rebuke him. His
answer is to stop asking. He has an elder stripped of his fangs and burned
by the sunrise, strips the rest of their authority, and imprisons them.

:::spoiler{title="La Magra, and Vanessa Brooks"}
Frost's plan is a ritual: twelve pure-blood vampires, and Blade's blood as the
key, to awaken the blood god La Magra. When Blade goes to rescue Karen from
Frost's penthouse he finds his own mother there, alive, and Frost reveals that
he is the vampire who bit her the night Blade was born. It is the film's
central twist, and the reason the reader who sent this list described him as
the man who killed and turned Blade's mother.

He completes the ritual and gets the powers he wanted. Blade cannot beat him
until he reasons that the body is still a body, and gives Frost an overdose of
the anticoagulant EDTA, which reacts with vampire blood. Frost swells into
something grotesque and explodes.
:::
