White Vision is what Wikipedia's article on Vision calls, for clarity, "The
Vision": the original body, in S.W.O.R.D.'s custody since Thanos pulled the
Mind Stone out of it, reassembled and reactivated in *WandaVision* by the
agency's director Tyler Hayward using the remnants of Wanda Maximoff's magic
on a drone. He comes back all white, with light blue eyes, and with his
memories erased, programmed to get rid of Maximoff. Paul Bettany plays him
as well as the other one, and said he tried to make this version familiar
and intimidating at the same time.

He is sent into the Hex. He approaches Wanda, who asks whether it is really
him, pretends to caress her and attacks. The Vision she made inside the Hex
arrives and fights him, and the two end up in the Westview library talking
through the Ship of Theseus, the old question of whether a thing with every
part replaced is still the thing. The reader who sent this list described
him as a rebuilt Vision who has the powers but not the memories, which is
exactly right up to the moment it stops being true.

:::spoiler{title="The library"}
He permits the other Vision to restore his memories, realises that he is an
Avenger, and flies away to process it. The series does not say where.
:::

:::detail{title="One entry or two"}
This catalogue keeps him separate from Vision because the reader who asked
for him thinks of him as a different person, and because *VisionQuest*,
announced for 2026 and not yet released, is built around this one. Wikipedia
disagrees: it keeps him inside Vision's own article, as Vision's body with
Vision's memories restored, and calls the one Wanda made "Hex Vision". Both
readings are recorded here, and the Vision entry carries *WandaVision* too.
:::
