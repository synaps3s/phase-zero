Francis Freeman is Ajax, a mutant immune to pain, and the man who made Wade
Wilson into Deadpool. Ed Skrein plays him in *Deadpool*. He runs the
Workshop, the place Wade goes to be cured of cancer and where instead he is
tortured until the mutation surfaces, and he is disfigured when Wade
escapes.

The reader who sent this list described him as the man who made Wade Wilson
into Deadpool, which is accurate, and which Wade spends the whole film
holding against him along with the name Francis.

:::spoiler{title="Vanessa"}
He kidnaps Vanessa to bring Wade to him, and Wade kills him at the end.
:::
