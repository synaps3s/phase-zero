Aaron Davis is Miles's uncle, who moonlights as a masked enforcer for Kingpin
and keeps it from his family. Mahershala Ali voices him in both films.

He is the emotional engine of the first film, and the trick is that neither half
of him is a lie. The uncle who takes Miles out at night and tells him to be
himself is the same man in the mask, and *Into the Spider-Verse* refuses to let
either version cancel the other.

:::spoiler{title="Earth-42"}
In *Across the Spider-Verse*, the universe Miles ends up in is the one where he
was never bitten, so nobody became Spider-Man, and Aaron never died. That is
the version of Aaron Ali voices in the second film, and it is the worst news
the film has.
:::

:::detail{title="And the other Aaron Davis"}
Donald Glover appears in live action in *Across the Spider-Verse* as an
alternate Aaron Davis, reprising the role he played in
*Spider-Man: Homecoming*. Wikipedia records it as a reprise rather than a
lookalike, which makes it one of the few times a performer has carried the same
character between two of the continuities in this catalogue.
:::
