Shang-Chi is the son of Ying Li, who guarded a mystical village called Ta Lo,
and of Wenwu, who founded the Ten Rings. He was trained from childhood as an
assassin by his father and has spent a decade in California pretending to be
nobody, which the film treats as a legitimate strategy rather than as cowardice.

His single film is the best-structured origin in the later catalogue, because
the antagonist is not a threat to the world for most of it. It is a grieving
man who wants his wife back and has convinced himself he can hear her.
