Surtur is a fire demon, the lord of Muspelheim, and the figure in the prophecy
of Ragnarok who is meant to bring Asgard down. He imprisons Thor in his lair,
tells him that Odin is not on Asgard, and explains that if his crown is united
with the Eternal Flame he will grow large enough to finish the realm. Thor beats
him and escapes with the crown, which is the mistake.

He is motion captured by Taika Waititi, who directed the film, and voiced by
Clancy Brown, and the opening scene is one long joke at the expense of villain
monologues that turns out to have been the plot summary.

:::spoiler{title="The ending"}
Thor works out that Asgard is a people and not a place, and sends Loki to put
the crown into the Eternal Flame on purpose. Surtur does exactly what he said
he would do, and the survivors watch from a ship.
:::
