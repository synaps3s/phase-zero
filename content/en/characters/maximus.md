Maximus is Black Bolt's brother, devoted to the people of Attilan and set on
being king himself. He lost the Inhuman gene going through Terrigenesis, which
is why the other Inhumans look down on him. Iwan Rheon described him as the
runt of the family, and said that without his brother he would be working down
in the mines: he only has what he has out of pity.

*Inhumans* is the least defended thing in this catalogue and Rheon is the part
people still argue for. The executive producer Scott Buck called this a
different, more complicated and more real version of the character than the one
in the comics, and that claim is at least arguable.

:::detail{title="The caste system nobody mentions"}
The premise of Attilan is that people who do not develop powers are sent to
work in the mines. The series raises this, gives it to the villain to say out
loud, and never answers it, which is most of why the show does not work.
:::
