Miguel O'Hara is a "ninja-vampire" Spider-Man from Earth-928, set in the year
2099, and the leader of the Spider-Society, a group of Spider-People from
alternate universes tasked with protecting the multiverse. Oscar Isaac voices
him.

He is the antagonist of the second film and he is not a villain, which is the
difficult thing the film is doing. He has evidence. He has lost something by
trying what Miles wants to try. The film does not tell you he is wrong; it tells
you Miles is going to find out.

:::detail{title="Earth-199999, from the other side"}
Miguel is also the character who complains about "Doctor Strange and the little
nerd back on Earth-199999", meaning the Marvel Cinematic Universe. It is the
clearest evidence that Sony still uses the designation the comics assigned to
the MCU, against the five occasions on which the MCU's own films call
themselves Earth-616. This catalogue records that disagreement on the page for
the main continuity rather than settling it.
:::
