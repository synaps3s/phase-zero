Jackson Brice is part of Adrian Toomes's criminal enterprise, using a modified
version of Brock Rumlow's vibro-blast gauntlet and calling himself the Shocker.
After a weapons deal with Aaron Davis draws Spider-Man's attention, Toomes
sacks him for recklessness. Brice threatens to expose the operation, and Toomes
fires one of Phineas Mason's weapons at him.

He exists to establish, in about ninety seconds, that Toomes is not a
supervillain with a plan but a man running a business with an employee problem.
It is the most efficient character introduction in the film and it is somebody
else's introduction.

:::detail{title="The name moves"}
Herman Schultz takes the gauntlet and the name immediately afterwards, which is
why this catalogue carries two Shockers.
:::
