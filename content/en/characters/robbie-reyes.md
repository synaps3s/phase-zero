Roberto Reyes is a mechanic who was attacked, along with his brother Gabe, by
gang members hired to kill their uncle Eli Morrow. Gabe was paralysed and
Robbie was killed, and his life was given back by another Ghost Rider on a
motorcycle who passed him the power. He lives to look after Gabe, and at night
he hunts the guilty.

His arrival is the moment *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* stops being a spy series and
admits that magic exists in it. Gabriel Luna plays him quietly, which is the
right call when the effect budget is doing the shouting.

:::detail{title="Two Riders"}
The Rider who saved him is Johnny Blaze, who appears in the same series, and
this catalogue carries him separately. Blaze has since been recast and
announced for films that have not been released.
:::
