Johnny is the team's youngest and its performer, and the film gives him the
one job nobody else wants: paying attention to the herald who arrives before
Galactus. He is written as smarter than his reputation, which is a small
correction to a character who is usually only the joke.

Joseph Quinn plays him as a person who has learned that being the loud one gets
him left alone, and who quietly stops doing it when it matters.

:::detail{title="A separate Earth"}
These four live on Earth-828, not on the Earth the rest of the Marvel Studios
run takes place on. That was decided before the film was written, so that a team
this capable would not need an excuse for having been absent from everything
else.
:::
