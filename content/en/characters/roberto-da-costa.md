Roberto da Costa is a Brazilian mutant who manipulates solar energy. Henry Zaga
plays him as the confident one, which is a performance the character is giving
as well.

He has the same secret as the others, and it involves somebody he loved, and
the film holds it back until it needs it.

:::detail{title="Two actors, ten years apart in the wrong direction"}
Adan Canto played Sunspot in *X-Men: Days of Future Past* in 2014, as one of
the surviving mutants in the ruined future, and Henry Zaga plays him in
*The New Mutants* as a teenager in the present. Wikipedia keeps them in one
entry, so this catalogue does too.

Sunspot is Afro-Brazilian in the comics, and Zaga's casting was criticised on
that basis when the film was announced.
:::
