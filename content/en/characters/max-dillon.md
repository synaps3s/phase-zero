Electro is a character about being unseen. Max Dillon designed a system that his
employer takes credit for, nobody remembers his birthday, and the one moment of
recognition he gets is thirty seconds of kindness from a masked stranger. The
film is far more interested in the accident than in that, which is its mistake.

*No Way Home* gives Jamie Foxx a rewrite and a much better film to be in, and the
best line the character gets in either one is his reaction to seeing Peter
Parker's face.
