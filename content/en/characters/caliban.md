Caliban is a mutant with chalk-white skin and the ability to locate other
mutants, and two films use him for opposite things. Tómas Lemarquis plays
him in *X-Men: Apocalypse*, set in 1983, as an underground broker who tracks
mutants for money. Psylocke is his bodyguard until Apocalypse recruits her.
The reader who sent this list wondered what he was doing in the eighties;
the answer is that this is his first appearance, and the second is nearly
fifty years later in the story.

In *Logan*, set in 2029, Stephen Merchant plays him as a friend of Logan and
Professor X, living with them in the future with a severe sensitivity to
sunlight. Logan drives a limousine; somebody has to stay home with the old
man, and that is him.

:::spoiler{title="The Reavers"}
He is abducted by the Reavers to track Wolverine for them, and he does it,
under duress, until he kills himself with grenades rather than go on.
:::
