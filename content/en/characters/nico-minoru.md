Nico Minoru is a Wiccan and a member of the Runaways who keeps everyone at a
distance behind a gothic appearance. Lyrica Okano plays her, and her mother
Tina is a member of Pride.

The rule attached to her power is the best invention in the series: the Staff
of One will do anything she asks, and it will never do the same thing twice, so
every spell she casts is a word she can never use again.

:::detail{title="The other Nico"}
A variant appears in the *Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man* comic series
and in the animated series of the same name, voiced by Grace Song. That is a
different universe and not this one.
:::
