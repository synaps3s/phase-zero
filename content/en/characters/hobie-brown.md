Hobie Brown is a punk rock version of Spider-Man from Earth-138 who uses his
guitar as his main weapon. Daniel Kaluuya voices him.

He is animated differently from everybody else on screen, on a lower frame rate,
in torn paper and screenprint, and the film uses that as characterisation rather
than decoration: he does not move at the same speed as the institution he is
standing inside.
