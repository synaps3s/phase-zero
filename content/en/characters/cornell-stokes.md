Cornell "Cottonmouth" Stokes owns the Harlem's Paradise nightclub and deals
in illegal operations behind it. He is the cousin of Mariah Dillard, the
councilwoman, and Mahershala Ali plays him in the first season of *Luke
Cage*, with Elijah Boothe as the young Cornell. Ali described him as a
Godfather-type villain; Jeph Loeb, then the head of Marvel Television,
called him the other hero of the story.

Ali took the part knowing that the character would die early. Wikipedia
quotes him comparing it to shooting a film rather than entering another
marriage of unknown length, and saying it was the first time he had found
himself excited by a character's departure.

:::spoiler{title="Who kills him"}
Mariah does, in a rage. She first frames Luke Cage for it, and later shifts
the blame to Diamondback. The cast table on Wikipedia's list has him in the
second season only in archive footage, so that season is not in his list.
:::
