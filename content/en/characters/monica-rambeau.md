Monica Rambeau is the connective character of this part of the catalogue.
Carol Danvers lived with her family, her mother Maria flew with her, and the
five years Monica missed took the person she was closest to.

What the story does with her is deliberately unheroic. She acquires her
abilities by walking into a grieving woman's reality and being remade by it,
which means her power and her sympathy for the person who caused it arrive
together and cannot be separated.
