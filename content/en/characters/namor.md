Namor's people were Yucatec, and in 1571 they took a vibranium-laced herb to
survive smallpox brought by colonisers. It saved them and changed them: blue
skin, gills, and a life that could no longer be lived on the surface. They built
Talokan underwater. His mother took the herb while pregnant, so he was born able
to breathe both, with wings at his ankles, and he has been alive ever since.

This is the best villain construction Marvel Studios has managed, because he is
not wrong. Two hidden nations built on the same metal, one of which has just
revealed itself and made the world start looking. Tenoch Huerta Mejía plays him
as someone whose patience is a policy rather than a temperament.
