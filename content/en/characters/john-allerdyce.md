John Allerdyce can manipulate fire but not create it, so he carries a Zippo
everywhere, and that single limitation is the most efficient piece of character
writing in the first three films. He needs a source. He is always looking for
one, and Magneto is happy to be it.

His defection at the end of *X2* is the series at its most economical: the
school is a place that keeps telling him to hold back, and somebody finally
tells him not to.

:::spoiler{title="The Void"}
In *Deadpool & Wolverine* he shoots Cassandra Nova, tells her about Paradox's
plan, and is killed by her for it.
:::
