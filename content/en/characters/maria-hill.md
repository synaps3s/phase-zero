Maria Hill is the person who actually runs S.H.I.E.L.D. while Nick Fury is off
doing whatever Nick Fury does. Cobie Smulders plays her as a commander and
deputy director appointed by Fury as his right hand, and the films use her as
the voice of procedure in a building full of people who improvise. She oversees
the Avengers Initiative, helps fake Fury's death when Hydra moves against him,
and is part of exposing Hydra inside the agency.

When S.H.I.E.L.D. falls she goes to work for Stark Industries, keeps quietly
aiding Phil Coulson's missions on the television side, and comes back to
Fury's side to fight Ultron and for the Battle of Sokovia. After the Avengers'
civil war she leaves Stark and joins Fury's new team. The Snap takes her; five
years later she is back.

:::spoiler{title="Secret Invasion"}
She returns to the field beside Fury to help stop a Skrull attack, and is
killed by Gravik, the Skrull leading it, while he is disguised as somebody she
trusts. It happens during a bombing in Russia that she, Fury and Talos fail to
prevent.
:::

:::detail{title="The one that is not her"}
Cobie Smulders is on screen in *Spider-Man: Far From Home*, but the character
is not Hill: it is the Skrull Soren, Talos's wife, impersonating her while Fury
is off Earth. That film is therefore not in her list. An alternate version of
Hill appears in the animated *What If...?*, which this catalogue does not count
for live-action characters.
:::
