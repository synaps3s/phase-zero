The Grandmaster rules Sakaar, where he hosts the Contest of Champions, and he
is the Collector's brother. Jeff Goldblum plays him almost entirely as himself,
improvising, and the effect is a tyrant who has been in charge so long that
cruelty has become a mannerism rather than a decision.

He is eventually overthrown by his own subjects, which the film treats as a
small comic revolution rather than a triumph, and which he takes about as well
as you would expect.

:::detail{title="Where else he turns up"}
He appears in the sequence after the credits of *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol.
2*, and in *Team Darryl*, a short film from 2018 in which he moves into a flat
in Australia. This catalogue does not carry the short as a separate entry.
:::
