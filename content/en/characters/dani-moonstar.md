Danielle Moonstar is a Native American mutant who can create illusions based on
what other people fear and want. Blu Hunt plays her, and she is the point of
view the film is built around: she arrives at the facility last, so the
audience learns the place with her.

Her power is also the film's mechanism. Once you know what it does, everything
that has been happening in the building has an explanation, and the horror is
that it is not anybody's fault.
