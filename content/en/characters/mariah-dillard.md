Mariah Stokes-Dillard is a local councilwoman, Cornell Stokes's cousin and
Tilda Johnson's mother, looking to bring change to Harlem, whose life is
thrown into turmoil by the actions of Luke Cage and of Stokes. Alfre Woodard
plays her in both seasons of *Luke Cage*, having already played a different
character, Miriam Sharpe, in *Captain America: Civil War*.

The series' version is a long way from the comics' Black Mariah. Wikipedia
records that she is not necessarily a criminal herself, at first, but feels
a responsibility to a family that includes Stokes. The showrunner wanted the
comics nickname in there as a homage, and Woodard agreed on condition that
it be used sensitively. Woodard, who lives in Harlem, took the part after
the showrunner proved his love for the neighbourhood, and called the scripts
some of the smartest writing she had come across.

:::spoiler{title="The end of the second season"}
By the end of it she has embraced her birth name of Stokes, and is arrested
for her illegal activities. In her jail cell she is secretly poisoned by
Tilda, and dies of it.
:::
