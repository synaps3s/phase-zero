Angel Dust is a mutant with adrenaline-based strength and durability, a
member of the Workshop working under Ajax. Gina Carano plays her in
*Deadpool*, and the film gives her the fight it cannot give Wade: while
Deadpool goes after Ajax, she and Colossus take each other apart on the
carrier, and when it starts to collapse he saves her from it. The reader
who sent this list summarised her as the assistant to Ajax who fights
Colossus, which is the whole of her part and a fair account of it.
