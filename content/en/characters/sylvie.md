Sylvie is a Loki who never had Asgard. She was taken by the Time Variance
Authority as a child for a reason nobody will tell her, escaped into a
succession of apocalypses where nothing she did could be detected, and grew up
alone with one goal.

The series is built on the difference between the two of them. One Loki has
spent a thousand years being told he was a prince. The other has spent her life
being told she should not exist, and it is the second one who ends up making
the decision the whole story turns on.
