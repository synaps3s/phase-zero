Medusa is Black Bolt's wife and the queen of Attilan, able to control and move
her hair. Serinda Swan plays her.

Her real function in the story is translation: Black Bolt cannot speak, so she
is his voice, and the marriage is written as a working partnership rather than
a decoration. The series then removes her power in the opening hour for
production reasons that were widely reported, which is the single decision that
did it the most damage.
