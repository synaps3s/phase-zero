Elizabeth Ross, Betty, is a cellular biologist at Culver University, Bruce
Banner's first love, and the daughter of General Thaddeus Ross. Liv Tyler plays
her. She and Banner were recruited together by the United States Army for a
secret programme in bio-tech force enhancement, which is the research that
turned him into the Hulk, and her father is the man who has been hunting him
since. When Banner resurfaces in her life, on the run, she helps him stay ahead
of the authorities.

Then the films leave her for seventeen years, and Banner's life goes
elsewhere.

:::spoiler{title="Brave New World"}
She returns in 2025, visiting her father in the Raft, where he is imprisoned
after destroying the White House as the Red Hulk.
:::
