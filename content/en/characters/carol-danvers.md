Carol Danvers spends most of her own film being told what she is. She is Vers, a
Kree soldier with a short memory, a commander who trains her to control her
abilities and an artificial intelligence that keeps telling her to control her
feelings. The film is
structured so that the audience learns the truth roughly when she does, which
is why it plays quite differently the second time.

What she actually is, is the pilot who was in the room when a light-speed
engine went wrong, and who absorbed the energy of the Tesseract instead of
dying. Yon-Rogg took her to Hala, transfused his own blood into her, and she came out
of it a human-Kree hybrid called Vers, with the power described to her as
borrowed and revocable.

Because her film is set in 1995, she is also the point where the timeline
folds. She is the one who gives Nick Fury the idea, and the reason there is a
pager on his belt in 2018 with one number in it.

:::detail{title="Why the Avengers are called the Avengers"}
Fury meets Danvers on Earth in 1995, and it is her activities that year and her
call sign, "Avenger", that give him the name for the initiative. The whole team
is named after a person none of its members had met.

It is worth noting where this differs from the comics, because it is a real
divergence rather than a detail. There, the Avengers form in response to Loki
and are named by the Wasp.
:::

:::spoiler{level="major" from="captain-marvel"}
The Skrulls are refugees, not invaders, and she ends the film helping them look
for somewhere to live. The Kree are the aggressors, the training was there to
keep her working at a fraction of her strength, and she beats Yon-Rogg without
using any of it before sending him home with a message for the Supreme
Intelligence.
:::
