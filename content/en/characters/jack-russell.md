Jack Russell is a monster hunter of Mexican ancestry who carries a curse that
turns him into a werewolf. Gael García Bernal plays him in a special that runs
under an hour, shot in black and white, and modelled on Universal horror films
from the 1930s.

The trick of it is that the hunters are the villains and the monsters are the
people. Jack enters the Bloodstone hunt in order to free Ted, and the special
never once asks the audience to be afraid of either of them.

:::detail{title="An hour, and no follow-up"}
*Werewolf by Night* was released as a Disney+ special in 2022 and neither of
its leads has appeared since. It is one of the most self-contained things in
this catalogue and one of the few that loses nothing by being watched first.
:::
