Georges Batroc is an Algerian kickboxer, mercenary and pirate at the top of
Interpol's Red Notice, and a former DGSE agent with thirty-six kill missions
behind him before the French government let him go. By 2024 he leads a criminal
group called the LAF.

He is played by Georges St-Pierre, a former mixed martial arts champion, and
that casting is why the opening of *The Winter Soldier* is remembered: the
fight is not stylised, and Rogers does not win it easily.

:::spoiler{title="How it ends for him"}
Sharon Carter lets slip to him that she is the Power Broker, and kills him when
he tries to charge her more for the silence.
:::
