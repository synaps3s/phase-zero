Colleen Wing is a bushido master and the sensei of the Chikara Dojo in New York
City. She is integral to Danny Rand's reintegration into society, which is a
polite way of saying she is the only person who believes him, and she is the
one who foils Ward Meachum's attempt to have him committed indefinitely to
Birch Psychiatric Hospital.

She is also the answer to the most common complaint about her own series.
*Iron Fist* is a story about a man who was given a mystical power, standing
next to a woman who earned everything she can do in a room with a wooden floor.
Jessica Henwick's fight scenes are the ones people rewatch, and by the second
season the show knows it.

:::detail{title="Daughter of the Dragon"}
In the comics, Misty Knight and Colleen Wing are a detective agency called
Nightwing Restorations and are known together as the Daughters of the Dragon.
The series get them into the same frame twice, in *Luke Cage* and then properly
in the second season of *Iron Fist*, and stop there. Nothing that followed on
Disney+ has picked the pairing back up.
:::
