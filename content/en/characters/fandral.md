Fandral, called Fandral the Dashing, is a member of the Warriors Three and a
dashing warrior of Asgard. Joshua Dallas played him in *Thor* and Zachary Levi
in the two films after.

The recasting is the most interesting thing about him, and that is a fair
summary of the problem: three films, three appearances, and no scene that is
his.
