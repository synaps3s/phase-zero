Marie is introduced in her bedroom, talking to her boyfriend David. They kiss,
he falls into a coma, and she leaves home. She meets Logan in a bar in Alberta,
stows away in his truck, and is with him for the whole of the first film, which
is her story more than his.

She is the reason the mutant metaphor works in these films. Her power has no
upside. It cannot be trained, it cannot be aimed, and it makes ordinary
affection dangerous, which is why *The Last Stand* offering a cure lands on her
harder than on anyone else. The film gives her a choice that the audience
argues about to this day.

:::detail{title="The name, and the missing film"}
Wikipedia's list of characters gives her surname as D'Canto in the heading and
spells it D'Ancanto and D'Acanto in the tables on the same page. The films
themselves mostly call her Marie or Rogue, and this catalogue records the
disagreement rather than picking one.

Anna Paquin was cut from the theatrical release of *X-Men: Days of Future
Past*. Her scenes were restored in a longer edit released afterwards as the
Rogue Cut, in which the team recovers her to replace Kitty Pryde. This
catalogue lists appearances in released films, so *Days of Future Past* is not
counted among hers.
:::
