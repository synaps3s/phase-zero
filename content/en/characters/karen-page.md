Karen Page is an office worker at Union Allied who discovers corruption
there, is framed for murder, and then becomes the target of an attempted
one. Matt Murdock and Foggy Nelson help her with the first and the vigilante
with the second, and she joins them as their assistant, determined to take
down the corruption in Hell's Kitchen. Deborah Ann Woll plays her, and Woll
said she wanted a Karen who could be a genuinely kind person and a little
bit attracted to danger at the same time, looking for trouble rather than
falling into it.

She works with the reporter Ben Urich to expose Wilson Fisk. When Fisk's
right-hand man James Wesley discovers the investigation has reached Fisk's
mother, he kidnaps her and tries to blackmail her, and she kills him with
his own gun. It stays a secret. She quits the firm after learning Matt's
identity, and after Urich is murdered she becomes a reporter herself.

From there she is in most of the neighbourhood: *The Defenders*, both
seasons of *The Punisher*, where she is the reporter who befriended Castle
over his case, and *Born Again*, where she was not originally planned to
be and was added after a creative overhaul, as Matt's partner in the firm
and then, in the second season, part of his resistance.
