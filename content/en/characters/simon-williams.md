Simon Williams is an actor with ionic energy based powers, including
superhuman strength and the ability to create explosions. Yahya Abdul-Mateen
II plays him as an adult and Kameron J. Meadows as a child.

*Wonder Man* is a series about the film business rather than about saving
anything, which makes it the most unusual entry in the recent catalogue and
the hardest one to place on a timeline. A reader looking for a fight will not
find one.
