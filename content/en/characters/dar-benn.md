Supremor Dar-Benn is a revolutionary Kree warrior and an Accuser of the Kree
Empire, fighting to restore the Kree homeworld Hala after it was plunged
into a devastating civil war by Carol Danvers's killing of the Supreme
Intelligence. Zawe Ashton plays her in *The Marvels*. The reader who sent
this list called her the Kree Accuser who declared vengeance on Carol
Danvers, and the film is on her side of the argument more than you might
expect.

Having found a Quantum Band on MB-418, she tears apart jump points across
space to extract resources from other planets, Tarnax IV, Aladna and Earth
among them, ruins peace talks between the Kree and the Skrulls, and comes
into conflict with Danvers, Monica Rambeau and Kamala Khan, who wears the
other Band.

:::spoiler{title="The Sun"}
Before she can siphon energy from Earth's Sun to reignite Pama, the
Marvels intervene, but she takes the second Band from Kamala. Trying to
harness both, she opens a multiversal wormhole out of spite, and its energy
disintegrates her.
:::
