Judge Ravonna Renslayer is a former Minuteman of the Time Variance
Authority, codenamed Hunter A-23, who rose through the ranks to become a
respected judge, and who oversees the investigation into the Loki variant in
the first season. Gugu Mbatha-Raw plays her. Before the TVA took her, she
was a vice-principal called Rebecca Tourminet in Fremont, Ohio. The reader
who sent this list wondered whether she had feelings for He Who Remains;
what Wikipedia records is that she followed the roadmap he left.

:::spoiler{title="1893"}
In the second season she and Miss Minutes follow that roadmap, left by He
Who Remains before his death, to create a new version of him in the new
multiverse. They find Victor Timely as a child in the nineteenth century and
leave him a TVA handbook, and in 1893, when Loki and Mobius come for the
adult Timely, Renslayer, Miss Minutes and Sylvie interrupt them. Later she
kidnaps Timely from the TVA with Miss Minutes and X-5, and while they are
interrogating him Sylvie's enchantment makes X-5 prune her.
:::
