Ted, known as Man-Thing, is a swamp monster and a friend of Jack Russell. The
Bloodstone family capture him to serve as the quarry for their hunt, and Russell
enters the hunt to get him out, which is the entire plot of the special.

Calling him Ted is the joke and the point. He is treated by everyone else in the
special as an animal and a trophy, and by one person as a friend with a name,
and the film sides with the second view without arguing about it.
