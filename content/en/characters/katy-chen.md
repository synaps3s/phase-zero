Katy Chen parks cars at a San Francisco hotel with her best friend, who turns
out to be Shang-Chi, the son of the man who runs the Ten Rings. She did not
know. Awkwafina plays her, and the film gives her
the job the audience has: asking the obvious questions while everyone else
behaves as if this is normal.

She goes with him to find his sister Xialing, is captured with both of them by
their father Wenwu, and escapes to the hidden village of Ta Lo with Trevor
Slattery in tow. In the battle there she helps defend the village against the
Dweller-in-Darkness by putting an arrow into a weak spot in its armour, which
nobody expected of her, including her.

:::spoiler{title="Karaoke"}
The mid-credits scene has Wong summon her and Shang-Chi to Kamar-Taj, where
Bruce Banner and Carol Danvers, by hologram, work out that the rings are
sending a signal somewhere. Wong promises to keep looking. Shang-Chi suggests a
bar, and the three of them sing karaoke.
:::

:::detail{title="Her family, and a variant"}
Her mother is played by Jodi Long, her brother Ruihua by Dallas Liu, and her
grandmother by Tsai Chin. Her Chinese name is Chen Ruiwen. A version of Katy
appears in the animated *Marvel Zombies*, which this catalogue does not count
for live-action characters.
:::
