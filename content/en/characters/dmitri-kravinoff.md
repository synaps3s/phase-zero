Dmitri Kravinoff is Kraven's estranged half-brother and a master of
disguise, capable of mimicking other people's voices. Fred Hechinger plays
him in *Kraven the Hunter*, with Billy Barratt as the boy their father
Nikolai drags into the wild with Sergei after their mother's death. The
reader who sent this list said he is so good at impressions he is
practically a Chameleon, which is the joke the film is building to.

Sixteen years later Kraven comes to London for Dmitri's birthday, and
mercenaries take Dmitri during the reunion. Nikolai refuses to pay the
ransom. The man behind the kidnapping is Aleksei Sytsevich, the Rhino, who
proposes an alliance to overthrow Nikolai, and Dmitri is the bait in the
war that follows.

:::spoiler{title="One year later"}
Kraven visits Dmitri and finds that he has willingly inherited their
father's criminal empire, and that Miles Warren has given him the ability to
change his shape. Dmitri tells him that for all his claims of being the
moral one, he and Nikolai were the same: hunters looking for the next
trophy.
:::
