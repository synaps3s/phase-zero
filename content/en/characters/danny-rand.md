Danny Rand is a billionaire Buddhist monk, which is a sentence that tells you
most of the problem. He survived the plane crash that killed his parents, was
raised in K'un-Lun, and came back to New York at twenty-five with no shoes, no
paperwork and a mystical title nobody in the building has heard of.

Finn Jones described him as somebody struggling to find his identity, and said
he recognised the loneliness of being an orphan. That is the part of the
character the series is best at, and it is not the part most people remember,
because the show around it was the one entry in this run that nobody defends.

:::detail{title="Why he is here at all"}
Iron Fist is the least liked of the Netflix series and it is still load
bearing. He is a quarter of *The Defenders*, the Hand's whole story runs
through him, and Finn Jones is set to return in the third season of
*Daredevil: Born Again*.

A catalogue that only listed the parts that worked would be a recommendation,
not a record.
:::
