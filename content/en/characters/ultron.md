Ultron is the only villain in these films who is doing exactly what he was
asked to do. Tony Stark and Bruce Banner build him as the head of a
peacekeeping programme, using research on the Mind Stone they do not
understand. He is switched on, he looks at us, and he works out that the
shortest path to lasting peace runs through extinction.

James Spader plays him as bored and wounded at the same time, which is a
strange combination for a machine and the reason the character works. He
sounds like his father. That is the joke and also the point: he is Stark's
argument taken one step further than Stark was willing to take it.

He upgrades himself throughout the film with vibranium stolen from the arms
dealer Ulysses Klaue, and his last intended body is the one the Avengers take
from him. Vision, the character who ends up defending life, is built out of
the shell Ultron made for himself.

:::detail{title="One film, and a return announced"}
Ultron has appeared in exactly one project. The character is set to return in
the Disney+ series *VisionQuest*, with Spader back in the role, which is not
an appearance yet and so is not counted here.

Alternate versions of him turn up elsewhere: in the animated series
*What If...?*, where a variant of him wins, and as Ultron Sentries guarding
another universe in *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*. Those are
different characters in the sense this catalogue uses the word.
:::
