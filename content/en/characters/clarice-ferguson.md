Clarice Ferguson is a mutant with purple hair, large green eyes and the ability
to open portals, and one of the surviving mutants in the future. Fan Bingbing
plays her.

The two future battles in *Days of Future Past* are the best action in the Fox
films and Blink is the reason. The team fights as one machine because she can
put anyone anywhere, and the film shows the tactic rather than the power.
