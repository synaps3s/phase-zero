Carter Slade is the Caretaker, which is how the credits of *Ghost Rider*
list him. Sam Elliott plays him, and narrates the film. He is a former Texas
Ranger from the nineteenth century and the first Ghost Rider, the one who
was sent for the contract of San Venganza, saw what it would give the
Devil, and hid it instead. To Johnny he is an ally and a mentor: the man who
tells him that what happened to him was real, that it will happen again at
night near an evil soul, and that a predecessor named Carter Slade hid the
contract inside a spade.

:::spoiler{title="Who he is"}
When Johnny comes for the contract to save Roxanne, the Caretaker gives it
to him, tells him he is stronger than the Riders before him because he sold
his soul for love rather than greed, and reveals that he is Carter Slade.
He rides with Johnny to San Venganza, hands him a lever-action shotgun, and
having finally shaken off the curse, fades into dust as he rides away.
:::
