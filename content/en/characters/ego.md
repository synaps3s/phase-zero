Ego is a Celestial and the father of Peter Quill and Mantis. He rescues the
Guardians from the Sovereign's drones, takes them to his planet, which is an
extension of himself, and begins teaching Peter to use the Celestial energy he
inherited. Then he explains the rest: he seeded thousands of worlds, and
turning those seeds into more of him needs the power of two Celestials.

Kurt Russell plays him warm, which is what makes the film work. The scene is
a father and a son throwing a ball, and it stays that scene while it becomes
the worst thing in the film.

:::spoiler{title="What he did to get here"}
The seedlings needed a second Celestial, so Ego fathered children across the
galaxy and killed each one that could not use the power. He also gave Meredith
Quill the tumour that killed her, because he was starting to love her and did
not want to be held. Peter chooses Yondu over him, which is the film's whole
argument in one line.
:::
