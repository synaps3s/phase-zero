Davos is a skilled martial artist, the son of Lei Kung, and Danny Rand's former
best friend in K'un-Lun. He grew envious when Rand became the Iron Fist. That
one sentence is the whole engine of the character, and the second season is
built on it.

He is the most sympathetic villain in this corner of the catalogue, because his
grievance is legitimate. He did the work. He stayed. Rand left, abandoned the
post he was given, and still holds the title. The series never quite argues
Davos is wrong about that. It only argues about what he decides to do next.

:::detail{title="Where the second season goes"}
The second season of *Iron Fist* is generally held to be a large improvement on
the first, and Davos is the main reason cited. It was also the last one: Netflix
cancelled the series in October 2018, a month after the season was released.
:::
