Mercedes Kelly Knight, who nobody calls Mercedes, is a detective at the 29th
Precinct with a strong sense of justice and a habit of standing in an empty
street until it tells her what happened there. She is introduced as Rafael
Scarfe's partner and as the person most determined to find out who Luke Cage
actually is.

She is the argument the Netflix run keeps having with itself. Four people in
Manhattan have decided the law is too slow, and Misty Knight is the one who
still turns up with a badge and a notebook and does it properly. The series
does not let her win that argument, but it never treats her as naive for
making it either.

:::spoiler{title="The arm"}
She loses her right arm in an encounter with Bakuto. Claire Temple and Colleen
Wing help her through the recovery, and she is later given a prosthetic built
by Danny Rand and Colleen Wing. In the comics that arm is her defining
feature. Here it arrives late, and the show spends more time on what losing it
costs her than on what it gives her.
:::
