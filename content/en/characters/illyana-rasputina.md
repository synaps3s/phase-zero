Illyana Nikolaievna Rasputina is a Russian mutant with sorcery, able to
manifest the Soulsword and to travel by teleportation discs. Anya Taylor-Joy
plays her.

She is the most difficult character in the film and the best served by it. The
place she escapes into is not a refuge, and what she does there tells you what
was done to her before she arrived.

:::detail{title="Whose sister"}
Illyana is Piotr Rasputin's sister, and this catalogue carries him separately.
The two films never put them in a frame together and the Fox series ended
before they could.
:::
