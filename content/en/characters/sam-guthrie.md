Samuel Zachary Guthrie is an American mutant from Kentucky who can propel
himself into the air and is invulnerable while doing it. Charlie Heaton plays
him.

The five children in this film are held on the argument that they are
dangerous, and each of them arrived believing it. Sam is the clearest case: his
power went off underground, in a mine, with his father's crew in it.
