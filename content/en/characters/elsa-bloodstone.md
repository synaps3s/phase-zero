Elsa Bloodstone is the estranged daughter of Ulysses Bloodstone, and she
dislikes her family's tradition of hunting monsters. She comes back for her
father's funeral and the hunt that decides who inherits the Bloodstone, and
Laura Donnelly plays her as someone who has already decided the whole
institution is grotesque and is only here for what is hers.

:::detail{title="One appearance"}
Wikipedia records a single project for her as of 2026: the Disney+ special
*Werewolf by Night*.
:::
