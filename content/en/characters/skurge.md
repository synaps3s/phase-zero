Skurge is Loki's choice to replace Heimdall as gatekeeper of the Bifrost, and
is promoted to Executioner when Hela takes Asgard. He is played, for most of
the film, as comic and servile and a little vacant: a man who wants to be near
power and does not think about what he is being asked to do.

Then he sees what is happening to the people of Asgard, changes sides, and
sacrifices himself covering their evacuation. Karl Urban gets about four
minutes to make that turn believable and does it.

:::detail{title="Ragnarok, and the comics"}
In the comics the Executioner is Skurge, and the moment he is remembered for is
holding a bridge alone so that others can escape. *Thor: Ragnarok* does not
adapt that story, but it gives him the same ending, in a doorway, with the same
point.
:::
