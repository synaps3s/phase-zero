Doctor Octopus is the best-constructed antagonist in the Raimi films because he
is Peter's mentor first. Alfred Molina plays a warm, funny man who is genuinely
kind to a student, and the film spends real time on that before the accident
that removes the inhibitor chip and lets the arms talk.

His return in *No Way Home* is the film's clearest statement of intent: the plan
is not to defeat these people, it is to fix them, and he is the first one it
works on.
