Melinda Qiaolian May is a S.H.I.E.L.D. pilot and weapons expert, nicknamed the
Cavalry against her wishes after a mission in Bahrain where she saved an entire
S.H.I.E.L.D. team from a rogue Inhuman. Ming-Na Wen plays her.

She is the best-written character in that series and the reason is restraint:
she says very little, and the show waits two seasons before explaining the
nickname.

:::spoiler{title="Bahrain"}
What S.H.I.E.L.D. does not know is how she saved them. She killed a young girl,
Katya Belyakov, and she has not moved past it years later.
:::
