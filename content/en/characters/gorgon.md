Gorgon is Black Bolt's cousin and the leader of Attilan's Royal Guard, and he
can generate seismic waves with his hooves. Eme Ikwuakor plays him.

He gets the closest thing the series has to a good run: stranded among surfers
on Oahu who accept him immediately, which is the only sequence in *Inhumans*
where somebody is glad to meet an Inhuman.
