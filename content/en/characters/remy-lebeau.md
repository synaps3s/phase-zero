Remy LeBeau is a Cajun mutant who can charge any object with kinetic energy and
make it explode, which the films have almost always shown him doing with a
playing card. In *X-Men Origins: Wolverine* he is a man Stryker held captive
and lost, living in New Orleans, who fights Logan on a rooftop before agreeing
to take him to the island. In *Deadpool & Wolverine* he lives in the Void
alongside Blade and Elektra, opposing Cassandra Nova.

He is the clearest example of what happened to this film series. He was
supposed to be much more than this.

:::detail{title="Two actors, and the film that never happened"}
Taylor Kitsch played him in 2009 and Channing Tatum in 2024, and they are not
presented as the same man: the second lives in the Void with a much heavier
accent. Gambit was written into *X2*, where James Bamford was cast, and cut. He
was cut from *The Last Stand*. Tatum was set to play him in *Apocalypse*, and
was cut from that too, and the solo film that was meant to follow was pushed
back repeatedly, lost several directors, and was shelved in 2019 after Disney
bought Fox.

Tatum has been announced to return in *Avengers: Doomsday*.
:::
