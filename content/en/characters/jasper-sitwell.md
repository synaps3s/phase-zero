Jasper Sitwell is a S.H.I.E.L.D. agent who spends the first years of the shared
story in the margins: at the hammer site in *Thor*, across the table from Phil
Coulson in two of the short films, briefly in *The Avengers*. Maximiliano
Hernández plays him as the steady, slightly harried colleague you never think
about twice, which is the whole trick.

S.H.I.E.L.D. is the spy agency that runs through the early films, and Hydra is
the organisation that grew inside it in secret. Sitwell was one of Hydra's
sleeper agents. *Captain America: The Winter Soldier* is where that comes out:
Steve Rogers, Natasha Romanoff and Sam Wilson catch him, force Hydra's plans out
of him, and he is killed for that betrayal.

*Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*, the television series, fills in the earlier part of
the story. Adam Faison plays a younger Sitwell in a flashback, and the fifth
season episode "Rise and Shine" reveals that he attended a Hydra academy in his
youth. So he was never a S.H.I.E.L.D. agent who turned. He was Hydra before he
joined.

:::spoiler{title="Who kills him, and why he comes back"}
The Winter Soldier kills him in an ambush, and it is in that ambush that Rogers
recognises the Winter Soldier as Bucky Barnes. *Avengers: Endgame* brings
Sitwell back only because the heroes travel to 2012: Wikipedia counts the
Sitwell they meet there as a variant from an alternate timeline, and this
entry does too.
:::
