Phastos is an Eternal and an inventor who spent thousands of years pushing
human technology forward from behind the scenes. He abandoned that after the
bombing of Hiroshima, and by 2024 he lives under the human name Phil with his
husband Ben and their ten year old son Jack.

He is the sharpest version of the film's guilt. Every other Eternal is asking
whether not interfering was right; Phastos is the one who did interfere, as
helpfully as he knew how, and watched what it was used for.

:::detail{title="A first"}
Phastos, played by Brian Tyree Henry, is the first superhero depicted as gay in
these films. Haaz Sleiman plays Ben and Esai Daniel Cross plays Jack.
:::
