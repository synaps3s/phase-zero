Franklin "Foggy" Percy Nelson met Matt Murdock at law school and became his
best friend. After interning together at Landman and Zack, the two start
their own firm in Hell's Kitchen, where Foggy wants to fight for the little
guy. Elden Henson plays him across the Netflix years and into the Disney+
revival, and by the time the revival came around Charlie Cox was describing
him as the heartbeat of the whole thing.

His friendship with Matt falters when he discovers the vigilante work, and
the two are close again after they bring Fisk down. Between the Daredevil
seasons he turns up in the neighbouring series: working with Jeri Hogarth in
*The Defenders*, and as a guest in *Jessica Jones* and *Luke Cage*.

:::spoiler{title="Born Again"}
He is killed at the start of *Daredevil: Born Again*, shot by Dex at
Vanessa's behest, and his death is the wound the first season is built on.
Henson's return for the second season set off speculation that the show
would adapt the comics story in which Foggy fakes his death and enters
witness protection; the makers said that in their street-level stories
dying very often means you are actually dead, and he appears in flashbacks
set before the events of *Daredevil*.
:::

:::detail{title="The tie bar"}
The costume designer Stephanie Maslansky gave him one accessory to define
his look: a vintage tie bar with the letter F, which she imagined was a gift
from his father. His socks are patterned and colourful. The audience may
never see them, she said, but the actor does.
:::
