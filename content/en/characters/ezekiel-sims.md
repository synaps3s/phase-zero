Ezekiel Sims is the man Constance Webb hired for protection when her research
team went into the Peruvian Amazon in 1973 looking for a spider with rare
healing properties. Tahar Rahim plays him in *Madame Web*. He betrays the
team, shoots Constance in a struggle and claims the spider for himself; from
it he gains enhanced strength and health, and a limited clairvoyance that
shows him his own death. It leads him to hunt three young women who have the
potential to become Spider-Women, obsessively, before they can become it.

He can inject a neurotoxin into anyone he touches, and wears a black and red
suit that Wikipedia describes as similar to Spider-Man's. He is, in short,
the film's Spider-Man, on the wrong side.

:::spoiler{title="The fireworks factory"}
Cassie Webb, Constance's daughter, keeps getting between him and the girls:
at Grand Central, at a diner, at a hospital. In the end the group lures him
to a condemned fireworks factory and sets traps to disorient him, and
Cassie guides the girls out and lures him into the last one.
:::
