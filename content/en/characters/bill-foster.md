Bill Foster is a physicist who was Hank Pym's assistant on Project Goliath and
the Giant-Man of his day. Laurence Fishburne plays him. In 2018 he is teaching
quantum physics at UC Berkeley when Pym, Scott Lang and Hope van Dyne come
looking for help, and the film gets a great deal out of two old men who each remember the other
as the difficult one.

He is also the surrogate father of Ava Starr, the Ghost, since her own father
Elihas died, and everything he does in the film is for her. When Ava restrains
Pym, Lang and Hope, Foster explains that he has been working to cure her by
drawing quantum energy from the Quantum Realm. Pym knows the plan would wreck
the rescue of Janet van Dyne, and the three of them get away.

:::spoiler{title="The end of the film"}
Pym talks him down before going into the Quantum Realm, and promises to find
another way to help Ava, and his ants escort Foster out. When Janet is
rescued she gives Ava some of her own energy to stabilise her, and Foster takes
Ava away, with Pym still promising a permanent cure.
:::
