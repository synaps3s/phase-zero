Hank McCoy is the character the Fox films use to ask whether a mutation is
something to be treated. He is a scientist whose own mutation is mild enough to
hide, so he builds a serum to suppress it, and it does the opposite: it makes
him blue, large and unmistakable.

Nicholas Hoult plays the version that carries the arc, and Kelsey Grammer plays
the older one in *The Last Stand*, arguing for mutant rights from inside a
cabinet. Both are the same idea seen from two ends of a life.

:::detail{title="A different Beast"}
Grammer plays a version of McCoy in *The Marvels* as well. The reference article
records that as a different version of the character rather than a continuation
of this one, so it is not listed here.
:::
