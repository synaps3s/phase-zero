Fred Dukes is a mutant with increased strength and durability who served with
Logan and Victor Creed on Team X. After the team disbands he gains a great deal
of weight and takes up boxing, and *X-Men Origins: Wolverine* finds him there.
He fights Logan, loses, and gives up the information about Weapon XI that the
plot needs. Sabretooth kills him later.

He is a small part, and the reason he is in this catalogue is that Team X is
the only place several of these characters ever shared a scene.

:::detail{title="Three versions"}
Kevin Durand plays him in *Origins*. Gustav Claude Ouimet appears briefly in
*Apocalypse*, defeated by Angel in a cage match. Mike Waters plays a variant of
Blob in the Void in *Deadpool & Wolverine*. Blob was written into early drafts
of the first *X-Men* film and cut.
:::
