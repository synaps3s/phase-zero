Blackagar Boltagon, called Black Bolt, is the head of the Inhuman Royal Family
and the king of Attilan, and his voice can cause destruction with the slightest
whisper. Anson Mount plays him.

The premise is a genuinely good one for television and the series does almost
nothing with it. A king who cannot speak has to be played entirely in the face
and the hands, and *Inhumans* strands him for most of its eight episodes in a
place where nobody knows who he is.

:::detail{title="The other one"}
A variant of Black Bolt, also played by Mount, appears in
*Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* as a member of the Illuminati.
That is a different man in a different universe, and it is the only time these
films have acknowledged the series at all.
:::
