Crystal is Medusa's sister and the youngest member of the royal family, able to
control the elements. Isabelle Cornish plays her.

She is the one member of the family who is treated as a child by everybody
including the script, and the series is at its most interesting when she stops
accepting that. Lockjaw, the teleporting dog, is hers, and is the thing most
people remember about the show.
