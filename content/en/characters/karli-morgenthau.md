Karli Morgenthau is the teenage leader of the Flag Smashers, an
anti-nationalist group Wikipedia describes as terrorist, made into super
soldiers with the help of the Power Broker and using violent tactics,
bombings among them, to achieve open borders for refugees in the Baltic
states. Erin Kellyman plays her in *The Falcon and the Winter Soldier*. The
reader who sent this list described her as a young girl who wanted to make
sure there were no more borders for the world, which is how she would put
it herself.

:::spoiler{title="The Power Broker"}
The Power Broker is Sharon Carter. Karli is killed by her, to hide that
identity, after Karli tries to kill Carter and an oblivious Sam Wilson.
:::
