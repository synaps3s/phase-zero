Cassandra Lang is the daughter of Maggie and Scott Lang. Her parents divorced
over her father's criminal record, and her mother is with the police officer
Jim Paxton. In 2015 she is held hostage by Darren Cross. She is played by three
actors across four films, which is the clearest sign of how long these films
have been running.

She is one of the few characters here whose story is entirely about the Blip
rather than about a fight. Scott is gone for five years that he does not
experience, and comes back to a daughter who has lived every day of them.
