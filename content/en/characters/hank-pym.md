Hank Pym is the reason any of this works and he is not a pleasant man about it.
He discovered the particle, wore the suit, lost his wife to the Quantum Realm
using it, and then spent decades making sure nobody else could repeat any of
that, including his own daughter and the government he used to work for.

The films are unusually clear-eyed about him. His caution is proved correct
almost immediately, and it is also the thing that has damaged everyone close to
him. Both are true at once and neither cancels the other.

:::detail{title="Which name the sources give"}
The reference entry for the version in these films calls him Dr. Henry "Hank"
Pym. The comics article gives Henry Jonathan Pym, but that describes the comics
character, and no page about the film version supplies a middle name.

This catalogue records Henry Pym and cites both pages, which is the honest
position when two sources are describing two different men with the same name.
:::
