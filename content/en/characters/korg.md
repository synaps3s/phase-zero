Korg is Taika Waititi in a motion capture suit, and he is the clearest signal
of what happened to the Thor films when Waititi arrived: they got funnier, and
the jokes started coming from characters rather than at their expense.

The trick with Korg is the register. He describes a failed armed uprising, the
death of his friends and his own imprisonment in the same mild, friendly tone
he uses for everything else, and the films never explain the joke.
