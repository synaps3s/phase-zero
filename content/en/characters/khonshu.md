Khonshu is the Egyptian moon god, an outcast among the gods for waging what
Wikipedia calls a one-god war on perceived injustices, which is why he
needs an avatar, Marc Spector, to act through. F. Murray Abraham is the
voice and Karim El Hakim the motion capture. He is based on the Egyptian
deity of the same name, which is spelled Khonsu; the reader who sent this
list used that spelling, and the series uses this one.

:::detail{title="What is not counted"}
An alternate version of him appeared in the animated *Marvel Zombies*,
which this catalogue does not count for live-action people.
:::
