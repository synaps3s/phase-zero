Cyclops is the most thankless part in these films. He is the field leader, the
rule follower and the one who is correct about the risk, and the story is
usually about somebody who is not. James Marsden played him for three films and
was written out at the start of the third, which is one of the reasons that film
is remembered the way it is.

Tye Sheridan's version gets the better introduction: a teenager whose power
arrives with no warning at all, in a school corridor, and cannot be turned off
again.
