Kingo can project cosmic energy from his hands. Taken with fame, he blends in
on Earth by being a popular Bollywood actor and director, and he runs a
production company in Mumbai.

Kumail Nanjiani plays the film's only real comedy, and it works because the
joke has a floor under it: a man who cannot die has to keep restarting a public
life every few decades, and he decided to enjoy it.
