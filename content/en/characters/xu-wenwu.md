Xu Wenwu is the correction of a joke. The Ten Rings were named in *Iron Man* in
2008 and used as a front twice more, and this film finally puts a person at the
head of them: a conqueror who has been alive for centuries, has run the
organisation through most of them, and gave it up when he met Ying Li at the
gate of a hidden village.

Tony Leung plays him without any of the theatre the part invites. What is left
is a father who trained one child into a weapon and locked the other out of the
room, and who is now being told by a voice he believes is his wife's that she
is still alive behind a door.

:::detail{title="Three films' worth of cleanup"}
The Ten Rings appear as the kidnappers in *Iron Man*, as a name borrowed by
Aldrich Killian's fake Mandarin in *Iron Man 3*, and in the one-shot
*All Hail the King*, which establishes that the real organisation exists and is
annoyed. This film is the one that says who was actually in charge.
:::
