Bobby Drake is one of the students at Xavier's school, a mutant who can make
ice, and the boy Rogue likes, which is a problem for both of them because
Rogue cannot touch anyone. Shawn Ashmore plays him across four films, from a
face in the corridor to a member of the X-Men.

*X2* gives him his best scene. When the school is raided he gets the remaining
students out and takes them to his parents' house, where it turns out that his
parents, William and Madeline, had no idea what kind of school it was, and his
younger brother Ronny calls the authorities on the lot of them. It is the
series' clearest statement of what it has been using mutants to talk about,
and it is done in a family home.

By *The Last Stand* his closeness to Kitty Pryde, and Rogue's inability to
touch him, are what push Rogue towards the mutant cure. He joins the X-Men for
the battle at Alcatraz, beats Pyro, and turns his whole body to ice for the
first time doing it.

:::spoiler{title="The future, and the future after that"}
In *Days of Future Past* he is one of the survivors of the Sentinels' war on
mutants, and he is killed once before Bishop resets the day. In the longer
Rogue Cut of the film he dies a second time, saving Rogue. In the revised
timeline the film ends on, he is back at the school and dating her.
:::
