Jane Foster is an astrophysicist, played by Natalie Portman, and she appears in
*Thor*, *Thor: The Dark World* and *Thor: Love and Thunder*, where she becomes
the Mighty Thor.

The first two films use her as the reason Thor cares about Earth, which is a
thin job for a good actor, and Wikipedia is blunt that the reception of those
two was mixed. *Love and Thunder* gives her the story from the comics instead:
Mjolnir reassembles itself for her while she is having chemotherapy, and every
time she lifts it she undoes the treatment. That version was much better
received, and is often named as the best thing in the film.

:::detail{title="The versions that are not her"}
Alternate Janes appear in *Avengers: Endgame*, where the team travels back to
2013, and in the animated *What If...?*. Neither is this Jane living forward.
:::
