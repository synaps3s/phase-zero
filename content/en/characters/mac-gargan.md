MacDonald Gargan is a professional criminal and one of Adrian Toomes's
potential buyers. After an encounter with Spider-Man on the Staten Island ferry
in 2017 he is arrested by the FBI and swears revenge, and while recovering he
approaches Toomes in prison on the strength of a rumour, wanting to know
Spider-Man's identity.

That prison scene, after the credits of *Homecoming*, sat unresolved for nine
years. It is one of the longest running unpaid setups in this catalogue.
