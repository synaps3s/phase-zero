Adam Warlock is a perfect Sovereign created by Ayesha to destroy the Guardians
of the Galaxy. He has the mentality of a child, because the High Evolutionary
forced Ayesha to bring him out of the cocoon before he was ready in order to
use him against Rocket.

That decision is the character. He is enormously strong and has no idea what he
is doing, and *Vol. 3* uses him as a study of what it is like to be made into
somebody's instrument, which is what the entire film is about.

:::spoiler{title="Where he ends up"}
Counter-Earth is destroyed and his mother is killed. Gamora captures him, he
fails to kill Quill, and when the High Evolutionary abandons his own creations
Adam saves Quill's life instead. He joins the team Rocket assembles at the end.
:::
