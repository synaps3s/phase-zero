Emma Frost is a mutant with telepathic abilities who can turn her skin into
diamond, played by January Jones. She acts as Shaw's second in command until
the X-Men capture her in the Soviet Union, and Magneto later frees her and she
joins his Brotherhood.

The diamond form is the interesting half. It makes her immune to telepathy,
which in a film about two telepaths is a real tactical fact rather than a
costume.

:::detail{title="Two near misses and one lookalike"}
Frost was going to appear in *The Last Stand*, played by Sigourney Weaver, and
did not after Bryan Singer left the project. A character based on her, named
Emma Silverfox and played by Tahyna Tozzi, appears in
*X-Men Origins: Wolverine* as Kayla Silverfox's sister and a prisoner of
Stryker. Wikipedia describes that one as based on Emma Frost rather than as
her, so this catalogue does not count it among her appearances.

She is killed after the events of *First Class* by Trask's experiments.
:::
