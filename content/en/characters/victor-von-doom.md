Doctor Doom has been in Marvel comics since 1962 and has been waiting for this
for a long time. What exists on screen so far is a mid-credits appearance at the
end of *The Fantastic Four: First Steps*, establishing him as the despotic ruler
of Latveria.

The casting is the story: Robert Downey Jr., returning to these films as
somebody else entirely, six years after Tony Stark's funeral. There is nothing
yet to say about the performance beyond that it exists, and this entry will be
worth rewriting when there is.

:::detail{title="An honest entry"}
This is the shortest character entry in the catalogue, because the character
has appeared for well under a minute. He is announced for *Avengers: Doomsday*
and *Avengers: Secret Wars*, which are not out. Writing more than this would
mean writing about the comics or about the announcements, and neither is what
this catalogue is for.
:::
