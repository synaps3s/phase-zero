Sharon Carter is the great-niece of S.H.I.E.L.D.'s founder Peggy Carter. She
follows her aunt into the agency and takes the codename Agent 13. On Fury's
orders she watches over Steve Rogers as an undercover nurse, and then helps him
bring down a S.H.I.E.L.D. that Hydra had been inside for seventy years. She
joins the CIA afterwards, and goes into hiding after helping Rogers during the
Avengers' civil war.

Then the films leave her there. She survives the Blip in exile, unpardoned,
while the people she helped are celebrated.

:::spoiler{title="The Power Broker"}
By the time of *The Falcon and the Winter Soldier* she has established herself
in Madripoor as the Power Broker, running the city's criminal underworld. She
is pardoned and reinstated at the end of the series without anyone finding
out. The turn divided viewers, and this catalogue notes that the reaction to
the character was generally positive and the reaction to this specific
development was not.
:::
