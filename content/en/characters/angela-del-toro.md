Angela del Toro is Hector Ayala's teenage niece, and her part of the series is
the one that indicts everybody else in it. After her uncle's murder she works
out that he had been investigating a string of killings by the assassin Muse,
and she takes that to Matt Murdock, who does nothing with it.

So she goes after Muse herself, and is kidnapped for it. Murdock reaches her
before Muse can kill her, which is a rescue that only had to happen because he
would not listen the first time.

:::detail{title="Why she is a separate entry"}
She and her uncle share a name, an amulet and a mantle, and this catalogue
still gives them a row each: they are two people, and what happens to the
second one is a consequence of what happened to the first.
:::
