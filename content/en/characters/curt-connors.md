Curt Connors is the closest thing the Webb films have to a good villain,
because his goal is not power. He wants his arm back, and then he wants everyone
who has lost something to get it back too, which is where it stops being medicine
and starts being a plan for the city.

Rhys Ifans plays the scientist rather than the monster, and the film is at its
best in the lab. *No Way Home* is oddly kind to him: he is one of the characters
the plot exists to fix.
