Mephisto is a powerful demon from outside this dimension whose method is the
pact: he manipulates people into making deals with him, and what he gives is
real. Sacha Baron Cohen plays him. He is the one who gave Parker Robbins the
cloak that makes him the Hood, which is the source of most of what goes wrong
in *Ironheart*.

The series' executive producer Zoie Nagelhout described him as a way of tying
the characters' journeys together, Riri's in particular. Cohen worked with the
trainer Alfonso Moretti to get noticeably bigger for the part in a short time,
quickly enough that the costume had to be altered to keep fitting him.
