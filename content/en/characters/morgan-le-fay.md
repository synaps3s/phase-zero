Morgan le Fay is the villain of the third and last season of *Runaways*,
played by Elizabeth Hurley. She is the enchantress of Arthurian legend as the
comics reimagined her: a powerful sorceress and the leader of a coven of
witches, trapped in the Dark Dimension, the place the films first showed in
*Doctor Strange*.

She gets out through Nico Minoru. She manipulates Nico into accepting her
abilities as a witch and joining the coven, and once free she takes the
Darkhold, enthralls Nico's father Robert, and takes over his company WIZARD,
whose Corvus WizPhones become the means of building an army of slaves. All the
while she keeps trying to take the Staff of One from Nico.

:::spoiler{title="How the series ends her"}
The Runaways and the Pride, the children and their parents together for once,
foil the plan, and Nico's mother Tina banishes her back to the Dark Dimension.
:::

:::detail{title="The coven, and the connections"}
Scarlett Byrne plays Bronwyn, a member of her coven. The Dark Dimension and
the Darkhold both come from the films and series around this one, which makes
her season one of the points where a Marvel Television show borrowed most
directly from the shared story.
:::
