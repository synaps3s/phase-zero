Corvus Glaive is one of Thanos's adopted sons, voiced and motion captured by
Michael James Shaw. In 2018 he joins his father's search for the six Infinity
Stones along with Proxima Midnight, Ebony Maw and Cull Obsidian, beginning with
the attack on the Statesman carrying Thor and the Asgardian refugees.

He and Midnight are the pair sent for the Mind Stone, which is why they are the
ones who ambush Vision.
