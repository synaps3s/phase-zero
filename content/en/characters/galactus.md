Galactus is a gigantic cosmic being who consumes the life force of planets, and
he comes from the Earth-828 timeline, which is the one the Fantastic Four live
in. He was a mortal man before he became this. He targets that Earth and sends
his herald, the Silver Surfer, ahead to announce its last days. The Fantastic
Four try to negotiate.

Ralph Ineson plays him as something closer to weather than to a villain. There
is no bargaining position and no grudge, which is the correct reading of the
character and the one the earlier film adaptations avoided.

:::detail{title="Where else he has appeared"}
Wikipedia records two projects as of 2026: *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* and
the one-shot comic *Fantastic Four: First Foes - Shalla-Bal*. Ineson had
appeared in this continuity once before, as a Ravager pilot in
*Guardians of the Galaxy*.
:::
