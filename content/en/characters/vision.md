Vision is assembled out of three things that did not want to be combined: a
synthetic vibranium body Ultron built for himself, the Mind Stone, and the
software of J.A.R.V.I.S., which had spent years being Tony Stark's house. The
Avengers fight over whether to switch it on, and Thor decides for them.

What comes out is the only character in this material with no history at all,
which the films use well. He has no grudge, no country and no childhood, so he
reasons about everything from the beginning, and he is the one who says the
uncomfortable thing about the Avengers' existence inviting the challenges that
follow.

He is also, quietly, the most dangerous person on the team, and he never quite
seems to notice. His one significant mistake is a miss.

:::detail{title="Worthy, and unbothered"}
Moments after being created, he lifts Mjolnir and hands it back to Thor. The
Avengers had spent the film arguing about whether he could be trusted, and this
settles it: the hammer can only be lifted by the worthy, so the room takes the
gesture as a verdict.

The joke is that Vision is doing nothing of the kind. He is passing somebody
their hammer. He is the only character in this material to pass that test
without knowing it was one.
:::
