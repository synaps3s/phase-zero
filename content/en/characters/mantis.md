Mantis was raised by Ego as a tool: she put him to sleep, every night, for
years, and had no other function and no other company. Pom Klementieff plays her
as somebody with no social calibration at all, which is funny until you work out
where it came from.

The friendship with Drax is the good part. Two people who cannot read a room
find each other, and neither has to translate.

:::detail{title="Half-sister"}
She is Peter Quill's paternal half-sister, one of Ego's many children, which the
third film makes explicit. The reference article records five films plus the
Holiday Special.
:::
