Patrick Mulligan is a detective hoping to use Eddie Brock to find the
remains of Cletus Kasady's victims, and Stephen Graham plays him in *Venom:
Let There Be Carnage*, with Sean Delaney as the young officer he once was.
That young officer, in the opening, is the one Frances Barrison attacks with
her scream on the way to Ravencroft; he shoots her in the eye, comes away
with an injured ear, and believes for years that he killed her.

In the present he asks Eddie to speak to Cletus in San Quentin, grows
suspicious of Eddie as the bodies pile up, and arrests him. Cletus and
Frances take him and Anne Weying hostage at their wedding.

:::spoiler{title="What he comes out with"}
He survives Frances's attempt to kill him, and is infected by a piece of
Carnage. *The Last Dance* opens with his apparent murder making Eddie a
wanted man, then reveals that he survived; he is captured by Strickland and
bonded to one of the contained symbiotes, an unnamed green one Graham also
voices, which tells Strickland what Knull wants. The Xenophage devours him
in the last battle.
:::
