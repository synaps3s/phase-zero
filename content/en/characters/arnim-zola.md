Dr. Arnim Zola is a scientist working for Hydra and the Red Skull, and Toby
Jones plays him. Captured and recruited into S.H.I.E.L.D., he masterminds
Hydra's infiltration of the agency's infrastructure from within, and when a
terminal illness comes for him in the 1970s he transfers his consciousness
into a computer system at Camp Lehigh. The reader who sent this list
summed him up as the Hydra scientist who moved his mind into a computer,
which is the part everybody remembers.

In 2014 that computer keeps Steve Rogers and Natasha Romanoff talking long
enough for a Hydra missile barrage to wipe the camp out.

:::detail{title="Three, and what is not counted"}
Wikipedia counts him in three projects: the two Captain America films and
the *Agent Carter* series. It also records alternate versions in the animated
*What If...?*, where a copy of his consciousness ends up in Ultron's body
fighting Killmonger for the Infinity Stones; none of that is counted for a
live-action person.
:::
