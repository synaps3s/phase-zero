William Stryker is the human villain of the Fox series, an American colonel
who leads Team X and then Weapon X and who hates mutants with the steadiness of
a man who thinks he is being reasonable. Three actors play him: Brian Cox in
*X2*, Danny Huston in *X-Men Origins: Wolverine*, and Josh Helman in *Days of
Future Past* and *Apocalypse*, and the films visit his life in no particular
order.

In *X2* he is the main antagonist. He raids the school, takes many of the
children and Xavier himself to his base at Alkali Lake, and plans to use his
own son Jason's power of illusion and a copy of Cerebro to make the professor
kill every mutant on Earth with his mind. The X-Men and the Brotherhood stop
him together. It is Magneto who reveals, in this film, that Stryker was the
one who grafted the adamantium onto Wolverine's bones.

*Origins* shows that history: Stryker running Weapon X, manipulating Logan into
the bonding process, and wiping his memory with a shot to the head. *Days of
Future Past* has a younger Stryker working with Bolivar Trask, and *Apocalypse*
has him attack the mansion and capture Moira MacTaggert, Beast, Quicksilver and
Mystique to interrogate them about Xavier. He is arrested at the end of that
one.

:::spoiler{title="Alkali Lake"}
At the end of *X2* Wolverine leaves him to die in the destruction of Alkali
Lake. The prequels are set before that, so no film has to explain how he
survived it.
:::

:::detail{title="Two timelines"}
*Days of Future Past* rewrites the series' history, and Stryker sits on both
sides of the rewrite. In the original timeline he apprehends Mystique after
she assassinates Trask and uses her DNA in the Sentinels. In the revised one he
captures Wolverine for Weapon X, which is the version *Apocalypse* follows.
:::
