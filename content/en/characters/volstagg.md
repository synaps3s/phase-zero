Volstagg is a member of the Warriors Three, a warrior of Asgard who loves to
eat. Ray Stevenson plays him in all three *Thor* films.

There is an irony worth noting. The first two *Thor* films kept their comedy in
one character and played the rest straight; *Ragnarok* made the whole film
funny and had no use for him at all.
