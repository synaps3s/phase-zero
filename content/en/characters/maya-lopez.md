Maya Lopez is Choctaw and deaf, and the series built around her treats both as
facts rather than as subjects. Wilson Fisk is her adoptive uncle. He took her in
after her father was killed and made her useful, and the thing she learns in
*Hawkeye* is that he arranged the killing.

*Echo* sends her back to Oklahoma, to a grandmother, cousins and a town that did
not follow her to New York. Alaqua Cox plays her almost entirely without speech,
and the series is confident enough to run long stretches with no dialogue and no
score, which is a real risk that mostly pays.

:::detail{title="Where it sits"}
*Echo* is the first Marvel Studios series released under the TV-MA label, and it
is tied more tightly to the Netflix era than to anything around it: Fisk and
Daredevil both come from there. It is also the point at which those series stop
being a separate shelf.
:::
