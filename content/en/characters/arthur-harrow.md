Arthur Harrow is a cult leader and the avatar of Ammit, and Ethan Hawke
plays him in *Moon Knight*. He encourages Marc Spector to embrace his inner
darkness. The reader who sent this list described him as the cult leader who
believed in the Egyptian god Ammit, which is the whole of him: he does not
merely believe in her, he acts for her.

:::detail{title="Doctor Harrow"}
Hawke also plays Doctor Harrow, a psychiatrist version who exists only in
Steven Grant's and Marc Spector's mind, and who helps Grant confront the
truth of his mother's death while denying that Khonshu exists. The reader's
spelling, Arther, is corrected to the one Wikipedia uses.
:::
