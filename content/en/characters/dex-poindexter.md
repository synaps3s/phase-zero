Benjamin Leonard "Dex" Poindexter is a psychopathic orphan and a highly skilled
marksman capable of using almost any object as a lethal projectile. He had
psychiatric therapy after killing his baseball coach as a child and, when his
psychiatrist died, joined the U.S. Army and then the FBI, using the structure
of both to keep himself in one piece. Then Wilson Fisk found him.

What makes him work is that the series shows the scaffolding first. Dex has a
north star, a set of instructions and a voice on a tape telling him how to be a
person, and *Daredevil* spends an episode explaining exactly how that was built
before it shows Fisk taking it apart. The result is the best villain the run
produced after Kilgrave.

:::spoiler{title="The suit, and the spine"}
Fisk puts Dex in a copy of the Daredevil suit to ruin Murdock's reputation.
At the end of that season Fisk breaks his back, and Dex returns in *Born Again*
after surgery that enhanced his body.
:::
