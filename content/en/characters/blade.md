Blade is a half-vampire daywalker who hunts vampires. In 2024 he approaches
Dane Whitman and asks whether he is ready to pick up the Ebony Blade. That
scene, after the credits of *Eternals*, is the only appearance the character
has made in this continuity, and Mahershala Ali is not on screen for it.

He is in this catalogue because a reader would look for him and because the
gap is itself the story. A recorded voice from 2021 is the whole of it.

:::detail{title="The film that keeps not happening"}
Ali was announced for a *Blade* film that went through several writers and
directors and was repeatedly delayed. Wikipedia records that he was set to
return in it and that his contract expired in July 2026.

Variants of Blade appear elsewhere: in *Deadpool & Wolverine*, where Wesley
Snipes reprises the role he played in New Line Cinema's trilogy between 1998
and 2004, and in the animated *What If...?* and *Marvel Zombies*. Ali had
already appeared in this continuity once before, as Cornell Stokes in
*Luke Cage*.
:::
