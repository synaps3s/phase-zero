Domino is the best joke in *Deadpool 2* because the film takes it seriously.
Wade Wilson tells her luck is not a superpower and not cinematic; the rest of
the film is a sustained argument that he is wrong, filmed as a chain of
accidents that always end well for her.

Zazie Beetz plays her entirely unbothered, which is the correct reading. She is
never in danger and she knows it.
