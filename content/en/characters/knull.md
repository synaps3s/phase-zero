Knull is the creator of the symbiotes, and he wants the codex that Eddie and
Venom carry because it would free him. Andy Serkis plays him, having directed
*Venom: Let There Be Carnage* first. Kelly Marcel has said they knew during
that film that Serkis would be this character.

He is the largest thing the Sony films ever pointed at, and the trilogy ends
before it has to deal with him. This catalogue records that as an open thread
rather than a resolved one.

:::detail{title="What is not named on screen"}
The same film is careful about names in general. It never names the green
symbiote that bonds with Patrick Mulligan; the official subtitles for the
trailer call it Toxin, and the symbiote that saved Mulligan's life is left
unidentified. Where a film declines to name something, this catalogue does not
name it for it.
:::
