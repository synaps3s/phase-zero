General Dreykov is a high-ranking officer in the Soviet armed forces who
acts as the Red Room's overseer, and the father and superior of Taskmaster.
Ray Winstone plays him in *Black Widow*. The reader who sent this list
described him as the head of the Red Room who over the years collected so
many women as recruits, which is what the Red Room is.

:::spoiler{title="2016"}
Wikipedia records that he is killed by Yelena Belova in 2016. His daughter,
Antonia, has an entry here of her own.
:::

:::detail{title="What is not counted"}
An alternate version of him, voiced by Piotr Michael, appeared in the
animated *What If...?*, which this catalogue does not count for
live-action people.
:::
