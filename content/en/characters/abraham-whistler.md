Abraham Whistler is the man behind Blade: mentor, father figure and
weaponsmith, according to the cast list of the first film. Kris Kristofferson
plays him in all three, which is a small miracle given what the second film
has to undo for him to be there.

In *Blade* he is the one who explains the rules. He and Blade have been
waging a secret war on vampires with weapons built around what they cannot
stand, sunlight, silver and garlic, and when Blade brings home a bitten
hematologist, Karen Jenson, Whistler is the one who treats her. He is also
the one who mixes the serum that keeps Blade's thirst down, and who knows it
is wearing off.

:::spoiler{title="What happens to him, three times"}
Frost's men attack the hideout, bite Whistler and abduct Karen. Blade comes
back to find him turning, and helps him kill himself. That is the end of the
first film, and *Blade II* opens by taking it back: he was turned rather
than killed, held prisoner by the vampires for two years, and Blade finds him
in Prague and cures him. He spends the rest of that film doing what he does,
building UV weapons with Scud, locating the Reaper nest, and quietly releasing
a cartridge of Reaper pheromones that gets the vampire who threatened him
eaten. When Damaskinos's forces capture the three of them, Whistler is the one
who frees himself and pulls a weakened Blade out.

*Blade: Trinity* kills him for real, and early. FBI agents locate the hideout
and he dies in the raid. Blade surrenders. The daughter he never mentioned,
Abigail, is one of the two people who then break Blade out.
:::
