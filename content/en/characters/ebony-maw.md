Ebony Maw is an adopted son of Thanos and a member of the Black Order. Tom
Vaughan-Lawlor voices and motion captures him, and the performance is the
reason he is the one people remember: he speaks about Thanos the way a convert
speaks, not the way a soldier does.

:::spoiler{title="How he dies"}
Tony Stark and Peter Parker defeat him aboard his own ship and blow him out of
an airlock into space. A past version of him, brought forward from 2014, is
disintegrated with the rest of Thanos's forces when Stark uses the Gauntlet in
*Endgame*.
:::
