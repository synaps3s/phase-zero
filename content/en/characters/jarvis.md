J.A.R.V.I.S. is an artificial intelligence Tony Stark built as his personal
assistant, and Paul Bettany is the voice. The name is an acronym, Just a
Rather Very Intelligent System, and the character is based on two things at
once: Edwin Jarvis, the Stark family's butler in the comics, and H.O.M.E.R.,
another of Stark's AIs there. Wikipedia records that he was made a program
rather than a person to avoid comparisons to Alfred and Batman, and that
Bettany recorded the part as a favour to Jon Favreau with little idea of
what it was.

He runs and takes care of the internal systems of Stark's buildings and the
Iron Man suits, and later controls the armour and the Hulkbuster for him.
He is in four films before *Age of Ultron*, from the workshop in *Iron Man*
to the tower in *The Avengers*.

:::spoiler{title="Age of Ultron"}
Ultron seems to destroy him. He has in fact distributed his consciousness
into the internet, where he delays Ultron's attempts to get at nuclear
launch codes, and Stark and Bruce Banner then use him as the core software
for the android who becomes Vision. F.R.I.D.A.Y. replaces him as Stark's
assistant.
:::
