Elektra Natchios is described by her own series as a mysterious and dangerous
woman from Murdock's past, and the second season of *Daredevil* is largely
about what she does to him. Doug Petrie called her "the best bad girlfriend you
can possibly have", and put it plainly: she is Matt's id, the wild side he
spends every other scene taming.

Élodie Yung's reading of the character is worth quoting because it explains the
performance. She thought Elektra was close to a sociopath, that the world is a
game to her, and that she would use anything to get what she wants. But she
also insisted on the layers: "I think Elektra isn't a bad person. She's not a
good person. She's a person with different traits."
