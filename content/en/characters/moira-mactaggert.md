Moira MacTaggert is a CIA agent, a doctor and an ally to the X-Men, and
she has been played by two people. Olivia Williams is the first, in *X-Men:
The Last Stand*, and Rose Byrne is the second in every appearance since.
The order the films came out in is the reverse of the order she lives them.

In *First Class*, set in 1962, she is an American CIA agent who infiltrates
the Hellfire Club and begins working with a young Charles Xavier to stop
them. She is initiated into Division X, helps Charles and Erik train the
first class, and is on the beach in Cuba for the final battle, where she
fires at Magneto and one of the deflected shots goes into Xavier's spine.
She becomes his caretaker until he wipes her memory, and leaves the CIA to
become a doctor, which is what she is in *The Last Stand*: teaching students
at the Institute remotely from her base on Muir Island, where she cares for
a brain-dead patient the source identifies as Xavier's brother.

:::spoiler{title="After the credits of The Last Stand"}
After Xavier's death his consciousness enters that patient's body and greets
her. It is the scene after the credits, and it is why he is alive in the
films that follow.
:::

*Apocalypse* is the revised timeline, so none of that has to hold. Moira is
still at the CIA, has had a son, and has been married and divorced.
Investigating mutant cults in Cairo she discovers Apocalypse's awakening.
After the mansion is destroyed she is taken by Colonel Stryker and
interrogated until the student X-Men free them, and after the final battle
Xavier restores her memories. She has Stryker arrested for treason at the
end of the film.
