Billy Russo is Frank Castle's former best friend from their time together in
Force Recon, and he runs Anvil, a private military corporation. Ben Barnes
plays him as the man Frank could have become if he had been willing to sell the
thing they both did overseas, and the first season works because it takes its
time before making you choose which one of them is right.

The second season is where the comics catch up. Russo develops scars on his
face, memory loss and, as the series puts it, a mind broken like a jigsaw
puzzle, after Castle bashes his head into a mirror at the end of the first
season. That is where the name comes from.

:::detail{title="The name is never said"}
Wikipedia lists him as Billy Russo / Jigsaw and the second season builds the
scars, the mask and the psychiatrist around exactly that idea. The show itself
is more reluctant with the name than the comics are, which is true of most of
the Netflix run.
:::
