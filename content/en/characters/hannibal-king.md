Hannibal King is one of the two people who rescue Blade after the FBI takes
him in *Blade: Trinity*. With Abigail Whistler he invites Blade into their
band of vampire hunters, the Nightstalkers, and the film gives him most of
its jokes. Ryan Reynolds plays him, twelve years before he put on the red
suit in *Deadpool*.

The Marvel Database lists him as human and a former vampire, which is where
the grudge comes from.

:::spoiler{title="Captured"}
When Abigail and Blade return to the Nightstalkers' hideout they find
everybody dead except King and Zoe, Sommerfield's daughter, who have been
taken. The vampires torture King for information and he refuses to talk, even
when they threaten to turn him, starve him, and then give him Zoe to feed on.
Blade and Abigail free them, and King kills Jarko Grimwood, the vampires'
bodyguard, in the fight that follows.
:::
