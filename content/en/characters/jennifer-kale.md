Jennifer Kale is a witch specialising in potions, a beauty guru, a social
media influencer and the owner of Kale Kare, and Sasheer Zamata plays her in
*Agatha All Along*. The reader who sent this list described her as the
witch with immense experience with potions, which is her craft in a phrase.
Hoping to regain her magic and to be able to face the legal charges against
her, she joins the coven Agatha Harkness assembles with the Teen to face the
trials of the Witches' Road. On the Road, when Billy is injured breaking a
curse, she is the one who manages to heal him.

:::spoiler{title="The end of the Road"}
She unlocks her magic and survives, which not everybody in the coven does.
:::
