Sersi is an Eternal with an affinity for humankind, empathetic and able to
manipulate matter. She has loved Ikaris for centuries and poses on Earth as a
museum curator, and she becomes the Eternals' leader after Ajak dies.

She is the film's argument in one person. The Eternals were sent here to
protect humanity from Deviants and were forbidden to interfere in anything
else, and Sersi is the one who has spent seven thousand years actually living
among the people she is not allowed to help.

:::detail{title="Gemma Chan, twice"}
This is Chan's second role in these films. She played Minn-Erva in
*Captain Marvel* in 2019, a different character in a different species, which
the films have never addressed.
:::
