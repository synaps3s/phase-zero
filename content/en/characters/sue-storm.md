Sue Storm is the most powerful member of the team and, unusually, the film
says so. Her force fields are the only thing on the board that can hold, and her
diplomacy is presented as the reason her Earth has largely disarmed, which puts
the negotiation at the centre of the story rather than the fight.

Vanessa Kirby plays her as someone who has been the adult in every room for
years and is tired of it in a way she will not discuss.

:::detail{title="A separate Earth"}
These four live on Earth-828, not on the Earth the rest of the Marvel Studios
run takes place on. That was decided before the film was written, so that a team
this capable would not need an excuse for having been absent from everything
else.
:::
