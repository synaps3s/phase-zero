Cain Marko is a mutant with superhuman strength and durability who wears a
distinctive helmet. In *The Last Stand* Magneto frees him from a prison
transport along with Mystique and Multiple Man, and he is sent to find and kill
Leech during the battle of Alcatraz. Kitty Pryde slows him down and beats him,
which is one of that film's few well made ideas.

*Deadpool 2* rebuilds him at roughly twice the size and gives him the best
sequence in the film. In *Deadpool & Wolverine* he stands with Cassandra Nova
in the Void, and his helmet, which blocks telepathy, is what finally stops her.

:::detail{title="Three performers, one of them the star"}
Vinnie Jones played him in *The Last Stand* and turned the role down later
because of what the costume demanded. In *Deadpool 2* he is performed by David
Leitch and voiced by Ryan Reynolds, who is also playing Deadpool in the same
scenes. Aaron W. Reed plays him in *Deadpool & Wolverine*, where X-23
decapitates him.
:::
