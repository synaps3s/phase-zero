Magneto's first scene is a boy being pulled away from his parents at the gates
of a camp, and the films never let anyone forget it. Everything he does
afterwards proceeds logically from having watched what a society does to a
minority when it decides to, and the series is honest enough not to give him a
bad argument.

He and Charles Xavier agree on the facts and disagree about what follows, which
is why they keep working together and keep ending up on opposite sides of a
field.

:::detail{title="Two actors, and a real choice"}
Ian McKellen plays him in the original trilogy and beyond; Michael Fassbender
plays him across the four prequels. McKellen has the greater number of released
films by one, which is why this catalogue lists him, and Fassbender carries the
run where the character's history is actually told.

There is no correct answer here, only a recorded one.
:::
