The Ancient One is a teacher who is not what she claims. She trains the Masters
of the Mystic Arts and forbids drawing power from the Dark Dimension, and she
has been drawing power from the Dark Dimension for centuries, which is how she
is still alive.

Tilda Swinton plays the hypocrisy without defending it. Her best scene is her
last: dying, slowed to a stop between heartbeats, telling Strange that his
arrogance and his fear are the same thing and that she never learnt to stop
either.

:::detail{title="The casting"}
Casting a Celtic actor as a character written as Tibetan was criticised at the
time, and the studio's own explanations have shifted since. It is worth knowing
about a film that is otherwise about who is allowed to hold knowledge.
:::
