Eric Brooks is a half-vampire daywalker, a dhampir, who hunts vampires. He is
highly skilled in martial arts and always carries weapons made for killing
them. Wesley Snipes plays him across three films.

*Blade* came out in 1998, made money, and is the film that convinced a studio
that Marvel characters could carry a picture. Everything else in this catalogue
follows from that, and almost nothing in this catalogue acknowledges it.

:::detail{title="Which Blade this is"}
This catalogue carries two. This one is the New Line trilogy, in its own
continuity. The other is the one Mahershala Ali voices from off screen in
*Eternals*. When David Goyer first pitched the film, the head of New Line
suggested Denzel Washington, Wesley Snipes or Laurence Fishburne. Goyer wanted
Snipes from the start.
:::
