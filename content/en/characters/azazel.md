Azazel is a mutant with red skin, a prehensile tail, a demonic appearance
and the ability to teleport. Jason Flemyng plays him in *X-Men: First
Class*, where he is the Hellfire Club's teleporter: he brings Colonel Hendry
to Sebastian Shaw, and kills the agents of Division X by taking them up and
letting go. He is at the Cuban Missile Crisis, and when it is over he
teleports the newly formed Brotherhood of Mutants away from the beach.

In *Deadpool & Wolverine* a variant of him, played by Eduardo Gago Muñoz, is
a member of Cassandra Nova's gang in the Void.

:::detail{title="What the films never say"}
Wikipedia records that he is killed by Trask's experimentation after the
events of *First Class*, which is why *Days of Future Past* shows him only
in a photograph, and that film is therefore not in his list. It also records
that the writer Simon Kinberg revealed him to be Mystique's father, which
the films never do.
:::
