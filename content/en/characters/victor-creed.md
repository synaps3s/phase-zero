Sabretooth is Logan with the brakes removed, which is the only thing he needs to
be. Tyler Mane plays him in the first film as a physical problem: bigger,
faster, and clearly enjoying it. Liev Schreiber plays a different reading
entirely in *Origins*, where he is written as Logan's brother and given most of
the film's actual grief.

Two performances, two ideas, and no attempt to reconcile them, which is a fair
description of the Fox run as a whole.
