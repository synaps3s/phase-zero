Parker Robbins, called the Hood, is a Chicago criminal and gang leader who
wears a cloak that lets him reach into dark arts and magic. Anthony Ramos plays
him.

He matters more than a first appearance usually does, because *Ironheart* is the
only place in the recent catalogue where street crime and actual magic meet,
and because the cloak's terms are the point of his story rather than a detail
of it.

:::spoiler{title="Who he owes"}
The debt behind the cloak is named at the end of the series, and it is the
first time that name has been spoken in this continuity.
:::
