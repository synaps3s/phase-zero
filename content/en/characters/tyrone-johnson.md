Tyrone Johnson is a teenager who can engulf others in darkness and move through
it, connected to Tandy Bowen by a shared childhood tragedy. Aubrey Joseph plays
him.

His half of the series is about a Black teenager in New Orleans trying to get a
police detective held responsible for his brother's death, and the show is not
coy about it. James Connors, the scar-faced vice detective, is the antagonist
for two seasons and is written as ordinary rather than monstrous, which is the
point.
