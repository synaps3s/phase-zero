Aleksei Sytsevich is a member of the Russian mafia, dim and boastful, whom
Spider-Man catches in the opening of *The Amazing Spider-Man 2* trying to
steal plutonium from Oscorp. Paul Giamatti plays him at full volume. He goes to
prison, is freed by Harry Osborn, and comes back at the very end in a
rhinoceros-themed mechanical suit with heavy machine guns on the forearms and
missile launchers on the shoulders, provided by Gustav Fiers, the Gentleman.

The film's visual effects supervisor, Jerome Chen, told Entertainment Weekly
that the suit was built from modified Soviet-era military gear of the 1980s,
which is the kind of detail that explains why it looks like a tank that has
been to the gym.

:::detail{title="The film that never came"}
Giamatti confirmed that the Rhino was meant to return in a third *Amazing
Spider-Man* film, before that series was cancelled. His universe is the one
this catalogue files as Earth-120703.

Two other Rhinos exist on screen and are not him: a pair of displaced versions
held prisoner by the Spider-Society in *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse*,
without lines, and a
different Aleksei Sytsevich played by Alessandro Nivola in Sony's *Kraven the
Hunter*, a mercenary who turns into a rhino-human hybrid. That one belongs to
Sony's separate universe, and would need an entry of his own.
:::
