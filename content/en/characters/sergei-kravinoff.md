Kraven the Hunter is the last film Sony made in this line and the most
conventional. Sergei Kravinoff survives a lion attack as a boy, is given a
mixture that saves him and changes him, and grows up to hunt criminals, starting
with his own family's.

Aaron Taylor-Johnson plays it seriously. The film is a revenge thriller with an
origin attached, and it is the closing entry in a shelf that never found the
shape it wanted.
