Zeus is the king of the Olympians, drawn from the Greek god of the same name
and played by Russell Crowe as a man who has enjoyed being worshipped for a
very long time. He heads the Council of Godheads in Omnipotence City, which is
where Thor goes for help against Gorr the God Butcher, and where he gets none.

Zeus will not raise an army. The gods Gorr has been killing were, in his
telling, lesser gods, and he is more interested in the orgy he has planned.
When Thor pushes, Zeus turns his thunderbolt on the group and breaks Korg's
body with it. Thor throws it back through Zeus's chest, and Valkyrie takes the
bolt to use against Gorr.

:::spoiler{title="The mid-credits scene"}
He survived. The mid-credits scene has him sending his son Hercules to deal
with Thor, and to make men fear the gods again.
:::

:::detail{title="A kinder Zeus was cut"}
Deleted scenes had him more helpful than the finished film: turning up at the
hospice where Jane Foster is being treated, explaining to Thor how the
thunderbolt works, and telling him to find the Gates of Eternity. A different
Zeus, voiced by Darin De Paul, appears in the animated *What If...?*, which
this catalogue does not count for live-action characters.
:::
