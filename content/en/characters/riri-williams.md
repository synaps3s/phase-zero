Riri Williams is introduced by consequence rather than by origin. She built a
vibranium detector as coursework, the CIA used it, and Wakanda and Talokan both
concluded she was a threat. She is a teenager in a dorm room being hunted by two
countries.

The series that follows moves her home to Chicago and makes the money the
problem: genius is not enough to build the thing she can already design, so she
takes the funding that is available. Dominique Thorne plays her as a person who
is used to being the smartest one present and has not yet learned what that
costs.
