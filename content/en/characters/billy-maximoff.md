William Maximoff is the son of Wanda Maximoff and Vision and the younger twin
of Tommy. Wanda created him with chaos magic inside the Westview anomaly, a
reality shaped like the sitcoms she watched as a child, in a town whose
residents she was controlling.

What makes him interesting is what happens when the anomaly ends. He was made,
and then he was unmade with the rest of it, and *Agatha All Along* is about
what came back and where it landed. The name he uses on the road, and in the
comics, is Wiccan.

:::detail{title="Two actors, two projects, one variant"}
Julian Hilliard plays him at ten and Baylen Bielitz at five. Wikipedia records
two projects for him as of 2026, plus a variant in
*Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*, which is a different boy in a
different universe and one of the reasons that film hurts.
:::
