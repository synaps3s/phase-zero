Seth Voelker, also known as Sidewinder, is the leader of Serpent, a violent
special ops team, and Giancarlo Esposito plays him in *Captain America:
Brave New World*. That is the whole of what Wikipedia's list says about him,
and this entry does not add to it. The reader who sent this list described
him as the leader of Serpent who later gained a grudge against Sam Wilson,
and the grudge is recorded here as the reader's reading of the film rather
than as a fact from the page.
