Miss Minutes is the creation of He Who Remains, the animated mascot of the
Time Variance Authority, who takes the form of a female anthropomorphic
clock with a Southern accent. Tara Strong voices her. Wikipedia files her
under the MCU's artificial intelligences rather than its people, and this
catalogue gives her an entry anyway, on the same terms as J.A.R.V.I.S. and
F.R.I.D.A.Y.

Her design was inspired by Felix the Cat and other early twentieth-century
cartoon characters; the first season's director Kate Herron called her a
Roger Rabbit kind of character. Her introductory video in the first episode
was modelled on Mr. DNA from *Jurassic Park* and on public service
announcements from various eras. One discarded design resembled Cogsworth
from *Beauty and the Beast*.

:::spoiler{title="Who she works for"}
In the first-season finale she reveals that she works for He Who Remains at
the Citadel at the End of Time; the head writer Michael Waldron called her
turn to evil really scary. In the second season she and Ravonna Renslayer
follow a roadmap he left to create a new version of him, find Victor Timely
as a child and again in 1893, where she attempts to seduce him, and are
sent back to the Citadel, where she tells Renslayer her history. When they
kidnap Timely from the TVA she is deactivated.
:::

:::detail{title="Into the comics"}
She made her comics debut on a variant cover in December 2021 and on the
page in the limited series *TVA* in December 2024, after the MCU's TVA had
met several Marvel Comics realities in the wake of *Deadpool & Wolverine*.
:::
