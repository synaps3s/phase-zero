Layla El-Faouly is Marc Spector's wife, an archaeologist and adventurer, and
May Calamawy plays her in *Moon Knight*. She becomes the avatar of the
goddess Tawaret, which makes her the Scarlet Scarab.
The reader who sent this list called her the love of Marc Spector who later
became the Scarlet Scarab, and Wikipedia adds one thing: the character did
not exist in the comics before the series, and made her comics debut in
2023.
