X-24 is an aggressive clone of Wolverine developed by Alkali-Transigen, and
Hugh Jackman plays him opposite himself in *Logan*. He exists to do what
Donald Pierce's men cannot, and the film uses him for exactly that.

:::spoiler{title="What he does, and how it ends"}
He kills Charles Xavier. At the end he fatally wounds Logan too. X-23 kills
him with an adamantium bullet.
:::
