Odin Borson, also called the Allfather, is the ruler of Asgard, the son of Bor,
the biological father of Hela and Thor, the adoptive father of Loki and the
husband of Frigga. He is based on the Odin of Norse mythology, and Anthony
Hopkins plays him for three films across seven years, in three quite different
registers.

The first film has him as a father with a hard lesson to teach. The third
reveals that the golden peace he ruled over was paid for by a conquest he
buried, along with the daughter who carried it out. Very little in these films
is as neatly retroactive as that, and it works because Hopkins plays the
regret rather than the twist.

:::detail{title="The years he was not himself"}
In 2013 Loki removes him from power and puts him under a spell that makes him
forget his own life, leaving him in a retirement home in New York City while
Loki wears his face on the throne. He breaks free of it before *Ragnarok*, and
what he has left by then is one conversation.
:::
