Elena Rodriguez is a Colombian Inhuman who can move at super speed for the
length of a single heartbeat before returning to the point she started from.
She lost her father to drug dealers young and lived with her uncle Oscar and
her cousin Francisco. Natalia Cordova-Buckley plays her.

The limit is what makes her good. Every other speedster in this catalogue can
simply go; she gets one heartbeat and has to have decided in advance what to do
with it.
