Thaddeus Ross is a lieutenant general in the United States Armed Forces who
later serves as Secretary of State and is then elected President. He is Betty
Ross's father, and their relationship is the one consistent wound in the
character. He leads the manhunt for Bruce Banner and, in doing so, helps create
the Abomination.

He is played by William Hurt from 2008 to 2021 and by Harrison Ford from 2025,
after Hurt's death in 2022. Both readings are of a man who believes he is the
adult in the room and is never once right about it.

:::spoiler{title="Brave New World"}
Samuel Sterns, whom Ross had held for years, feeds him gamma radiated pills.
Ross becomes the Red Hulk and destroys the White House. He stands down as
president and is imprisoned in the Raft.
:::
