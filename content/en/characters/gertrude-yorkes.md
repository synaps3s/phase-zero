Gertrude Yorkes is a riot grrrl and a social activist, and she has a telepathic
link to a genetically engineered dinosaur. Ariela Barer plays her, and the link
is not a command: the animal responds to what Gert feels, including the things
she is trying not to show.

She carries the series' politics, its jokes and most of its anxiety, and the
show gives her an anxiety disorder rather than treating her nerve as bottomless.
