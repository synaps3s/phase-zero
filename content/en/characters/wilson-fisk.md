Fisk is the best villain in this material and the reason is structural: the
show introduces him a full quarter of the way in, and by then it has spent
hours on his money, his associates and his taste, so that the man himself
arrives as the last piece rather than the first.

He is also written as somebody who cannot hold a feeling without it becoming
violence, and the series is very careful about when it lets that show. The
scariest thing he does in his first season is have dinner.
