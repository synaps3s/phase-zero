Yuriko Oyama has extendable adamantium claws and accelerated healing, and in
*X2* she is under William Stryker's control the entire time. She takes Xavier
and Cyclops to Alkali Lake, and the fight she has with Logan at the end is
built on a simple, cruel idea: he cannot out-heal her, he cannot out-cut her,
and the only thing he has that she does not is a choice.

Kelly Hu says almost nothing in the part. It works because of that.

:::spoiler{title="How the fight ends"}
Logan fills her with molten adamantium. She drowns in the substance that made
her, which is as close to a comment as that film makes.
:::
