Frank Castle is the argument these series keep having with themselves. In
*Daredevil* he exists so that Matt Murdock has to defend a line he cannot fully
justify. Then he gets his own series, and it turns out to be a conspiracy
thriller about the war he fought rather than a revenge story about the family he
lost.

Jon Bernthal plays him without any coolness at all, which is the only honest way
to play him. The best scenes are the ones with other veterans, where the show
stops being about violence and starts being about what happens to people who
come back.
