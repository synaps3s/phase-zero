Bastian Cooper, known as Muse, is a masked serial killer in New York City who
makes art with the blood of his victims. He attended a book signing for Heather
Glenn's book *Live Without Fear: A Guide to Confronting Trauma* at the Inkwell
Gathering Space and told her he was a fan of her work. He is confronted by
Daredevil after capturing Angela del Toro and starting to drain her of blood.

He is the first season's answer to a structural problem. *Born Again* is mostly
a story about a city government, and Muse is what that city government is
failing to notice while it argues. He belongs to the oldest tradition in this
character's stories: the villain who is not a threat to the world, only to the
neighbourhood.

:::spoiler{title="There are two people under that mask"}
Heather Glenn, Murdock's partner through the first season, is stalked and
almost killed by Muse. She takes on the mantle herself heading into the third
season. The catalogue keeps Bastian Cooper and Heather Glenn as separate people
because they are, whatever they have in common.
:::
