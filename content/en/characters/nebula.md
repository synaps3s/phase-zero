Nebula is introduced as a henchman and is the character who changes more than
anyone else in this material. Thanos raised her and Gamora as competitors,
made them fight, and every time Nebula lost he replaced another part of her
with machinery. The films let that sit as background for a whole movie before
anybody says it.

What she does with it is the interesting part. She does not become warm. She
becomes useful, and then reliable, and then, eventually, somebody who can hear
her sister say something kind without leaving the room.

She also does one of the strangest things anyone does in these films, which is
kill a version of herself. The 2014 Nebula who travels forward with Thanos is
not a metaphor. She is the person Nebula used to be, and she is shot by the
person Nebula became.

:::detail{title="One person, not two"}
It is worth being precise, because the other Guardians have the opposite
problem. Gamora's later appearances are a different individual from another
timeline. Nebula's are not.

The reference article gives her a single unbroken first and last appearance,
from *Guardians of the Galaxy* to *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3*, and records
the 2014 variant as a separate person who dies in *Avengers: Endgame*. This
catalogue follows that.
:::
