Owen Wilson playing a middle manager at the end of time is the joke, and it
works because he plays it entirely straight. Mobius investigates dangerous
variants, believes the paperwork, and is kind to a god of mischief for long
enough that the god notices.

What the second season does with him is quietly cruel: he has been at the TVA
for at least four centuries, he has been mind-wiped repeatedly, and he had a
life before it. He was Don, who sold jet skis in Cleveland and had two sons.
Being told that does not undo four hundred years, and the series knows it.
