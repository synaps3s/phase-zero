Dr. Cecilia Reyes is the sole medical professional at Milbury Hospital in
*The New Mutants*, and a mutant herself, with the ability to generate
force fields. Alice Braga plays her. Wikipedia's list says she operates
under the Essex Corporation to train young mutants as personal killers: she
recruits five of them to the hospital and traps them there using her
powers, to observe them. The reader who sent this list called her the head
scientist who will not let any of the New Mutants leave, which is the
experience of watching the film.

:::spoiler{title="The demon bear"}
She is instructed to kill Dani by the Essex Corporation, is wounded by
Rahne, confronts the young mutants, and is killed by the demon bear.
:::

:::detail{title="Before the film, and before the casting"}
The cast table on Wikipedia's list has her mentioned, without appearing,
in *X2*. Rosario Dawson was initially set to play her and left the
production.
:::
