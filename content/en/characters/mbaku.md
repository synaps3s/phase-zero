M'Baku leads the Jabari, the tribe that left Wakanda's technological society
and lives in the mountains, keeping to their own religion and their own
grievances. He is introduced as a threat and is one, briefly, and then the film
does something better with him than beating him.

Winston Duke plays him with a sense of humour that most of the antagonists in
this catalogue are not allowed. The barking, the threat to feed a man to his
children, the pause: the joke is that the Jabari are vegetarians, and nobody
explains it.
