Makkari can move at superhuman speed, and the film implies she has feelings for
Druig. Lauren Ridloff plays her, and Makkari is the first deaf superhero in
these films.

The best decision in *Eternals* is how it shoots her. Speed is usually shown by
sound, and this film shows it by taking sound away, which puts the audience
inside her hearing instead of watching it from outside.
