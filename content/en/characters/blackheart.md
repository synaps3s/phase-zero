Blackheart is a demon, the son of Mephistopheles, and the reason the Devil
needs a new Ghost Rider. Wes Bentley plays him in *Ghost Rider*. He comes
to Earth looking for the contract of San Venganza, a binding note of a
thousand evil souls that his father's previous Rider refused to hand over,
and he means to use it to unleash Hell on Earth and overthrow the father
who is hunting him for it. Three fallen angels bonded to air, earth and
water, the Hidden, do his work for him until Johnny burns through them one
at a time.

:::spoiler{title="Legion"}
He kills Johnny's friend Mack and takes Roxanne hostage, and Johnny brings
him the contract. Blackheart absorbs the thousand souls, calls himself
Legion, and has Johnny beaten at sunrise, when the Rider goes out. Roxanne
separates them with a shotgun. Johnny shoots him, then turns the Penance
Stare on him, which burns every corrupt soul inside him at once and leaves
him catatonic. Mephistopheles disappears with the body.
:::
