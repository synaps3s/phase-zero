Storm is the character the Fox films most consistently under-served. Halle Berry
plays her for four films and is given the jet, the weather and very little of
her own. The Apocalypse version does better in one respect: it starts her as a
thief in Cairo, so the offer she accepts is an offer of power from someone who
sees her, which is at least a decision.

Her one great scene is in *The Last Stand*, arguing with Charles about whether
being cured is something anyone should want. She is the only person in that film
who treats the question as personal.
