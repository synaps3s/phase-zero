F.R.I.D.A.Y. is an artificial intelligence Tony Stark created to replace
J.A.R.V.I.S. as his personal assistant, after J.A.R.V.I.S. became the core of
Vision. Kerry Condon is the voice, in five films from *Age of Ultron* to
*Endgame*. Wikipedia's editors note on the page itself that her acronym from
the comics is not confirmed to be the same in the films, so this entry does
not expand it.

:::detail{title="Announced, and not counted"}
The same page says she will return in *VisionQuest*, portrayed by Orla
Brady, and that an alternate version appeared in the animated *Marvel
Zombies*. The first is unreleased and the second is animated, so neither is
in her list.
:::
