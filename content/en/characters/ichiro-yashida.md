Ichiro Yashida is the grandfather of Mariko, the father of Shingen, and the
founder of the Yashida Corporation. Logan saved him during the bombing of
Nagasaki in the Second World War, and decades later he sends for Logan to say
he is dying. What he actually wants is the healing.

*The Wolverine* is the only film in this series that is really about the cost
of not being able to die, and Yashida is the argument on the other side: a man
who has had a long life and does not accept that it is ending. The film is at
its best before the armour shows up.

:::detail{title="The name the film gives to somebody else"}
In the comics the Silver Samurai is Kenuichio Harada. *The Wolverine* has a
character called Harada, played by Will Yun Lee, who is a different man
entirely, and puts the armour on Yashida instead. Wikipedia's cast table for
the series carries both names in the same row, which is where the confusion
usually starts.
:::
