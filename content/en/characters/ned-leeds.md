Ned Leeds is Peter Parker's best friend, and the first person other than Tony
Stark and Happy Hogan to find out that Peter is Spider-Man. Jacob Batalon
plays him across six films. He is Filipino-American, and the films never make a
point of it, which is its own kind of point.

He is the person the secret can be told to. Every version of Spider-Man has
needed one, and these films give the job to a friend rather than a love
interest, which changes the shape of the story: Peter has somebody to talk to,
and the films are lighter for it.

The Blip takes him in 2018 and returns him in 2023.

:::spoiler{title="The spell, and what came after"}
When Mysterio exposes Peter's identity, Ned and MJ lose their university
places along with him. In the fight over the villains from other universes, Ned
accidentally summons two other Spider-Men, the ones the catalogue files as
Peter Parker of the Raimi films and Peter Parker of the Amazing films, and they
help cure the visitors. Then Stephen Strange casts the spell that makes the
world forget Peter, and Ned's friendship with him goes with it.

*Brand New Day* picks him up four years later. He and MJ have graduated from
MIT and share an apartment in New York. At the housewarming, Peter turns up
with flowers, introduces himself as a neighbour called Maynard, and watches Ned
demonstrate an invention he calls the Spidey Tracker, built to find out who
Spider-Man is so he can thank him. The film ends with Peter recovering from his
injuries and reintroducing himself to Ned with their old secret handshake. What
that means for Ned's memory the film does not say, and neither does this
catalogue.
:::
