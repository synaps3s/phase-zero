Taneleer Tivan is the keeper of the largest collection of interstellar fauna,
relics and species in the galaxy, working out of the mining colony at Knowhere.
He is the Grandmaster's brother.

He exists in these films for one structural reason and does it very well: he is
the man who explains the Infinity Stones. In 2013, Sif and Volstagg bring him
the Reality Stone for safekeeping, on the argument that keeping two Stones in
one vault is foolish, and *Guardians of the Galaxy* has him narrate the whole
history of the Stones to a group of people who have just stolen one.

:::spoiler{title="Knowhere, and after"}
His assistant destroys the Power Stone's containment in *Guardians*, and his
collection with it. In *Infinity War* Thanos arrives for the Reality Stone, and
what the Guardians walk into on Knowhere is not what it appears to be.
:::
