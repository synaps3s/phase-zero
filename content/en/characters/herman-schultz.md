Herman Schultz is a former salvage worker and professional criminal. When
Jackson Brice is killed he takes the Shocker gauntlet and the name, tracks down
a weapon Spider-Man had recovered, and helps run the deal on the Staten Island
ferry. When the FBI and Spider-Man ambush it, he and Toomes get away.

Bokeem Woodbine plays him as somebody who took a promotion he did not want in
circumstances that made refusing unwise, which is the whole texture of Toomes's
crew and the reason *Homecoming* works better than its villains should allow.
