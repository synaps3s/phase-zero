Luke Cage is the least tormented protagonist in this part of the catalogue. He
does not want a mission and he does not brood; he wants work and a street that
is left alone. His series knows this and spends its time on the barbershop, the
club and the politics of the block rather than on the powers.

Mike Colter plays him quiet, which makes the size of him do the work. The show
was also, at the time, a rare thing on this shelf: a Marvel series about a
neighbourhood rather than about a city skyline.
