Emil Blonsky is a soldier who could not accept being outmatched. He takes an
unfinished version of the serum to keep up with the Hulk, it works, he asks for
more, and the second dose plus gamma radiation makes him something that cannot
be undone.

What makes him worth an entry is the third act, fifteen years later. *She-Hulk*
picks him up in prison, has him apply for parole, and treats his rehabilitation
as genuine rather than as a joke about a monster doing yoga. It is one of the
few times this catalogue lets a villain simply grow up.
