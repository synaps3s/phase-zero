Ajak is the wise and spiritual leader of the Eternals, with healing abilities,
and she functions as the bridge between the Eternals and the Celestial Arishem.
Because of that link she knows the Emergence is coming, and out of a growing
love for humanity she decides to try to stop it.

Salma Hayek plays her for a short time and the whole film runs on the decision
she makes off screen, before it starts.

:::spoiler{title="How the film opens the door"}
Ikaris sees her decision as betrayal and leads her to the Deviants, who kill
her. Sersi finds the body, inherits the link to Arishem, and the film becomes a
question rather than a mission.
:::
