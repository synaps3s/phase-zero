Stick is a mysterious martial artist and Matt Murdock's mentor, blind
himself, and in his late nineties. Scott Glenn plays him. He approaches Matt
as a boy after the accident that blinded him and trains him to hone his
other senses until he can "see" with them, and teaches him to fight, because
Stick wants a soldier for his secret war. He leaves when he realises the boy
wants a father figure instead. The reader who sent this list summarised him
as the man who trained Matt to master his skills despite his blindness,
which is the version Matt would give too.

He is the leader of the Chaste, who wage a war against the Hand and its
quest to retrieve the Black Sky, and that war is what brings him back in the
second season and in *The Defenders*.

:::detail{title="Casting"}
Marvel announced Glenn in September 2014. The showrunner Steven DeKnight
had initially wanted Sonny Chiba, and Glenn, whom the writers had raised
early on, was reconsidered after his performance in *The Leftovers*. He had
never played a blind character before, and had to learn, in his words,
completely about the world of comic books.
:::
