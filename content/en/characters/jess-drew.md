Jess Drew is a pregnant Spider-Woman from Earth-332 who is Miguel's right hand
and rides a motorcycle. Issa Rae voices her. Unlike most Spider-People, Jess
does not maintain a secret identity.

That detail is not decoration. She is the one member of the Spider-Society who
has already refused one of the rules the others treat as inevitable, which makes
her position on the rest of them harder to hold than she admits.
