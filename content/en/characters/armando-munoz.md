Armando Munoz has the power of rapid evolution to any danger or environment,
and is a former member of the X-Men. Edi Gathegi plays him.

The power is written as unbeatable and then the film beats it. Shaw absorbs
Havok's energy blast and puts it inside Darwin, which is the one thing he
cannot adapt around.

:::detail{title="The oldest complaint about this film"}
Darwin is the only Black member of the first team and he is killed in his first
scene, in the same sequence in which the film's other Black character defects
to the villains. It is the most frequently made criticism of *First Class* and
it is a fair one.
:::
