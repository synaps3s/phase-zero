Taskmaster is a good idea executed as an argument. She fights by watching, and
what she has watched is these films: Captain America's shield work, Black
Panther's stance, Hawkeye's draw. Fighting her means fighting the series.

Underneath is Antonia Dreykov, the daughter of the man who ran the Red Room,
kept alive and controlled by him after an explosion Natasha Romanoff set. The
film's whole moral position rests on her: Natasha did not know the child was in
the building, and the film refuses to let that be enough.

:::spoiler{title="Thunderbolts*"}
She reappears as one of Valentina's contractors, sent to kill John Walker, and
is shot dead by Ava Starr in the opening sequence. It is a very abrupt end for
a character the previous film had spent its running time on.
:::
