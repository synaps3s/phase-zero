Brock Rumlow commands S.H.I.E.L.D.'s counter-terrorism S.T.R.I.K.E. team and is
a Hydra double agent. When Rogers refuses to hand information to Secretary
Alexander Pierce, Rumlow and his unit are sent after him, and the lift fight
that follows is the best action sequence in the film and the moment the series
learned what to do with Captain America.

After he is exposed he fights Sharon Carter and Sam Wilson, and is left in the
wreckage of the Triskelion.

:::spoiler{title="Lagos"}
He returns in *Civil War* burned and armoured as Crossbones, steals a biological
weapon in Lagos, and detonates a vest when Rogers has him. Wanda throws the
blast upward into a building. The people killed there are the reason the
Sokovia Accords exist, and the reason the Avengers break.
:::
