Ruth Bat-Seraph is an Israeli former Black Widow assassin, trained in the
Red Room, and Shira Haas plays her in *Captain America: Brave New World*.
After the fall of the Red Room she became a high-ranking United States
government official, the security advisor to President Thaddeus Ross. The
reader who sent this list described her as the ex-Black Widow agent who
later became the chief of security for President Ross, which is close
enough to be the same job.

She attends an international summit and fights Isaiah Bradley and the
others who attempt to assassinate Ross during it. After working out that
Bradley was brainwashed, she assists Sam Wilson and Joaquin Torres in
stopping the person behind it.

:::spoiler{title="Who that was"}
Samuel Sterns, trying to destroy Ross's legacy for having enslaved him and
used him for labour on a false promise of freedom. After watching Ross turn
into a Red Hulk, she befriends an exonerated Bradley, who buys them tickets
to a basketball game.
:::
