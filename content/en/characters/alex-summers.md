Alex Summers can generate plasma discs from his body, and he is Cyclops's older
brother and a former member of the X-Men. Lucas Till plays him across three
films.

The relationship is the odd part of the series. Alex is introduced first,
decades before Scott, so in this continuity the famous Summers brother is the
one who arrives second and does not get told much.

:::spoiler{title="Apocalypse"}
When Apocalypse takes Xavier, Havok attacks and blows up a generator and the
school with it. He is too close to the blast for Quicksilver to reach him, and
he dies.
:::
