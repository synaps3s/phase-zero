Patricia Walker, known to everyone as Trish, is a former model and child star
who was famous as "Patsy" and now hosts a radio show. She is Jessica Jones's
adoptive sister and best friend, and for one season she is the warmest thing in
a very cold series.

The show's real subject is what happens to that warmth. Trish becomes envious
of a sister who has powers and, in her view, wastes them. After Will Simpson's
death she starts using his combat enhancer inhaler so she does not have to feel
powerless. When the inhaler stops working she goes to Karl Malus.

:::spoiler{title="Where it ends"}
The third season turns Trish into the antagonist, and Jessica is the one who
has to stop her. It is the most complete arc in the Netflix run, and it is the
argument the whole series was making from the first episode: wanting to be a
hero and being one are not the same appetite.
:::
