Karnak is Black Bolt's cousin and closest adviser, able to see the fault in all
things, which lets him avoid errors and find the weak point in anything. Ken
Leung plays him.

The best idea in *Inhumans* is what it does to him. He is defined entirely by
being right, and the series breaks the power early and leaves him to work out
who he is without it.
