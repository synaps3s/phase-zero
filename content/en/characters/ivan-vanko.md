Ivan Vanko is the best idea *Iron Man 2* has. His father Anton designed the arc
reactor alongside Howard Stark and was deported and forgotten, and Ivan builds
the same technology in a flat with no money and takes it to Monaco.

The film then loses interest in him and hands the second half to Justin
Hammer's drones, which is a shame, because the argument Vanko is making is the
one the Iron Man films never quite answer: the technology belonged to two
people, and only one family got rich.
