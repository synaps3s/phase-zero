Alex Wilder is intelligent, wants his childhood friends back together, and ends
up leading the Runaways. His parents are Catherine, a successful lawyer, and
Geoffrey, a self-made businessman who had a hard road to it, and both of them
are members of Pride.

He is the one who has thought about it. That is his function in the group and
also his weakness, because the person who plans everything is the person with
the most room to be planning something else.
