Tandy Bowen is a teenager and a street thief connected to Tyrone Johnson by a
shared childhood tragedy, with the ability to emit light. Olivia Holt plays
her.

The series pairs the powers with what each of them needs. Tandy sees people's
hopes, and she is the one who has given up on her own. Her mother Melissa is
addicted and optimistic in roughly equal parts, which is the most precisely
observed relationship in the show.
