Rocket is a raccoon who was experimented on until he could speak, and he is
furious about it in a way the first two films never explain and the third one
does. The comedy in the character is real and it is also load-bearing: he is
funniest when he most needs somebody to stop asking questions.

He is the Guardians' engineer and their best pilot, and the thing he actually
provides is competence. When a plan works in these films, it is usually because
Rocket built the part that had to work.

The performance is worth knowing about because it is three people. Bradley
Cooper voices him, Sean Gunn provided the on-set movement, and a raccoon called
Oreo was the reference for how the body moves.

:::detail{title="Do not call him that"}
Every film in the run has somebody call him a raccoon, a rodent, a trash panda
or a fox, and every time it lands badly. It reads as a running joke for two
films.

*Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3* goes back and shows what the word is actually
attached to, and the joke stops being a joke retroactively, across three films
that were already finished.
:::
