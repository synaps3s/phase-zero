Doctor Olivia Octavius is the head scientist and chief executive of Alchemax
and a scientific adviser to Kingpin, using highly advanced tentacles. Kathryn
Hahn plays her.

The reveal is played as a joke and works as characterisation: she is
introduced as a warm, helpful scientist and simply carries on being warm and
helpful while trying to kill everybody in the room.

:::detail{title="Not that Doctor Octopus"}
This catalogue also carries Otto Octavius from *Spider-Man 2*, played by Alfred
Molina, who is a different person in a different universe. The name is the only
thing they share.
:::
