John Wraith is a mutant who can teleport and a former member of Team X, the
covert unit Stryker ran with Logan and Victor Creed in it. will.i.am plays
him in *X-Men Origins: Wolverine*.

After Team X disbanded he opened a fitness centre in Las Vegas, which is
where Logan finds him. He reveals vital information and accompanies Logan to
New Orleans to find Gambit.

:::spoiler{title="Sabretooth"}
He is killed in a fight with Sabretooth, and his DNA goes into Weapon XI,
the thing Stryker builds out of everybody's powers at the end of the film.
:::
