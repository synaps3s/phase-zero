Sean Cassidy has a supersonic scream capable of shattering glass and propelling
him into the air, and is a former member of the X-Men. Caleb Landry Jones plays
him.

*First Class* is at its best in the middle, when it is about a group of
teenagers finding out what they can do in a garden, and Banshee is the clearest
example: the power is useless until somebody makes him jump.
