Miles G. Morales is the Prowler of Earth-42, voiced by Jharrel Jerome. He is
the same person as Miles Morales, in the universe where the spider that was
meant for him ended up somewhere else.

He is the strongest idea in either film. Everything Miles is told about canon
and inevitability is answered by one room: a version of himself who had the same
family, the same city and the same character, and became this instead, because
of a spider that took a different lift.

:::spoiler{title="What happened to that universe"}
Without a Spider-Man, his city has no protection, his father is dead rather than
his uncle, and his uncle Aaron raised him. Nothing about him is written as
inevitable, which is the point being made against Miguel.
:::
