The High Evolutionary is an alien geneticist who set out to take what he
considered lower life forms and improve them into a perfect species. He is
played by Chukwudi Iwuji as a man with no charm at all, which is deliberate and
correct.

He is the most upsetting antagonist in this catalogue, and the reason is that
his crimes are procedural. He does not conquer anything. He runs a laboratory,
files results, and incinerates whole populations when the results disappoint
him. *Vol. 3* is a film about a talking raccoon and it is the closest the
Marvel films have come to a horror film about animal testing.

:::spoiler{title="Subject 89P13"}
Rocket is one of his experiments. The flashbacks in *Vol. 3* show the cage, the
friends and what happened to them, and the film is careful that the High
Evolutionary never once sees any of it as cruelty. When his own creations turn
on him he is simply annoyed.
:::
