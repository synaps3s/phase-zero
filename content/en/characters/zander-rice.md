Zander Rice is the lead scientist of Alkali-Transigen, and Richard E. Grant
plays him in *Logan*. Wikipedia's list describes him as the man responsible
for the near eradication of mutants, and for the two mutant weapons the
film is about, X-23 and X-24. He is the reason Laura exists and the reason
the thing that hunts her exists, which is a lot of a film for a man with so
few scenes.

:::spoiler{title="What Logan does about it"}
Wolverine shoots and kills him.
:::
