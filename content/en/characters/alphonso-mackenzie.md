Alphonso Mackenzie is a S.H.I.E.L.D. mechanic under Robert Gonzales, a founding
member of the so-called real S.H.I.E.L.D., who infiltrates Coulson's group with
Morse. After being briefly mind-controlled by Kree technology his distrust of
the alien and the superhuman deepens, and he leaves when his own leaders agree
to work with the other side. Henry Simmons plays him.

He is the one character in that series who keeps saying the sensible thing out
loud, which is why the show has to keep answering him.
