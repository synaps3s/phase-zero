Molly Hayes Hernandez is the youngest member of the Runaways and is
characterised by her positive attitude. Allegra Acosta plays her, and Molly
discovers she has superhuman strength.

The group needs her for a structural reason. Five teenagers arguing about
whether their parents are evil is a good scene once; a child who wants to know
whether they are still going to have dinner is what stops it becoming only that.
