Gwen Stacy is the Spider-Woman of Earth-65, a universe where she got the bite
and Peter Parker did not survive what he did about it. Hailee Steinfeld plays
her. Her father George Stacy is a police captain, and he is investigating the
death that she was there for.

*Across the Spider-Verse* opens with fifteen minutes that are entirely hers,
and they are the best fifteen minutes in either film: a whole life told in
colour, with the walls changing behind her according to what she is feeling.

:::detail{title="Two Gwen Stacys here"}
This catalogue also carries the Gwen Stacy played by Emma Stone in
*The Amazing Spider-Man*, in a different universe with a different ending. They
are not the same person and neither is a version of the other.
:::
