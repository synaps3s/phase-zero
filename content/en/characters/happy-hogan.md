Harold "Happy" Hogan is Tony Stark's bodyguard and close friend, and the
head of security for Stark Industries. Jon Favreau, who directed the first
two *Iron Man* films, plays him in all of them and kept playing him long
after he stopped directing. Stark later makes him head of asset management
for the Avengers. After Stark's death he becomes a mentor to Peter Parker,
and is romantically drawn to Peter's aunt May.

The reader who sent this list wrote that he later becomes "forehead of
security", which is a joke about the character rather than a mistake about
him, and it stays here as sent.

:::detail{title="Eight, and what is not counted"}
Wikipedia counts him in eight projects: the three *Iron Man* films, the
three Tom Holland *Spider-Man* films, *Endgame* and *Deadpool & Wolverine*.
*Infinity War* is not among them, and neither is *Brand New Day*. The same
page records two alternate versions in the animated *What If...?*, one of
whom becomes a Hulk-like creature called Freak after a dose of Banner's
blood, and a Sir Harold Hogan in a 1602 universe; neither is counted for a
live-action person.
:::
