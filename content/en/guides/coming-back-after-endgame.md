Most people who stopped, stopped there, and it was a reasonable place to stop.
The story that had been building for eleven years finished. What has happened
since is not more of the same, and knowing how it is different is most of what
you need.

## Three things changed at once

**Television became the story.** Before 2021 the series were a parallel track you
could ignore. From *WandaVision* onward they carry plot the films then assume.
This is the single biggest change and the most common reason a returning viewer
feels lost in a film.

**The single timeline became several.** *Loki* ends by removing the thing that
kept history to one line. Everything after that operates under different rules,
and a large part of the appeal since has been characters from other studios'
films arriving as versions from elsewhere.

**The cast turned over.** The two people the first saga was about are gone. What
replaced them is a wider, looser ensemble that has not yet been pulled into one
shape, and opinion on that is genuinely divided.

## The short way back in

Four entries, in this order, and you are current on the rules:

*WandaVision*, because the film that follows it assumes all of it.
*Loki*, both seasons, because it is where the multiverse arrives.
*Spider-Man: No Way Home*, because it is the first film that uses the new rules.
*Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*, because it is the consequence of
the first two.

After that, the paths on this site will take you the rest of the way, and the
timeline's second control will keep the list to a size you can actually watch.

:::detail{title="What you have not missed"}
Quite a lot, and this catalogue will not pretend otherwise. Several entries

The pages for those say so on their own, which is the point of them saying it
rather than a guide trying to rank them.
