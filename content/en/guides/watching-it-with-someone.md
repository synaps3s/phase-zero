title: Watching it with somebody who has seen none of it
oneLine: How to introduce this to a person who is willing but not enthusiastic,
  without losing them in the first month.
order: 5
audience: everyone
---

The failure mode is always the same. Somebody who loves this hands somebody who
does not a list of thirty-five films, and the list itself is the thing that ends
it.

## Do not start with a list

Start with one film that works on its own and stop. *Iron Man* is the obvious
one and the best one for this: it is a self-contained story about an arms
manufacturer, it has no homework attached, and it ends properly.

If they want another, you have a viewer. If they do not, they have watched a good
film and nothing has been wasted, which is a much better outcome than abandoning
a project.

## Let the second one be the payoff

*The Avengers* is the film that explains what the whole idea was for, and it
works with almost nothing before it. Going straight from the first to the fourth
is a real shortcut, and it is more persuasive than watching the two in between.

Nothing between them is required to follow it. The one-evening path on this site
is exactly these two.

## Then let them choose

Once somebody is in, hand them the timeline rather than an order. Let them pick
the character they liked. The catalogue is built so that following one person
through it is a legitimate way to watch, and the watch paths on this site are
several versions of that.

## What to avoid

Do not explain the phases. Do not start with story order. Do not put a series
first. And do not tell them the surprise in the film you are about to watch
because you are excited about the one after it, which is the most common way this
goes wrong and the hardest to resist.
