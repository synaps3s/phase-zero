Eight episodes, released daily over Christmas as the second season had been, and
the end of the series. It includes an episode about the Red Guardian that had
been written for the previous season and held.

The anthology ends without pretending it was building to something, which is
the correct decision for a format whose whole premise is that none of it counts.
