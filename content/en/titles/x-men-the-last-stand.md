title: "X-Men: The Last Stand"
oneLine: "A cure for being a mutant goes on sale, and everyone has to decide whether taking it would be a betrayal."
tagline: "The one with the cure, and the one that pays for it"
---

The premise is the best in the series and the film knows it for about forty
minutes. A pharmaceutical company can make you normal. It is voluntary, it
works, and it is instantly a political crisis, because the existence of a cure
turns a fact about people into a choice they can be blamed for not making.

It also adapts *The Dark Phoenix Saga* at the same time, in the same film, and
the two halves are at war for the running time. Bryan Singer left for
*Superman Returns* and Brett Ratner took over, and the shift is visible.

:::detail{title="What it broke"}
This is the film later entries spend a decade working around. *The Wolverine*
follows directly from its ending, *Days of Future Past* is built to undo large
parts of it, and *Dark Phoenix* eventually re-adapts the same comics storyline
from scratch with a different cast.
:::
