Every beat that the 2011 film would later make famous is already here: the
serum, the war, the Red Skull, the ice, the man out of time. Matt Salinger plays
Rogers, Albert Pyun directs, and the whole thing was produced by the 21st
Century Film Corporation, a company that no longer exists.

It is worth watching as an argument about resources rather than ideas. The story
Marvel would eventually spend two hundred million dollars telling was told here
for a fraction of that, and the difference is not in the plot.

:::detail{title="Why the date looks wrong"}
The catalogue records 14 December 1990, a United Kingdom date, because the film
had no United States theatrical release. It got a limited run abroad and then
went to video and cable television in the United States in the summer of 1992,
through a home video label.

The article contradicts itself here in a way worth knowing about: its infobox
gives the 1990 United Kingdom date, its Versions list says the theatrical cut has
been available in the United Kingdom since 1991, and its box office paragraph
refers to a 1990 release in Europe. The certification behind the running time is
dated November 1990, which supports the earlier date, so that is the one recorded
here.
:::
