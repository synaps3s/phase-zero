title: "The Fantastic Four: First Steps"
oneLine: "Four astronauts who came back from a mission changed have been famous for four years, and something arrives that eats planets."
tagline: "The one set somewhere else"
---

It skips the origin. When the film opens the team have been the Fantastic Four
for four years, they are celebrities, Reed Richards' inventions have moved the
technology of the whole world forward and Sue Storm's diplomacy has largely
disarmed it. The film is not about how they got their powers. It is about what
a family that famous does when the bill arrives.

The bill is Galactus, who eats planets because he is hungry and has been for
longer than the Earth has existed, and who makes the team an offer that no
story of this kind normally dares put on the table.

:::detail{title="Why it is set in another universe"}
It takes place on Earth-828, not on the Earth the rest of the run happens on,
and that is a production decision made early rather than a twist. Director Matt
Shakman did not want a plot hole about where a team this powerful was during
*Avengers: Infinity War*, and Kevin Feige has described the alternate setting as
freeing.

It also lets the film be a period piece. The comics were made by Jack Kirby and
Stan Lee in the 1960s, and this is a 1960s that kept its own idea of the future,
built out of the optimism of the space race rather than out of nostalgia.
:::
