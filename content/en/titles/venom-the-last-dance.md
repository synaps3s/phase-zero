The third film closes the arrangement. Eddie is hunted by humans who want the
symbiote and by symbiotes who want what Eddie has become, and the film strips
away everything except the relationship the series was actually about all along.

Kelly Marcel wrote the first two and directs this one, which gives it a
consistency the middle film did not need to have. It is the fifth film of Sony's
Spider-Man Universe and the last of the three about this pair.

:::detail{title="One writer across three films"}
Marcel has a writing credit on all three, and this is her directorial debut.
That is unusual for a series of this size, and it explains why the tonal swerve
between the first two films reads as a decision rather than a correction.
:::
