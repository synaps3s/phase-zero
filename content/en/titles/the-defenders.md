---
title: "The Defenders"
oneLine: "Four New Yorkers who have spent their own series avoiding each other end up in the same building against the same enemy, and get on badly."
tagline: "The crossover"
---

Matt Murdock, Jessica Jones, Luke Cage and Danny Rand each arrive at the Hand
from their own direction, and the miniseries is at its best in the middle
stretch where the four of them are in a room being irritated by each other. The
casting does the work that the plot does not.

It is eight episodes, it assumes all four of the preceding series, and it is
the closest the Marvel Television era came to its own *Avengers*.
