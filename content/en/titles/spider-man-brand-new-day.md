title: "Spider-Man: Brand New Day"
oneLine: "Nobody remembers who Peter Parker is, including everyone he loved, and he has to build a life from nothing while still doing the job."
tagline: "The one after the erasure"
---

The previous film ended by removing him from the world's memory. This one
starts there: no Stark money, no Avengers on the phone, no friends who know
what he does at night. It is the first time the character has been where the
comics have always had him, which is alone and broke in New York.

It was released on 31 July 2026 and took over two billion dollars, which makes
it one of the largest openings this catalogue records.
