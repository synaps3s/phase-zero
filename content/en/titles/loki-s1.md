---
title: Loki
oneLine: An earlier version of Loki is arrested by a bureaucracy that exists
  outside time, and told that free will was never real.
tagline: Six episodes that rewrote the rules of everything after them
---

The premise is a joke that turns serious. The most self-important character in
the story is picked up by an organisation that finds him neither dangerous nor
interesting, processed like paperwork, and put to work.

The series spends its first half being funny about institutions and its second
half being serious about what those institutions were protecting. By the end it
has taken the single timeline that every previous film depended on and shown
what was holding it in place.

:::spoiler{level="major" from="loki-s1"}
The finale ends the protection deliberately, which is what opens the multiverse.
Every multiverse story that follows is downstream of a choice made in this room.
:::

:::detail{title="Where it sits for a newcomer"}
This is the first entry where a series is not optional. Films released after it
assume you know what happened here, and they do not stop to explain it.

If you are watching at the "films only" level, this is the point at which that
level starts costing you.
:::
