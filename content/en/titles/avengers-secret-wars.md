---

It is scheduled for release on 17 December 2027, and filming is expected to
begin in late 2026 at Pinewood Studios in England. That is the whole of what
can be written about it from a source today.

:::detail{title="The dates have moved twice"}
It was announced for 7 November 2025, then moved to 7 May 2027, and is now
scheduled for 17 December 2027. The companion film has moved twice as well.

Older articles and older lists still carry the superseded dates, so if you find
this film in two places with two different years, both were true when they were
written.
:::
