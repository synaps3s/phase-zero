A sheriff investigates a series of deaths on drained oil land and finds a
Seminole legend walking. The Man-Thing is not a villain in the ordinary sense.
It is a swamp with intent, and its defining property, that its touch burns
whoever feels fear, is one of the better ideas in this material.

It is loosely based on the comic and plays as a monster film rather than a
superhero one.

:::detail{title="Why the date looks wrong"}
It was made for cinemas and did not get them in the United States. The date
recorded here, 30 April 2005, is its premiere on the Sci Fi Channel. It did
receive an international theatrical release, which took roughly a million
dollars, but the reference article gives no date for it, so there is no earlier
date to record.

Its reference entry is not even filed as a film. It uses a television infobox,
which is the clearest signal available about how it eventually reached its
audience.
:::
