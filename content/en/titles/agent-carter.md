Peggy Carter is the most competent person in every room she is in and is
treated as a secretary in all of them, and the series is honest that this is
the actual obstacle rather than a framing device. The cases are pulp, the
period detail is exact, and Jarvis is the best comic performance in this part
of the catalogue.

Two seasons, eighteen episodes, and one of the very few entries here that
begins and ends on its own terms.

:::detail{title="Close to the films by design"}
Because Carter came out of a film, Marvel Studios stayed involved. The
showrunners describe a liaison who would tell them what they could not touch,
and Christopher Markus, who wrote the film she came from, noted that "you
really only need to drop the tiniest bit of hint and its connected".

The series claims to originate both the Black Widow programme and the Winter
Soldier programme that the films would later use, and its second season
introduces the substance that a later television series builds a character's
powers out of.
:::
