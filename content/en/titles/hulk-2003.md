---
title: "Hulk"
oneLine: "A scientist survives a dose of gamma radiation that should have killed him, and discovers that what it woke up was already there."
tagline: "The one directed as a family tragedy"
---

Ang Lee made this, and it is the least like anything else in the catalogue. It
is a film about inheritance, about a father who experimented on himself and
passed the result to a son who did not consent to any of it, and it is willing
to be slow and sad about that for two hours.

The Hulk arrives as the consequence of a lab accident, but the film is clear
that the accident only released something Bruce Banner was born carrying. That
is a much bleaker reading of the character than the one that followed it.

:::detail{title="Cut like the page"}
Its most discussed feature is the editing, which splits the screen into panels
and slides them across each other, deliberately imitating comic book layout.
Nothing else in this catalogue tries it. Opinions on whether it works have not
converged in twenty years.
:::
