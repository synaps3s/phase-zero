A television film made a decade before *Iron Man*, with a Fury who has an eye
patch, a cigar and a bad attitude, brought back to stop a Hydra plot involving
the Death's Head virus.

It is here because the catalogue is a record rather than a recommendation. There
is a straight line between this and the character who walks into Tony Stark's
living room ten years later, and the line is worth being able to see.
