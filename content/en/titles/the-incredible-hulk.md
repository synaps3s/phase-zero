---
title: The Incredible Hulk
oneLine: A scientist hiding from the army he used to work for tries to cure the
  thing he becomes when his pulse rises.
tagline: The one that is easy to skip and probably should not be
---

Bruce Banner is in Brazil, working in a bottling plant, learning breathing
exercises and counting the days since his last incident. He is not looking for
a fight. He is looking for a cure, and for the army general who wants to
weaponize what happened to him to leave him alone.

The film is unusual in this run because its hero spends most of it running away.
The transformation is a symptom, not a power, and everyone who wants to
replicate it is presented as making an obvious mistake.

:::detail{title="Why this one feels separate"}
It is the only entry in the whole run whose lead was recast afterwards, and the
only one Marvel Studios made while another studio held the distribution rights,
which is why it stayed off streaming services for years and why later films
reference it far less than they reference their own predecessors.

Watching it is optional for following the plot. Skipping it costs you the reason
one character is already tired when you meet him again.
:::
