The third season strips the show back. There is barely a costume in it. Murdock
is at his weakest and Fisk is at his most patient, working the system from the
inside with a legal strategy rather than a gang, and the season is about the
slow demolition of a person's public identity.

It is the last of the Netflix run and the one the revival picks up from, which
makes it, as it turned out, a pause rather than an ending.
