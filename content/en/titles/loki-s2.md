---

The first season ended by removing the thing that kept a single timeline in
place. The second is about the machinery failing: an organisation that never
understood what it was maintaining, a loom that cannot take the load, and a man
slipping uncontrollably through his own history while trying to fix it.

It resolves into a choice rather than a fight, which is unusual here, and the
choice is made by the character least likely to make it.

:::detail{title="Two seasons, two entries"}
This catalogue records a season as its own entry when the source material does,
which is why *Loki* appears twice. The two seasons are three years apart and
the second assumes the first completely.
:::
