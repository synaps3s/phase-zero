The Mutant Underground is still running a refugee operation. The Inner Circle of
the Hellfire Club has concluded that hiding is a strategy for losing. Sixteen
episodes in which the argument between them is the plot, with the Morlocks and
the Purifiers pulled in on either side.

It is the last season of the series and the last live-action X-Men television
made under that licence.
