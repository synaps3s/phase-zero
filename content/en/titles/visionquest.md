---
title: "VisionQuest"
oneLine: "A series about Vision, scheduled for October 2026, and already filmed."
tagline: "Announced, and shot"
---

It is scheduled to debut on Disney+ on 14 October 2026 and will run to eight
episodes, as part of Phase Six: the first two on launch day, then one a week,
with the finale on 25 November. Filming wrapped in late July 2025 and was
completed in August.

The character it is named for died in *Avengers: Infinity War* and was rebuilt
twice afterwards by two different parties for two different reasons, which is
the situation the series inherits. What it does with it is not yet on the
record.
