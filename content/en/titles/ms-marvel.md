---
title: "Ms. Marvel"
oneLine: "A sixteen-year-old fan of the Avengers from Jersey City finds out that the family heirloom she borrowed for a costume is not a costume accessory."
tagline: "The one that is genuinely about a family"
---

Kamala Khan writes fan fiction about Captain Marvel, cannot get permission to
go to a convention, and is the only person in her school who is more interested
in superheroes than in the people around her. Then a bangle from her
great-grandmother turns out to do something.

The series is warm in a way little else here is, and its most serious material
is not the powers. It is the Partition of India, which the show reaches by
following the heirloom backwards, and which it treats as the real inheritance.
