---
title: "Daredevil: Born Again"
tagline: "Season one"
oneLine: "A blind lawyer who gave up the mask and a former crime boss who gave up the guns each try to do it legitimately, and neither of them can."
---

Matt Murdock has stopped. Wilson Fisk has run for mayor of New York and won.
Both of them are attempting to get what they want through the system rather
than around it, and the season is the slow, unbearable process of each of them
finding out that they cannot.

It is a revival of the Netflix series rather than a reboot, with the same two
lead performances, and it is the point at which those thirteen seasons stopped
being an open question and became part of the record.
