The first film was a body-horror premise played mostly straight. This one gives
up on that entirely and commits to what audiences actually liked: two
personalities sharing a flat and a body, arguing about chickens, ethics and
whose turn it is to be in charge.

Cletus Kasady is the counterweight. Where Eddie and Venom are a bad marriage,
Kasady and Carnage are a mutual amplification, and the film is clear that the
difference between the two pairs is the only thing that makes Eddie a hero.

:::detail{title="Directed by a performance capture specialist"}
Andy Serkis directed it, which is a neater fit than it looks. The film's central
relationship is between an actor and a creature that only exists after the fact,
and the choice of director is the clearest signal Sony gave about what kind of
film it thought it was making.
:::
