The first of three television films made after the series ended, and the first
crossover in live-action Marvel television. Donald Blake summons Thor, who is
loud, arrogant and entirely uninterested in Banner's problem, and the film is
built around the two of them being incompatible.

It was intended as a pilot for a Thor series that did not happen.
