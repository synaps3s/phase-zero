The film's subject is exhaustion. He is broke, late for everything, and losing the powers precisely when he stops wanting them, which the film treats as a psychological event rather than a plot device.

Its antagonist is a decent man ruined by his own work and mourned by the film, and the train sequence remains the best action scene any of these films has staged.
