title: "The Amazing Spider-Man"
oneLine: "The story starts again from the beginning, with a teenager, a spider, and a father who disappeared without explaining anything."
tagline: "The reboot"
---

Five years after the last film in the previous series, Sony began again with a
different director, a different cast and a different Peter Parker. This one is
prickly, awkward in a specific modern way, and given a thread the earlier films
did not have: his parents left when he was a child, and the film wants to know
why.

Everything else you would expect is here, done again. The bite, the loss, the
decision, the first villain. Watching it next to the 2002 film is the clearest
demonstration in this catalogue of how much a story changes when only the tone
is different.

:::detail{title="Two Peter Parkers, no shared history"}
This is a separate continuity, not a sequel. Nothing here follows from the Raimi
films and nothing in them leads to it. The catalogue keeps them in different
universes for that reason, and both eventually turn out to be real at the same
time, which is a sentence this material has earned.
:::
