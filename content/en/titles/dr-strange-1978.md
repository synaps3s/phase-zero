---

A television film that gets the shape of the character right decades before
anyone spent money on him. Stephen Strange is a psychiatrist rather than a
surgeon, the mysticism is played as real, and the antagonist is Morgan le Fay
working to a deadline set by something worse.

It was intended as a pilot. It did not become a series, and the character waited
thirty-eight years for a film.
