title: Spider-Man
oneLine: A teenager is bitten by a modified spider, discovers he can do things
  nobody can, and learns what it costs when he decides that is not his problem.
tagline: The film that proved this material could carry a blockbuster
---

Before this, comic book films were a genre studios were nervous about. This one
took the origin seriously, played the emotional beats straight, and made the
central lesson a consequence rather than a speech.

Its Peter Parker is broke, unpopular and unglamorous, and the film keeps him
that way. That is a choice most later versions of the character quietly stopped
making.

:::detail{title="Its place in the wider picture"}
This is a separate continuity from the connected films, made by a different
studio two decades ago. For most of that time it was simply a different
Spider-Man that had nothing to do with anything else.

That changed retroactively when the multiverse films decided these versions all
coexist, which is a good example of how this material rearranges its own past.
:::
