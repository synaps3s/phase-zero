---

It is lighter and shorter than the first film, and it has the better idea. The
Silver Surfer is not an enemy. He is an advance scout for something that eats
worlds, and he has been doing it long enough to have made a peace with it that
the film slowly takes away from him.

Doctor Doom returns and the team has to work with him, which is the sequel doing
what sequels do. The Surfer is why anyone remembers it.

:::detail{title="Galactus as weather"}
The thing the Surfer works for is Galactus, and in this film it is rendered as
an enormous cloud rather than as a figure. That choice was still being discussed
eighteen years later: reference material on the 2025 Marvel Studios film notes
that its comic-accurate Galactus was designed in explicit contrast to the
cloud-like version used here.
:::
