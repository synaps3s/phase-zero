title: "Iron Fist"
tagline: "Season two"
oneLine: "The Iron Fist tries to be the protector of a city that did not ask for one, while other people work out how to take the power off him."
---

The second season is shorter, faster and much better disciplined than the
first. It narrows to a street-level fight over who gets to hold the power, and
it treats the fist as a thing that can be lost rather than as an identity.

It is the last season of the show. The improvement arrived a year too late to
save it.
