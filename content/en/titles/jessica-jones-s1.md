Kilgrave is the most frightening antagonist in this catalogue and he never
throws a punch. He tells people what to do and they do it, and the season is
about what that leaves behind in the people it was done to.

The show is a detective story about coercive control with the metaphor made
literal, and it is careful with it. What Jessica Jones is fighting is not a
plan to take a city. It is a man who thinks he did nothing wrong because
everyone said yes.
