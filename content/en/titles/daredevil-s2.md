The season is two films stapled together and it mostly gets away with it. Frank
Castle is introduced as an antagonist and then given the best argument in the
show: he does not think Murdock is wrong, he thinks Murdock is squeamish. The
courtroom sequence built around him is the strongest thing either season had
done.

Elektra Natchios arrives from Murdock's past with a mystical order attached,
and that half of the season is where the series starts writing cheques for
plots it will spend two years failing to cash.
