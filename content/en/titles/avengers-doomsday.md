There is very little that can honestly be written here yet, so this page says
what is known and stops. It is scheduled for release in the United States on 18
December 2026, in over four thousand cinemas. Filming began in April 2025 at
Pinewood Studios in England and wrapped that September. It is part of Phase Six.

Everything else in circulation about it is either a cast announcement or a
guess, and this catalogue does not carry either.

:::detail{title="A runtime that is not a runtime"}
Reference material lists an approximate running time of 165 minutes. The trade
reporting behind that figure says plainly that it exists so that premium-format
tickets can go on sale, and that the final cut is unlikely to be locked this
far ahead.

This entry therefore records no runtime. A number that was published in order
to sell a seat is not a fact about a film.
:::
