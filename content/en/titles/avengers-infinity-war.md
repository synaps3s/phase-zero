The structural achievement is that it is Thanos's film. Everyone else is
reacting, the story follows his plan rather than theirs, and it gives him the
one thing this material usually withholds from its antagonists, which is
interiority.

It is also the only entry that assumes almost everything. Ten years of setup is
called in, and the film does not stop to explain any of it.

:::spoiler{level="major" from="avengers-infinity-war"}
He succeeds. The film ends with the plan carried out and the heroes having
failed, and it does not undo it before the credits.
:::

:::detail{title="What you need before it"}
More than any other title here. The three Avengers films before it at minimum,
plus Guardians of the Galaxy for half the cast and Doctor Strange for how the
ending works.

This is the film that makes the case for release order better than any argument
could.
:::
