---
title: "The Falcon and the Winter Soldier"
oneLine: "Two men who barely tolerate each other are made to work together after the shield they both have a claim on is given to somebody else."
tagline: "The one about who gets to be the symbol"
---

Six months after *Avengers: Endgame*, Sam Wilson has handed the shield to a
museum and the United States government has taken it back out and given it to a
soldier of its own choosing. What follows is a road film about two people who
each think the other is the problem, wrapped around a genuine argument about
what the shield means when a government is holding it.

The antagonists are the best thing in it: a group who were better off during
the five years everyone was gone, and who are being pushed back out of the
countries that took them in.
