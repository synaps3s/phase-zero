---
title: "Dark Phoenix"
oneLine: "A rescue in orbit goes wrong, something enormous gets inside Jean Grey, and the team has to decide what to do about one of their own."
tagline: "The one that ends it"
---

It re-adapts the comics storyline that *X-Men: The Last Stand* had already tried
to fit into half of a film, this time as the whole of one. Jean Grey absorbs a
cosmic force during a mission and comes back with power she did not ask for and
memories she was not supposed to have, and the film is about the people around
her arguing over whether she is a patient, a weapon or a threat.

It is the twelfth film in the series, the fourth and last of the prequels, and
the end of the road. It is also the first main entry without Hugh Jackman, which
turns out to matter more than anyone expected.

:::detail{title="The last one made under Fox"}
Marketed internationally and released on home media as *X-Men: Dark Phoenix*.
It was the last film produced by Bad Hat Harry Productions for this series,
though the company went uncredited following allegations made against Bryan
Singer in 2019.
:::
