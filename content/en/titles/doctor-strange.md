---
title: Doctor Strange
oneLine: A brilliant surgeon loses the use of his hands and, looking for a cure,
  finds people who bend space instead.
tagline: Where the rules stop being physics
---

The arrogance is the point again, as it was with Stark, but the arc is
different: Strange does not learn humility so much as learn that being the best
in the room is worthless when the room is infinite.

The film's real argument is in its ending, which refuses to be a fight. The
solution is patience applied as a weapon, and it is the most interesting thing
this run has done with a climax.

:::detail{title="What it opens"}
Magic becomes a working part of the universe here, with rules stated plainly
enough that later films can bend them without cheating. The Time Stone is here,
and so is the first sustained look at what it costs to use one.
:::
