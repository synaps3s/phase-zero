---
title: "The Marvels"
oneLine: "Three heroes swap places whenever any of them uses her powers, which is a disaster and also the film's best idea."
tagline: "The shortest film in the run, and it moves"
---

The body-swapping turns every fight into a puzzle in three places at once, and the film is smart enough to build a whole set piece out of choreographing it.

It assumes two series you may not have watched, which is the fairest complaint against it. It is also the lightest thing here in years and does not overstay.
