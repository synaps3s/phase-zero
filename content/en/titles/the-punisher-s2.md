---

The second season opens with Castle in a bar in the middle of nowhere, and the
question it puts to him is whether the Punisher was a phase or a person. He
saves a girl who is being hunted, and the answer arrives by the end of the
first episode.

It is the last of the Netflix run to be produced, and the character came back
years later in a very different production.
