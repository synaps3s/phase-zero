title: "Ant-Man and the Wasp"
oneLine: A rescue mission for someone lost thirty years ago in a place nobody has come back from.
tagline: "The lightest film in the run, and it knows it"
---

It arrives immediately after the heaviest entry so far and refuses to acknowledge it, which reads as tone-deaf until you realise it is deliberate: this film is happening at the same time as that one.

The chases are its best idea. Everything in them changes size mid-motion, so a car chase becomes a physics joke that keeps escalating.
