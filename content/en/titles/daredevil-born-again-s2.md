oneLine: "The mayor of New York has a task force for people in masks, and the blind man who spent a season trying to stay out of one goes looking for allies."
---

Wilson Fisk is running the city and has put a name and a budget on his
hostility: an Anti-Vigilante Task Force. Matt Murdock spends the season doing
the thing the first season would not let him do, which is gather people.

Eight episodes, weekly, from March to May 2026. The season stops being about
whether either man can work inside the system and starts being about what
happens when one of them owns it.
