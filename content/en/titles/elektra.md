---
title: "Elektra"
oneLine: "An assassin who has already died once takes a contract, refuses to complete it, and ends up protecting the two people she was sent to kill."
tagline: "The spin-off"
---

Jennifer Garner returns from *Daredevil* in a film that has almost nothing else
to do with it. Elektra Natchios was killed in that film and has been revived
here by a blind martial arts master called Stick, which the film treats as
background rather than as a question. She is a contract killer with a
reputation, and the story turns on the moment she looks at her targets and
stops.

The Hand are the antagonists: a syndicate of ninja mercenaries who want the
girl she was sent to kill, because the girl is what they call a Treasure and so,
it turns out, was Elektra.

:::detail{title="It did not end there"}
Garner played the character again nineteen years later, in
*Deadpool & Wolverine* in 2024. A film widely treated as a dead end turned out
to have a thread still attached to it, which is worth remembering before writing
off anything else in this section.
:::
