---
title: Iron Man 2
oneLine: The thing keeping Tony Stark alive is also killing him, and he tells
  nobody.
tagline: Success, and the bill for it
---

Six months after announcing himself, Stark is a celebrity, a one-man deterrent,
and quietly dying: the reactor in his chest is poisoning his blood. He responds
by behaving worse, which is a more honest portrait of the character than any
speech about responsibility.

Meanwhile a man whose father was written out of Stark family history builds the
same technology in a basement in Moscow, and the film's real argument arrives
with him. Stark's defence has always been that nobody else can do this. Ivan
Vanko does it in six weeks with scrap.

:::detail{title="What this film is carrying for later"}
More setup lives here than in any other Phase One entry, and some of it is
buried: the wider organization behind Nick Fury, a second armored character, a
government that wants the suits, and a briefcase of material that becomes
important two films later.

If the middle feels crowded, that is why. It is doing two jobs.
:::
