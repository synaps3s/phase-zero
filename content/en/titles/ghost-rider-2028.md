It is scheduled for release on 28 July 2028. There is a date and there is trade
reporting behind it, and beyond that nothing on the record.

The character has been to cinema twice before, in 2007 and 2011, in a
continuity connected to nothing. Those two films are in this catalogue and this
one is not a sequel to them.

:::detail{title="Announced, or in development"}
Reference material puts this film's status as in development while also
reporting a studio release date from named trade coverage. Those two things do
not usually sit together.

This catalogue records it as announced, because a date in a schedule is a fact
even when the film behind it is not yet a production. The phase, Seven, is
described in the source as expected rather than designated.
:::
