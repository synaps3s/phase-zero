title: "X-Men: The Animated Series"
oneLine: "The Saturday morning cartoon that taught a generation these characters, and the only version of them that ran for five years without recasting anybody."
tagline: "Five seasons on Fox Kids"
---

For most people who were children in the nineties, this is where the X-Men
came from. It ran for five seasons and seventy-six episodes on Fox Kids,
starting on Halloween 1992 and finishing in September 1997, and it adapted the
comics with a seriousness the format was not supposed to allow: the Phoenix
saga, Days of Future Past, the Legacy Virus, mutant registration.

It is in this catalogue for a specific reason. *X-Men '97* is not a reboot of
it and not a homage to it. It is the next season, thirty years late, with most
of the same voices and the same theme tune, which means this show is a
prerequisite rather than a curiosity.

:::detail{title="Where it sits"}
It shares no continuity with the Fox films or with anything Marvel Studios has
made, which is why it is filed outside the connected universes rather than
inside one of them.

The episodes run twenty-two minutes each, which is the standard half-hour slot
with the advertisements taken out.
:::
