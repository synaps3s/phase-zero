title: "Captain America: The First Avenger"
oneLine: A man too small and too sick to enlist is chosen for an experiment
  precisely because he has spent his life being the one who gets up again.
tagline: Set seventy years before everything else
---

Most of this film happens in 1943. Steve Rogers is repeatedly refused by the
army, keeps lying on the forms, and is eventually picked by a scientist who is
not looking for the strongest candidate. The point is made plainly and early:
the serum amplifies what is already there, so it matters enormously who you give
it to.

What follows is a war film with a propaganda tour in the middle of it, which is
the film's sharpest idea. The government's first instinct is not to deploy him
but to sell bonds with him.

:::detail{title="Where this sits in time"}
This is the earliest point in the whole story, seventy years before the rest of
Phase One, and it is the reason a chronological first watch is a worse
experience than a release-order one: the film is full of objects and names that
mean nothing yet and land hard once you have seen the later films.

Watch it fifth, not first.
:::
