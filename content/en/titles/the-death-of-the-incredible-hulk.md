---
title: "The Death of the Incredible Hulk"
oneLine: "Posing as a janitor to get near a geneticist whose work might cure him, Banner is caught between a spy ring and himself."
tagline: "The one that ends it"
---

The last of the three television films, and it does what the title says. Banner
has spent thirteen years trying to stop being this, gets close enough to hope,
and is pulled into somebody else's operation instead.

It was made expecting more to follow. There were none, which means the ending
lands as the end of a fourteen-year run whether it was meant to or not.
