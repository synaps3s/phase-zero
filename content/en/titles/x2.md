title: "X2"
oneLine: "A military scientist raids the school for mutant children, and the two sides who spend every film arguing have to work together."
tagline: "The one where the two sides need each other"
---

The first film had to explain what a mutant was. This one does not, and it uses
the room that buys to do something the genre rarely tries: it puts the heroes
and the villains on the same side without pretending the argument between them
has been settled.

William Stryker is a soldier who wants every mutant on Earth dead and has the
machinery to do it. Against that, the school and the Brotherhood are the same
minority with two strategies, and the film is honest that only one of them was
ever going to be proved right by the ending.

:::detail{title="Two titles, one film"}
It was marketed with the subtitle *X-Men United* and released internationally as
*X-Men 2*, which is also how the Italian release names it. The film's own titles
say only *X2*.
:::
