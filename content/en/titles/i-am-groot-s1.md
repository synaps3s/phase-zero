---
title: "I Am Groot"
tagline: "Season one"
oneLine: "Five shorts, almost wordless, about a very small tree getting into trouble on a spaceship."
---

Each one runs about five minutes and has no plot to speak of. Baby Groot takes a
bath, meets something in a puddle, and is generally a hazard to a vessel he is
too small to be trusted on.

They sit between the first *Guardians of the Galaxy* and one of the mid-credits
scenes of the second, and they require nothing at all. It is the lightest thing
in this catalogue and it does not pretend otherwise.
