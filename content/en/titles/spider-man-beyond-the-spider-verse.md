Scheduled for 18 June 2027, after moving twice. It is described as the
conclusion of the trilogy and of Miles Morales' arc, with the Spot carrying over
as the antagonist.

Its directors said in July 2026 that the crew was well into editing and on
schedule, which is why this is recorded as in production. Everything else about
it is unsourced, and this page stops here.
