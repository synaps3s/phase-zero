---

The second season widens the format. Some episodes are jokes, one is a
Christmas caper with Happy Hogan, and one goes to a place none of the films have
been: Kahhori, a Mohawk woman in the fifteenth century, empowered by the
Tesseract long before anybody in the story knows what it is.

Nine episodes released over nine consecutive days, which is a strange way to
watch anything and suits an anthology better than it would suit a serial.
