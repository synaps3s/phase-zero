title: "Helstrom"
oneLine: "A brother and a sister with a monstrous parent hunt the worst of humanity, in a series that spent its release insisting it was not part of anything."
tagline: "The one that opted out"
---

Daimon and Ana Helstrom are the children of a serial killer, and the series is
a horror show about inheritance that runs for ten episodes and stops. It was
cancelled after one season.

It is here for completeness, and because it is the clearest possible example of
why this catalogue keeps the Marvel Television era at the fullest depth rather
than folding it into the main run.

:::detail{title="Not tied to anything, by its own account"}
It was announced as the first series of an Adventure into Fear franchise that
would exist inside the continuity of Marvel Television's other shows but would
not cross over with them or with the films.

On release the showrunner went further, describing the series as "siloed off",
"not tied to the MCU", "our own separate thing", and calling it freeing to have
"just this little pocket of the universe". By July 2020 Disney had dropped the
Marvel name from the title. A reviewer noted that beyond a single reference to
an oil company that appears everywhere in this material, it has essentially no
connection to anything else.

Very little in this catalogue has to be argued into a category. This one argued
itself out of one.
:::
