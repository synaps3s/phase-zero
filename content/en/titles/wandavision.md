---
title: "WandaVision"
oneLine: "A grieving woman puts a whole town inside a sitcom and lives there, and the show works out which decade she is in before it works out why."
tagline: "The first one, and still the strangest"
---

It is the first series Marvel Studios made, and it opens by refusing to look
like anything they had made before: black and white, laugh track, a set built
to 1950s specification. The premise takes three episodes to become legible and
it is worth the wait, because the format is the plot. Every decade the show
moves into is Wanda Maximoff holding something together for another week.

The people outside are a government agency with no idea what they are looking
at, which is exactly the position the audience is in for the first half.

:::detail{title="Where it sits"}
It is set after *Avengers: Endgame* and it is the load-bearing context for
*Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*. A viewer who skips it will find
that the later film assumes a great deal, most of it emotional rather than
factual.
:::
