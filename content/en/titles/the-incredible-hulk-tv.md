For a whole generation this was the Hulk. David Banner is officially dead, moves
from town to town taking casual work under a series of assumed surnames, and
tries not to be provoked. Each week he fails, and the transformation is played
as a private catastrophe rather than as an action beat.

The format is the western: a stranger arrives, helps somebody, and has to leave
before he is recognised. It ran for eighty episodes across five seasons on CBS.

:::detail{title="What the eighty does not include"}
The series began with a two-hour pilot broadcast on 4 November 1977, and a
second feature-length pilot followed later that month. The eighty episodes ran
from 1978 to 1982.

This entry records the first broadcast date and the eighty regular episodes,
which means the date and the count are describing different things. The
reference article has the same inconsistency, and its own two pages disagree by
a day about the second pilot's date.

The forty-nine minute runtime is the median of a stated range and does not
describe the pilots, which were feature length.
:::
