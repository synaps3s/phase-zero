It is a reboot rather than a sequel: new cast, new tone, no continuity with the
2004 film. Ray Stevenson plays Castle five years into the work, exhausted by it,
and Lexi Alexander directs the violence with a cheerfulness that the previous
two films carefully avoided.

The film is stylised on screen as *War Zone: Punisher*, and it was the first of
two films produced under the Marvel Knights banner, which was set up for exactly
this kind of smaller and harder adaptation.
