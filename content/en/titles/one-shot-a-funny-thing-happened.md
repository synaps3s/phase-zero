It is a single scene and it exists to make one point about a character the films
had been using as furniture: Phil Coulson is not the mild man he performs. He
handles two people with guns in about eight seconds and then finishes his
shopping.

The title is longer than the film.

:::detail{title="Where it came from"}
Released as a special feature on the
*Captain America: The First Avenger* Blu-ray in 2011. As with *The Consultant*,
no source this catalogue could open states a runtime, so none is recorded.
:::
