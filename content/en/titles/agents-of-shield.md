It was the first television series set in this continuity, it ran for seven
seasons and 136 episodes, and it is the entry in this catalogue that most
rewards patience. It begins as a case-of-the-week show about the people who
clean up after superheroes, and by the end it has been a spy thriller, a space
opera and a time travel comedy.

Phil Coulson leads it, which is the first thing worth knowing about it, because
he was killed in *The Avengers*.

:::detail{title="A one-way street"}
Its relationship to the films is documented, unequal and never fully resolved.
Episodes were built around *Thor: The Dark World* and
*Captain America: The Winter Soldier*, the latter forcing a retooling of the
whole series mid-season, and the show introduced both the Kree and the
Inhumans to this continuity.

The traffic never ran the other way. Joss Whedon described the process as
meaning "the TV show gets, you know, leftovers". Jed Whedon listed the early
restrictions: no spies, no Hydra, no artificial intelligence, no robots. Access
to characters was granted and withdrawn. In 2020 Chloe Bennet called the show
"the black sheep of the Marvel Universe".

And in August 2024, asked directly whether it belonged, Marvel Studios' head of
streaming praised its connections to the films, allowed that the studio could
consider it part of the Multiverse Saga, and would not confirm it. That is
where the question still stands.
:::
