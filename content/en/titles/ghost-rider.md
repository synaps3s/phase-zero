---
title: "Ghost Rider"
oneLine: "A teenager sells his soul to cure his father's cancer, the cure works, the father dies anyway, and years later the buyer comes to collect."
tagline: "The one where Nicolas Cage is on fire"
---

Johnny Blaze is seventeen in 1986 when Mephistopheles offers to cure his
father's cancer for his soul. The cancer goes. His father dies days later in a
stunt accident, and Mephistopheles considers the contract fulfilled. That is a
very cold opening for a studio film, and the movie plays it straight.

Years later the invoice arrives, and Blaze becomes the Ghost Rider, a bounty
hunter working for the thing he sold himself to.

Nicolas Cage plays Blaze, and whatever else is true about the film, that casting
is not a mistake. The character requires somebody willing to be both haunted and
ridiculous in the same shot.
