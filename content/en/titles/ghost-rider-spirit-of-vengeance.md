---

Neveldine and Taylor direct, and their handheld, over-cranked, permanently
unwell style suits a character who is on fire and does not want to be. The
premise is simple: protect the child, get the curse lifted, and find out what
the offer is actually worth.

It is a reboot in tone if not in cast, since Nicolas Cage returns while almost
everything else about the first film is discarded.

:::detail{title="Which year is it from"}
Reference works file it as a 2011 film, and its own article does the same. It
was shown publicly for one night, on 11 December 2011, and did not open widely
until 17 February 2012.

This catalogue records 2012, because the release date field here means the
general release rather than the first time an audience saw it. If you are
comparing this entry against a list that says 2011, that is the reason, and
neither of you is wrong.
:::
