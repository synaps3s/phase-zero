The first hour is the unusual part: a film about defeat, grief and the shapes people take when they stop trying. The plan does not begin until the survivors have been shown living with having failed.

It is the end of the run that started in 2008, and it assumes all of it. Watching it without the films before it is technically possible and mostly pointless.

:::detail{title="It came back, with four minutes that did not exist"}
*Endgame* has been re-released twice, and the second time matters.

In 2019 it returned with seven minutes after the credits: a tribute to Stan
Lee, an unfinished deleted scene, and the opening of *Spider-Man: Far From
Home*. Nothing in it changed the film.

On 25 September 2026 it returns as *Avengers Endgame: Encore*, with a custom
introduction, new footage and a new post-credits scene, about four minutes in
all. Joe Russo called that re-release "critically important" and said he
believed it was "required viewing" to follow the story of *Avengers: Doomsday*.
The new material plays in the IMAX and Infinity Vision screenings, which also
carry a further look at *Doomsday*.

This catalogue keeps one entry per title rather than one per cut, so the
runtime above is the film as it was released. The four minutes are not counted
in it.
:::
