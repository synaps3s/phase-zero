title: "Fantastic Four"
oneLine: "A group of young scientists build a machine that opens onto another dimension, use it on themselves without permission, and come back wrong."
tagline: "The one that came apart"
---

The reboot takes the team's origin somewhere genuinely different. There is no
space station and no cosmic storm. There is a transdimensional gate, built by
teenagers, tested by them at night without authorisation, and the place on the
other side changes what comes back. For about forty minutes it plays as a body
horror film about a laboratory, and it is good.

Then it stops. What is on screen after that point is the result of a production
that had turned into an argument.

:::detail{title="Stylised, and heavily rebuilt"}
It is stylised on screen as *Fant4stic*, in capitals. Reshoots were ordered in
January 2015, and the cut that was released was assembled by Stephen Rivkin
rather than by the director Josh Trank.

Trank said in 2020 that a great deal of what he had planned was never filmed at
all, which makes a director's cut, in his words, practically nonexistent. The
running time recorded here, a hundred minutes, is the only one that exists.
:::
