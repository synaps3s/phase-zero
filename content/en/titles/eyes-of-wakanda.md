The Hatut Zaraze are Wakanda's operatives abroad, and each of the four episodes
follows a different one in a different era. It is the first thing in this
catalogue to use Wakanda's isolation as a premise for historical adventure
rather than as a political argument, and the change of register works.

:::detail{title="Animated, and inside the continuity"}
Reference material makes a point of this one: it is set in the Sacred Timeline
with closer connections to the films than the studio's other animated series,
which are mostly set outside it.

That is why this entry is not flagged as an alternate universe and *What If...?*
is.
:::
