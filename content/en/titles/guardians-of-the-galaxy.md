title: Guardians of the Galaxy
oneLine: A thief, an assassin, a talking raccoon, a tree and a man who takes
  everything literally are thrown together in prison and end up saving a planet.
tagline: The film that proved this material did not need Earth
---

Nothing before this had gone this far from home. The gamble was whether an
audience would follow a cast of people who had never appeared on screen before
to a galaxy with its own politics, and the answer was that they would, if the
film was funny and meant it.

The trick is that everyone in the group is broken in a specific way and none of
them wants to admit it. The comedy comes from the avoidance, and the film earns
its ending by finally letting them stop.

:::detail{title="What it carries"}
The Power Stone is here, and so is the first proper look at the cosmic side of
this universe: its empires, its collectors, its enforcers. Films four years
later assume you know how that side works.
:::
