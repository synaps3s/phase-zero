---
title: "Moon Knight"
oneLine: "A gift shop worker with dissociative identity disorder keeps waking up somewhere else, and one of the people he shares a body with is working for an Egyptian god."
tagline: "The one with almost no continuity in it"
---

Steven Grant sells souvenirs in a museum and ties himself to his bed at night
because he loses time. Marc Spector is a mercenary in service to Khonshu. They
are the same man, and the series is told mostly from inside the one who does not
know that.

It is the least connected entry Marvel Studios had made at that point, and the
freest. Nobody from anywhere else turns up. If you want somewhere to start that
requires nothing, this is the strongest candidate in the whole catalogue.
