title: "The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special"
oneLine: "Mantis and Drax go to Earth to abduct Kevin Bacon as a Christmas present, having taken Peter Quill's childhood stories about him completely literally."
tagline: "The Christmas one"
---

Forty-odd minutes of two aliens loose in Los Angeles under the impression that
they are doing something kind. The joke works because it is played straight from
their side: Quill told them about a legendary hero from his childhood, and they
have come to get him.

It is the second Special Presentation and the stated conclusion of Phase Four,
which is a strange job for a Christmas comedy and one it carries better than it
should.

:::detail{title="Two runtimes"}
