title: "Blade II"
oneLine: "A new kind of vampire is eating the old kind, and the hunter has to work with the people he has spent his life killing."
tagline: "The one directed by a monster maker"
---

Guillermo del Toro directs, and it shows in the first minute. The Reapers are a
mutation that feeds on vampires as readily as on people, which forces the
vampire nation to hire the man who has been exterminating them, and the film
treats that alliance as a practical arrangement between professionals rather
than as a moral turn.

It is the entry in this trilogy that most resembles a creature feature. The
design work is genuinely unpleasant in the way good horror design is, and the
film is far more interested in what the Reapers are than in what Blade feels.
