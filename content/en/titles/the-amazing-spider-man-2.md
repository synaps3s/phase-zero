oneLine: "Peter Parker keeps a promise he should not have made, finds out what happened to his parents, and meets an electrical engineer nobody has ever looked at properly."

---

It carries three films' worth of setup: Gwen Stacy and a promise made to her
dying father, the mystery of Richard and Mary Parker, Harry Osborn arriving to
collect on a friendship, and Max Dillon becoming Electro after a lifetime of
being ignored. The film is visibly straining to launch a shared universe that
was cancelled shortly afterwards.

Underneath the machinery, Electro is one of the better ideas in these films. He
is not evil. He is somebody nobody thanked, given the power to be noticed.

:::detail{title="The universe that was not built"}
Sony had announced a set of connected films around this one. They did not
happen. The series stopped here, and the character returned only years later
through an arrangement with another studio.

Internationally the film carries the subtitle *Rise of Electro*, which is also
the shape of its Italian title.
:::
