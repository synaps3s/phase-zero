It is the third Marvel Special Presentation, a format the studio uses for
single, short pieces that stand outside the film and series schedule. Jon
Bernthal wrote it with Reinaldo Marcus Green, who directed, and it is the second
production centred on the character after the Netflix series.

That is a notable line to be able to draw. The same actor now anchors an entry
made by the previous television company and one made by Marvel Studios, which
is the clearest single piece of evidence that the two eras were joined.
