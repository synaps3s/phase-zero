---
title: "Item 47"
oneLine: "A broke couple find a Chitauri gun left in the rubble after the Battle of New York and start robbing banks with it."
tagline: "The one about the litter"
---

Twelve minutes, and the best idea any of the shorts had. An alien invasion has
been repelled and the clean-up is not perfect, and two people with no money find
a working weapon in the debris.

What makes it work is the ending. S.H.I.E.L.D. sends two agents to recover the
item, and what they do when they find the couple is not what the genre trains
you to expect.
