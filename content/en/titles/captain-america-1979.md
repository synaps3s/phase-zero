A television film in which Steve Rogers is a contemporary man, the serum is
called FLAG, the shield doubles as a windscreen on a motorcycle, and the plot
concerns a neutron bomb. The wartime Captain America is his father.

It is a long way from the character as this catalogue otherwise records him, and
that distance is the reason to know it exists.
