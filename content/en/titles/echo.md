---
title: "Echo"
oneLine: "The woman who shot Wilson Fisk goes home to Oklahoma, where the family she abandoned is still there and so is what she did to them."
tagline: "The one that goes home"
---

Maya Lopez leaves New York for the Choctaw Nation town she grew up in, and the
series is largely about whether a person who has been made into a weapon by
somebody else's grief can be taken back by her own people.

It is the first entry released under the Marvel Spotlight banner, which is the
studio's way of saying that this one does not require the rest. It is also the
point at which the Netflix-era series were formally folded into the official
timeline.
