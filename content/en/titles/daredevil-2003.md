title: "Daredevil"
oneLine: "A blind lawyer loses in court during the day and wins in the street at night, in a city that has already decided which of those it prefers."
tagline: "The first attempt at the man without fear"
---

Matt Murdock is blinded as a child, in 1982, by radioactive waste that leaves
every other sense sharpened past human range, and the film's best sequences are the ones
that try to show what that is actually like. His day job is a legal practice
that mostly loses. His night job is the reason the losses are bearable.

It was made when the modern run of comic book films was still finding its feet,
between the first *X-Men* and the first *Iron Man*, and it carries the marks of
that period: a hard-rock soundtrack, a leather costume, and a certainty that
darkness is the same thing as maturity.

:::detail{title="Two versions"}
A director's cut was released in 2004, rated R, restoring roughly thirty minutes
including a subplot that changes the shape of the film considerably. This
catalogue records the theatrical running time, which is what the reference
infobox states.
:::
