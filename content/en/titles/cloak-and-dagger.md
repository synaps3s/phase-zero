title: "Cloak & Dagger"
oneLine: "Two teenagers from opposite ends of New Orleans, connected by the same night years earlier, discover abilities that only work properly when they are together."
tagline: "The one about a city"
---

Tandy Bowen and Tyrone Johnson lost everything on the same night for different
reasons, and the series is about the two of them circling each other across a
city that has kept them apart by design. Her power is light and his is
darkness, which sounds schematic and plays as something much quieter: she can
see what people hope for, he can see what they fear.

Two seasons, twenty episodes, and one of the few entries here that uses its
setting as an argument rather than as a backdrop.
