The second television film, and the more interesting of the three, because it is
the first appearance of Daredevil and of Wilson Fisk in live action, thirty-five
years before the versions this catalogue records elsewhere.

Fisk runs the city and is never called Kingpin on screen. It was also intended
as a pilot, this time for a Daredevil series, which also did not happen.

:::detail{title="The title is a lie"}
Nobody puts the Hulk on trial. The writer has said a trial of the creature was
never even discussed.

The trial in the film is Banner's, and it is over quickly. The title was doing
marketing rather than describing the film.
:::
