title: "The Amazing Spider-Man"
oneLine: "A university student bitten by a radioactive spider gets a job photographing himself for a newspaper, and fights crime in a city with a very limited effects budget."
tagline: "The 1977 one"
---

Thirteen episodes across two short seasons on CBS, and the first American
live-action Spider-Man of any length. Peter Parker is a graduate student, the
Daily Bugle is a real newsroom, and the wall-crawling is done with wires and
patience.

It is slow and it is not trying to be a comic book, which is the interesting
part: it plays as a detective series in which the detective can climb.

:::detail{title="What the thirteen includes"}
The count includes the pilot, which was a ninety-minute film. The finale is
counted as two episodes that both aired on the same night in July 1979.

The forty-three minute runtime is the median of a stated range and does not
describe either the pilot or the finale.
:::
