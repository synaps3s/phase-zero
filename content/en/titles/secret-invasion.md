tagline: "The paranoid one"
---

The Skrulls can be anyone. Fury made them a promise thirty years ago that he
did not keep, and a faction of them has run out of patience. The series is a
conspiracy thriller in which the threat is that no conversation can be trusted,
including the ones the audience has already watched.

It is also the closest the catalogue comes to a story about Fury himself: what
he owes, to whom, and what happens to a man whose entire method is knowing more
than the person opposite.
