Black Bolt cannot speak without destroying what he speaks at, which is a strong
idea, and the series is largely about his brother deciding that a monarchy
based on a genetic lottery should not exist. The argument is better than the
show around it.

It was received poorly and cancelled after eight episodes, and it is the entry
in this catalogue that most clearly shows what happened when a film was
demoted into a series.

:::detail{title="It was a film first, and then a cinema release anyway"}
Marvel Studios announced an *Inhumans* film in 2014 as part of its Phase Three
slate, then removed it. The species had already been introduced to this
continuity through *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*

What arrived instead had an unusual release: the first two episodes premiered
in Los Angeles on 28 August 2017 and then played on 667 IMAX screens in 67
countries from 1 September, co-financed by IMAX, a month before the series
began on television. The broadcast versions of those two episodes carry nine
minutes the cinema cut did not.

This catalogue records the broadcast date, which is the one the reference
infobox gives, and puts the earlier one here.
:::
