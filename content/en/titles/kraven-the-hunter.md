title: "Kraven the Hunter"
oneLine: "A boy is mauled on a hunting trip with his father, survives it changed, and grows into the most effective predator on the planet."
tagline: "The one about a father"
---

Sergei Kravinoff's story is about inheritance. His father takes him hunting, the
trip goes wrong, and what he becomes afterwards is a rejection of that man
carried out with exactly the skills that man taught him. The film is more
interested in that relationship than in anything costumed.

It is the sixth film in Sony's Spider-Man Universe, and one of two released in
2024, which is a good indication of how quickly that project was being assembled
by the end.

:::detail{title="What this catalogue will not say about it"}
Whether this is the last film of its series is not established. The article this
entry is drawn from carries a note from its own editors asking contributors not
to call it final, because the franchise has not been declared over.

That is exactly the kind of claim this catalogue leaves alone until a source
makes it. The films released so far are recorded, and the ending is not
predicted.
:::
