---

Krista Starr comes home from Iraq to a brother who has been murdered, gets
turned by the man responsible, and is recruited by Blade to work the inside of
the House of Chthon. It is a police procedural about infiltration, with the
title character running an asset rather than being the lead of every scene.

Thirteen episodes, one season, following on from the film trilogy that is also
in this catalogue.
