---
title: "Madame Web"
oneLine: "A New York paramedic starts seeing a few seconds into the future, and finds a man who has been seeing much further for much longer."
tagline: "The one that is a thriller in a superhero costume"
---

Cassie Webb does not want powers and spends most of the film treating them as a
symptom. What she can do is see slightly ahead, badly and involuntarily, which
is a much smaller ability than these films usually hand out and a far more
interesting one to build a chase around.

The threat is Ezekiel Sims, who has the same gift with a longer range, and who
is trying to kill three young women because of what he has seen them become. The
film is the fourth in Sony's Spider-Man Universe and the one furthest from
anything the label implies.

:::detail{title="Set in 2003, and not by accident"}
The 2003 setting was in the script from the start. The director wanted a
timeless quality and built it out of music running from the 1990s to the edge of
that year and vintage clothing, and it also lets the film be a story about young
women who cannot simply text each other.

To be plain about it: critics panned it as one of the worst comic book films
ever made, and it lost money. That is the record, and it sits here next to the
premise rather than replacing it.
:::
