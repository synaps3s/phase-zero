title: Venom
oneLine: A disgraced journalist is taken over by an alien that eats people, and
  the two of them come to an arrangement.
tagline: A buddy film wearing a monster's face
---

The film is less interested in being frightening than in the comedy of a man
sharing a body with something that talks back and has opinions about his life.
That is what its audience came for, and the film knows it.

It sits in yet another separate continuity, one built around Spider-Man's
supporting characters without Spider-Man in it, which is an odd premise the
films never entirely resolve.

:::detail{title="On the missing Spider-Man"}
This continuity exists because of how film rights were divided decades ago. The
character these films orbit belongs, on screen, somewhere else, so the stories
are built around an absence.

It is worth knowing before you start, because otherwise the shape of these films
is hard to explain.
:::
