---
title: "All Hail the King"
oneLine: "A documentary crew interviews Trevor Slattery in prison about pretending to be the Mandarin, and the interviewer turns out to have another reason for being there."
tagline: "The one that undoes a twist"
---

Fourteen minutes of a failed actor enjoying prison celebrity, and then a
reversal: the man asking the questions is from the real Ten Rings, and he is
there because the real Mandarin is offended.

It exists to answer the loudest complaint about *Iron Man 3*, and it does it
without contradicting the film. Ten years later the thread it leaves hanging is
picked up in a feature.
