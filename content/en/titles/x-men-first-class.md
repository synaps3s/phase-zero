title: "X-Men: First Class"
oneLine: "In 1962, two young men on opposite ends of the same argument build a school and a war between them, against the background of the Cuban Missile Crisis."
tagline: "The one that starts the whole thing again"
---

It resets the series by going back rather than forward. Charles Xavier and Erik
Lehnsherr meet as young men, recruit together, fight together, and separate over
a disagreement neither of them ever wins, and the film puts all of it inside
thirteen days of real history that the audience already knows the ending to.

The period setting does most of the work. A story about a hidden minority being
recruited by governments who fear them lands harder in 1962 than it does in the
present, and the film is confident enough to let the political thriller carry
the superhero plot rather than the other way round.

:::detail{title="Stylised without the X-Men"}
The on-screen title card reads *X: First Class*. It is the fifth film of the
series and the first of the four prequels, and it is also the point at which the
series stops being a continuous sequence and starts being two casts playing the
same people at different ages.
:::
