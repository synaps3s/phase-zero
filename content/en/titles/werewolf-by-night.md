title: "Werewolf by Night"
oneLine: "Five monster hunters are summoned to a manor after the death of its master, to compete for a relic by hunting a monster in the grounds."
tagline: "The black and white one"
---

Fifty-three minutes, shot to look like a 1940s horror picture down to the grain
and the cigarette burns, and almost entirely uninterested in the rest of the
catalogue. Jack Russell is there to free the creature rather than kill it, and
the hunt goes wrong in the way you want it to.

It is the first Marvel Studios Special Presentation, a format the studio
introduced for exactly this: a single piece, an hour long, that requires nothing
and sets up nothing.
