title: "She-Hulk: Attorney at Law"
oneLine: "A lawyer who gets her cousin's blood in a cut inherits his condition, is better at controlling it than he ever was, and would very much like to keep her job."
tagline: "The one that argues with the audience"
---

Jennifer Walters is good at law and is now large, green and famous, which are
three things her career did not need. The series is a half-hour comedy about a
legal practice for superhumans, and its best episodes are the ones where the
case is small and the client is ridiculous.

It also talks to camera, argues with its own genre, and ends by breaking
something that most shows would not dare touch. Reaction to it split hard along
exactly that line.
