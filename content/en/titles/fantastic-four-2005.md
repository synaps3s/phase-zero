title: "Fantastic Four"
oneLine: "Four people and the man funding them are caught in a cosmic storm in orbit, and all five come back able to do something none of them asked for."
tagline: "The bright one"
---

It is the least cynical film in this part of the catalogue. Reed Richards,
Sue and Johnny Storm, Ben Grimm and Victor von Doom take a trip to a space
station, get hit by a cloud of cosmic rays, and come home changed, and the film
spends most of its length on the ordinary business of finding out what that
means before it remembers it needs a villain.

Ben Grimm is where it lands. The others get abilities they can hide. He gets a
body he cannot leave, and the film gives him the only genuinely sad material in
it.

:::detail{title="Two things worth knowing"}
Johnny Storm is played by Chris Evans, six years before he became Captain
America. The catalogue keeps both, in different universes, because both are
real.

This was also the second live-action Fantastic Four film to be made, not the
first. Roger Corman produced one in 1994, titled *The Fantastic Four*, which was
never released and is therefore not in this catalogue: there is nothing to point
a reader at.
:::
