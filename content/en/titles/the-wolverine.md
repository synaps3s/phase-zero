It picks up directly after *X-Men: The Last Stand*, with Logan in the worst
state the series has shown him: living rough, refusing to be anyone, and
haunted by Jean Grey. An old acquaintance in Japan calls in a debt, and the film
turns into something the series had not attempted, which is a smaller story with
one location and a mortal protagonist.

Stripping the healing factor is the whole idea. Every fight after that point
costs him something that will not grow back by the next scene, and the film is
better whenever it remembers that.

:::detail{title="A running time worth arguing about"}
An unrated extended edition exists. The article this entry is drawn from says in
one sentence that it contains twelve extra minutes, and in the next that the
British Board of Film Classification timed it at 132 minutes and 22 seconds,
"only six minutes longer" than the theatrical cut.

Both cannot be true against a 126 minute theatrical running time. This catalogue
records the theatrical figure, which is the one the infobox gives, and leaves the
contradiction visible rather than quietly picking a side.
:::
