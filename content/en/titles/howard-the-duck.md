It is a comedy, it is very strange, and it was executive produced by George
Lucas. Howard is an ill-tempered anthropomorphic duck yanked across dimensions
into Ohio, where he acquires a band, a job at a hot tub emporium, and a demonic
problem.

It matters to this catalogue for a reason that has nothing to do with quality.
It was the first theatrical Marvel film since the *Captain America* serial of
1944, and its commercial failure is a large part of why the next serious attempt
took more than a decade.

:::detail{title="It was supposed to be animated"}
The screenplay was written for animation. The film became live action because
of a contractual obligation, which is how a talking duck ended up being played
by a person in a suit rather than drawn.

It was released in the United Kingdom under a different title,
*Howard... A New Breed of Hero*, which is a sentence that tells you a great deal
about how the marketing was going.
:::
