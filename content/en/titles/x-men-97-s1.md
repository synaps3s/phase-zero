---

It is not a reboot and not a homage. It resumes *X-Men: The Animated Series*
where that show stopped, with the team running the school without its founder
and Magneto arriving to claim a place in it, and it treats thirty years of
absence as a gap in the story rather than as a reason to start over.

Ten episodes, Mister Sinister, Bastion and a new Sentinel programme, and an
ending that scatters the team through time.

:::detail{title="Where it sits"}
It belongs to the continuity of the 1992 cartoon, which is not the Fox film
continuity and not the connected run. This catalogue files it as unbound for
that reason.

It is also, by some distance, the best received thing in this part of the
catalogue.
:::
