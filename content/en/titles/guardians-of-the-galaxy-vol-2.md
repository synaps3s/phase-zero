tagline: "About fathers, and which ones count"
---

It is a sequel that refuses to repeat the first film's shape. The group splits up early, spends most of the runtime in separate arguments, and the plot is almost entirely a family one.

The other father in it is the one who was there, and the film's real move is to spend two hours making you understand that before it says so out loud.
