---

Broadcast ten months after the first, with the same lead and a larger plot: a
freelance terrorist, a kidnapped gerontologist, and Portland threatened with
accelerated ageing.

Two television films in one year, and then nothing for eleven years, when the
character got a cinema release that almost nobody saw either.
