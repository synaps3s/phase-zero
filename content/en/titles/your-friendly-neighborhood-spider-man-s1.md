The origin again, changed in one decisive way. A temporal paradox caused by
Doctor Strange gives Peter his abilities early, and the mentor who arrives to
fund and guide him is not Tony Stark. It is Norman Osborn, which the audience
understands and Peter does not.

Ten episodes, and the tension is entirely in that gap.

:::detail{title="A different timeline on purpose"}
The series is set in an alternate timeline from the films and the other series,
which its own article states outright, and this entry is flagged accordingly.

It is not a continuity error to be reconciled. It is the premise.
:::
