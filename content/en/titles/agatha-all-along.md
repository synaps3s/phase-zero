---
title: "Agatha All Along"
oneLine: "The witch from Westview is released from the spell she was left under and has to walk a road that kills most of the people who try it."
tagline: "The one about survival as a personality"
---

Agatha Harkness spent *WandaVision* pretending to be a neighbour, and was left
at the end of it locked inside that pretence. The series opens with the spell
breaking and follows her assembling a coven for the Witches' Road, a trial that
grants what you need at the end and takes something from you at every step.

It is a horror comedy with real cruelty in it, and it is honest about its
protagonist: Agatha is not misunderstood. She is exactly what everybody says
she is, and she survives everything.
