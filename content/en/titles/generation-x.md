A television film from 1996, four years before the first X-Men film, with
Jubilee and Skin arriving at a school run by two teachers the later films would
not get around to for a decade or more.

It is not good and it is genuinely interesting, because it shows what this
material looked like when a television budget was all anybody would risk on it.

:::detail{title="What is missing from it"}
Xavier is not in it. Neither is anybody from the team the films would build
around. The school exists, the premise exists, and everything that made the
concept expensive has been engineered out.

That is worth seeing once if you want to understand why the 2000 film was
treated as a surprise.
:::
