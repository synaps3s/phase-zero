---
title: "The Punisher"
oneLine: "An undercover agent's entire extended family is murdered at a reunion on the orders of one man, and he spends the rest of the film dismantling that man's life instead of simply shooting him."
tagline: "The one about patience"
---

Thomas Jane plays Frank Castle as somebody methodical rather than furious. The
film's best decision is that he does not go straight at Howard Saint. He moves
into a derelict apartment block occupied by three outcasts and takes the man
apart by studying his family until he knows their every move, which is a colder
idea than a shoot-out and a much better one.

Those three neighbours are the only warmth the film allows itself, and it makes
them pay for it.

It draws on two specific comic runs, *The Punisher: Year One* and Garth Ennis'
*Welcome Back, Frank*, and it is the only film here that reads as an adaptation
of a tone rather than of a plot.
