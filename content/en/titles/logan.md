---
title: Logan
oneLine: An old, dying Wolverine drives a limousine and looks after the man who
  once led the X-Men, until a child he did not ask for arrives.
tagline: The one that ends a twenty-year run by refusing to be a superhero film
---

There are no costumes and no team. There is a hangover, a failing body, a
caretaker relationship going badly, and a road trip that the film treats as a
western rather than as an adventure.

It is the last appearance of a performance that ran for seventeen years, and it
earns its ending by being about decline rather than about victory.

:::detail{title="Why it sits outside the main story"}
Logan belongs to the Fox X-Men films, made by a different studio under a
separate licence, which is why none of these characters appear in the connected
films of the same period. The two bodies of work only touch much later, through
the multiverse.

Watching it requires nothing else. It is one of the few films here that works
completely on its own.
:::
