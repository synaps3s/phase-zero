---
title: "The New Mutants"
oneLine: "Five teenagers with powers they cannot control are held in a facility that says it is treating them, and the treatment is the problem."
tagline: "The horror film at the end of the series"
---

It is a haunted house film with mutants in it. Five young people are locked in a
secret hospital, told they are dangerous, and made to relive the worst thing
each of them has ever done, and for once the genre furniture is not the point.
The point is that being told what you are by an institution is its own kind of
injury.

It is a spin-off, the thirteenth and final film of this continuity, and it
arrived after its studio had already been bought by the one that owns everything
else in this catalogue.

:::detail{title="Three years in a drawer"}
Filming finished in September 2017. The film was originally dated for April
2018, moved repeatedly, and did not reach cinemas until August 2020, by which
point 20th Century Fox no longer existed as an independent studio and the series
it closes had already been superseded.
:::
