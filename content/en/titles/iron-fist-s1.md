title: "Iron Fist"
tagline: "Season one"
oneLine: "A man presumed dead for fifteen years walks back into New York barefoot, claims to own a corporation, and says he is the sworn protector of a city nobody has heard of."
---

Danny Rand returns from a place he cannot prove exists, to a company that has
moved on and a family that buried him. The season splits between a boardroom
fight over the corporation and a duty he took on somewhere else, and it never
quite decides which show it is.

It is the least well received of the Netflix run by a distance, and it is worth
knowing that before starting it rather than after.
