Released before the modern run of Marvel films began, and made without any
intention of connecting to anything, this is an R-rated horror action film that
happens to be based on a Marvel character.

It works because it commits: the world is grim, the rules of it are explained
once and then obeyed, and the lead is unbothered by the audience's approval.

:::detail{title="Why it is in this guide at all"}
It belongs to no shared continuity, and for most of its life that was the end of
the story. It is here because the character has since been announced for the
connected films, and because a guide that only covers the connected films cannot
explain where these characters came from.

Entries like this are marked as outside the shared continuity rather than left
out, which is a deliberate choice: their separateness is itself information.
:::
