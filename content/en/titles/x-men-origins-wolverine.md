---

It goes back to James Howlett as a child, through a century of wars alongside
his half-brother Victor Creed, into William Stryker's Team X, and finally into
the Weapon X programme where adamantium is bonded to his bones. On paper that is
the most requested backstory in the series.

The production was troubled: weather delays, an incomplete cut leaking online
before release, and a version of Deadpool that its own eventual film would spend
a scene disowning. It is the least loved entry here and the hardest to skip,
because so much of what comes later assumes you saw it.

:::detail{title="The Deadpool problem"}
Ryan Reynolds plays Wade Wilson here, and by the third act the character has his
mouth sewn shut. When Reynolds returned to the role in *Deadpool* in 2016, the
new film treated that version as a mistake to be mocked rather than a history to
honour, which is why reference works can accurately say he was reprising the
role while the film itself refuses to accept it.
:::
