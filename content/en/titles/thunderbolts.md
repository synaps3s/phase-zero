title: "Thunderbolts"
oneLine: "A group of people who have all done something unforgivable are put in a room by somebody who expects them to kill each other."
tagline: "The team film about not being a hero"
---

Nobody in it is an Avenger and most of them have been an antagonist. The film is less interested in whether they can win than in whether any of them can stand being themselves.

It is the closest this run has come to a film about depression, and the strangeness of that arriving inside a team-up is most of what makes it work.
