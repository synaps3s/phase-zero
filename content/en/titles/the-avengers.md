---
title: The Avengers
oneLine: The five films before this one were an argument that these people would
  never work together, and this is the film where they have to.
tagline: The payoff that made the format
---

Nothing quite like this had been attempted: five films with separate tones,
separate directors and separate audiences, converging on one story without any
of them being reduced to a cameo in it.

The film knows that the fight is not the interesting part. The interesting part
is the middle hour, where the team is assembled, confined to one flying room,
and allowed to be insufferable to each other. Almost everything the next decade
does with these characters is set up in that hour.

:::detail{title="Why this changed how films get made"}
This is the moment the shared universe stopped being a promise in a
post-credits scene and became a commercial fact. Nearly every studio attempt at
a connected series since has been measured against it, and most of them have
tried to arrive at this film without making the five that earn it.

That order is the whole trick. It is also why this guide keeps insisting on
release order for a first watch.
:::
