---

The sequel builds a bureaucracy out of the multiverse. Miguel O'Hara runs the
Spider-Society, which polices canon events, moments that must happen in every
Spider-Man's life for their world to hold together. One of Miles' is his
father's death, and he refuses.

It is a chase film in which the person being chased is right, and it ends
without resolving, which was announced and still landed hard.

:::detail{title="Which universes, and which franchise"}
This film names them: Miles is from Earth-1610, Gwen Stacy from Earth-65,
Miguel from Earth-928, and the Marvel Cinematic Universe is referred to as
Earth-199999 in a joke about *Spider-Man: No Way Home*.

It also identifies the universe of the Venom films, which this catalogue records
separately, as Earth-688. That is a visit rather than a merger: reference works
list the Sony live-action films as their own six-film series and file these
animated ones as a separate one, which is why this catalogue gives them their
own franchise.
:::
