title: "Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings"
oneLine: "A man who has spent ten years parking cars in San Francisco is found by the father he ran away from."
tagline: "The best fights in the run, and a family argument underneath them"
---

The action is choreographed rather than assembled, staged in long takes on a bus and a scaffold, and it is the clearest the fighting has ever been in one of these films.

Underneath it is a story about a father who cannot accept a death and two children who were raised differently by the same grief. The antagonist wants his wife back, which is a motive the film treats as sad rather than stupid.
