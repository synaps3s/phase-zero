---
title: "Thor: The Dark World"
oneLine: A force that predates light itself returns to undo the universe, and
  Thor has to take his brother out of prison to stop it.
tagline: The one everybody forgets, carrying something important
---

The plot is the least memorable in this run and the film knows what it is
doing when it stops being about the plot. The half worth watching is the pair
of brothers, one of whom has just tried to conquer Earth, forced to work
together and unable to be honest for a single scene.

Everything else is scaffolding. It is competent scaffolding, and the film moves.

:::detail{title="Why it is still in the essential run"}
Two things pass through here that later films depend on: an Infinity Stone,
and a change to the relationship between Thor and Loki that the next several
films assume you have seen.

Skipping it is survivable. You will feel the gap twice.
:::
