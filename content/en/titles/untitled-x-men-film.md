It has a release date, 5 May 2028, and nothing else that can be sourced. No
title, no cast list worth printing, no reported filming.

It matters to this catalogue anyway, because it is the point where the two
largest bodies of work here stop being separate. The Fox X-Men films are
thirteen entries in a different universe. This one is not.

:::detail{title="The phase is not official"}
Reference material describes this film as expected to be part of Phase Seven
rather than announced as part of it. The number is recorded here because the
source names it and files the film under that heading, but Marvel has not
designated a phase.
:::
