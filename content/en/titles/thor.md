title: Thor
oneLine: An arrogant prince is stripped of his power and exiled to Earth until he
  learns to deserve it back.
tagline: The film that made the impossible parts possible
---

Until this point the run had been technology and radiation: things that could be
argued into plausibility. Thor is the film that asks the audience to accept
gods, a golden city, and a rainbow bridge, and it manages it by treating the
fantastical parts as normal and the ordinary parts as strange.

The exile does the work. Stripped of the hammer, Thor spends the film as a large
confident man being handled by a small New Mexico town, and the comedy of that
is what makes the mythology go down.

:::detail{title="The sleight of hand"}
The script solves the tone problem by declaring that this world's magic is
simply science the audience has not learned yet. It is a line of dialogue, not
an explanation, and it works because nobody in the film treats it as remarkable.

The brother is the other reason it works. Loki is given a real grievance and a
coherent plan, which is rarer in this material than it sounds.
:::
