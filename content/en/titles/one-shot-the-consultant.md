---
title: "The Consultant"
oneLine: "Two agents are ordered to get Emil Blonsky released into the Avengers Initiative, do not want to, and send Tony Stark to make sure the request fails."
tagline: "The first one"
---

Four minutes of two men in a diner working out how to disobey an order without
disobeying it. The World Security Council wants the Abomination on the team.
Coulson and Sitwell cannot refuse, so they send the one person guaranteed to
make General Ross say no.

It is the earliest of the five short films, and it does something the features
rarely have room for: it shows the machinery of the agency as a workplace, with
people managing upwards.

:::detail{title="How these were released"}
None of the five One-Shots had a cinema release. This one arrived as a special
feature on the *Thor* Blu-ray in 2011.

Its runtime is not stated by any source this catalogue could open. The article
says the five range from three to fifteen minutes and that the earlier ones were
no longer than four, which is why this entry records no number rather than an
estimate.
:::
