title: "How long does this take?"
order: 40
topic: starting
---

The timeline counts it for you, and the number changes with the level you pick
and with what you have already marked as watched.

Roughly: the films alone are about eighty hours. Films and the series that carry
the main story are about a hundred and sixty. Everything, including the older
television and the other studios' films, is closer to eight hundred.

At two hours a night, the middle number is about eleven weeks.
