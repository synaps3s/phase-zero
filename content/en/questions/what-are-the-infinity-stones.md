---
title: "What are the Infinity Stones?"
order: 140
topic: story
---

Six objects, each controlling one fundamental aspect of reality: space, mind,
reality, power, time and soul. Any single one makes its holder the most
dangerous thing in a solar system. All six together let one person do anything
at all, once.

The clever part is the pacing. They are introduced one at a time across a decade
in films that appear to be about something else entirely, so the audience is
usually several steps ahead of the characters, and the story that collects them
does not need to explain what they are.

They also have a beginning and an end. By the time the Infinity Saga closes,
they have been destroyed.
