No, and almost nobody has. The catalogue is a record of what exists, not a list
of homework.

The timeline has three levels for exactly this. **Films only** is the cinema run
on its own, which is the spine and about eighty hours. **Plus series** adds the
television that carries the main story, which since 2021 is not really optional,
and takes it to about a hundred and sixty. **Plus everything else** adds the
shorts, the specials, the older television and the films made by other studios,
and that is where the number stops being something a person does in a year.

If you want one answer: plus series, in release order. That is the whole story
with nothing important missing.
