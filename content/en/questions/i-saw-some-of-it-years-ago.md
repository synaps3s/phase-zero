Mark what you remember in the timeline, and the list will show you where the
gap starts. The tracking is a checkbox on every row and it stays in your
browser.

If you stopped after *Avengers: Endgame*, which is where most people did, the
honest answer is that the thing you come back to is not the same shape. The
single line became several, television became part of the plot, and the story
stopped building toward one ending.

Start with *WandaVision* and *Loki*. Between them they explain what changed,
and almost everything after leans on both.
