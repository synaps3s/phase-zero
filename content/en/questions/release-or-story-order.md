---
title: "Release order or story order?"
order: 30
topic: starting
---

Release order, the first time. Every one of these films was made assuming you
had seen the ones before it and not the ones after, and the surprises are built
on that. Story order hands you several of them early.

The clearest example is *Captain Marvel*. It is set in 1995, so story order puts
it first, which means meeting a character whose entire introduction depends on
you already caring about the people she is coming back for.

Story order is genuinely interesting on a rewatch, when nothing can be spoiled
and the shape of the history becomes the point. The timeline switches between
the two with one control.
