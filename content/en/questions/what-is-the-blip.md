---
title: "What is the Snap, or the Blip, that everything refers to?"
order: 130
topic: story
---

The event at the centre of *Avengers: Infinity War*. The antagonist collects six
objects, uses them, and half of every living thing in the universe disappears at
random. The film ends there, without undoing it.

It lasts five years inside the story. For the people left behind those five
years pass normally, with funerals and support groups and a world that gets
smaller. For the people who disappeared, no time passes at all: they come back
in *Avengers: Endgame* exactly as they were, into a world that has moved on
without them.

That gap is where most of the emotional material in the years afterwards comes
from. A teenager comes back to find his classmates are five years older than he
is.
