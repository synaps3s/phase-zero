---

*Iron Man*, from 2008. It is the film this whole thing was built out of, it
assumes nothing, and it is a self-contained story about an arms manufacturer who
changes his mind. If you watch it and do not want more, you have not been left
holding anything unfinished.

There is a case for starting elsewhere. *Captain Marvel* is set in 1995 and
comes first in story order. *Guardians of the Galaxy* requires nothing and is
the least like homework. *Moon Knight* is connected to almost nothing at all.
Any of those work.

What does not work is starting at *Avengers: Infinity War* because somebody told
you it is the good one. It is, and it is the payoff for eighteen films.
