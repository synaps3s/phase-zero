title: "Why is this title hidden, or right at the bottom of the list?"
order: 240
topic: catalogue
---

Three different reasons, and the row will tell you which.

If it is not in the list at all, the second control is filtering it out. The
Marvel Television era, the shorts, the older television and the other studios'
films all sit at the fullest level.

If it is at the very bottom with a window instead of a year, it is not out yet.
Announced entries sort last, carry no runtime, and cannot be marked as watched.

If it carries an Earth designation next to its franchise, it is part of this run
but its story happens somewhere else, and it stays in the list for that reason.
