---
title: "What does it mean when something is marked as an alternate universe?"
order: 160
topic: story
---

That its own sources say it happens somewhere other than the timeline the main
story runs on.

Some are obvious: *What If...?* is an anthology of divergences and says so.
*Marvel Zombies* continues one of them. *Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man*
states outright that it is a different timeline.

Some are less obvious. *Deadpool & Wolverine* is a Marvel Studios film in a
numbered phase, and it takes place on Earth-10005, which is where the Fox X-Men
films happen. *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* is set on Earth-828 for a reason
the film-makers have explained.

This catalogue marks all of them the same way, on the row and on the page, and
none of them are hidden. The flag follows what a source says, not the medium and
not the studio.
