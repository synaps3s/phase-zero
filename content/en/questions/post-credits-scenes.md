---
title: "Do I have to sit through the credits?"
order: 100
topic: watching
---

Yes, and it is one of the few genuinely universal rules here.

The convention started with *Iron Man*, whose scene after the credits is where
the word Avengers is said for the first time. Since then almost every entry has
one or two, and they range from a joke to the introduction of a character the
next three films are about.

There is no way to know in advance which kind you are getting, which is the
point. Where a scene matters enough to change what you should watch next, this
catalogue records it as a connection on the title's own page.
