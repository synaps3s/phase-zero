---
title: "What is the multiverse, and why does everyone keep saying it?"
order: 120
topic: story
---

Until 2019 this story had one timeline. Everything happened in one place to one
set of people, and that was the whole architecture.

From 2021, starting with the series *Loki*, that stops being true. The single
protected timeline is revealed to be one branch among infinitely many, each a
version of the same universe where something went differently, and the thing
that had been keeping it single is removed on purpose.

The practical consequence is that stories which looked finished are not.
Characters from other studios' films, made decades ago in continuities that had
nothing to do with this one, can now walk in as versions from another universe.
Almost everything from Phase Four onward passes through this idea.
