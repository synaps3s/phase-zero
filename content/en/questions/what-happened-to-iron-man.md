This one is a real answer to a real question, so it is behind a warning rather
than in the open. If you have not seen *Avengers: Endgame*, close this.

:::spoiler{level="major" from="avengers-endgame"}
Tony Stark dies. He uses the Infinity Stones himself in the final battle,
destroys Thanos and his army, and does not survive it. He leaves a wife and a
daughter.

Steve Rogers survives, returns the Stones to the moments they were taken from,
and stays. He lives the life he was cheated out of with Peggy Carter, comes back
as an old man, and hands the shield and the name to Sam Wilson.

Natasha Romanoff dies earlier, on Vormir, giving her life so the team can have
the Soul Stone. She does not come back.
:::
