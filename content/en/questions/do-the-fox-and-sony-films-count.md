For twenty years, no. Marvel had licensed those characters to other studios
before it started making its own films, which is why the connected run was built
out of Iron Man and Thor rather than Spider-Man and the X-Men. The Fox films and
the Sony films are separate continuities made by separate companies.

That has been coming apart since 2021. *Spider-Man: No Way Home* put three
studios' Spider-Men in one film. *Deadpool & Wolverine* crossed properly.
*The Fantastic Four: First Steps* is a Marvel Studios film, and the first X-Men
film made inside the connected run is scheduled for 2028.

You do not need any of them to follow the main story. They are in this catalogue
because their characters keep walking into it.
