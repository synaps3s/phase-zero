---
title: "What are the phases and the sagas?"
order: 90
topic: watching
---

They are the studio's own way of dividing its output, and they are editorial
rather than something anybody inside the story knows about.

A **phase** is a numbered block of releases, like a season of a very long
series. Phase One is 2008 to 2012 and ends with *The Avengers*. There are seven
so far, and the seventh has not been officially designated.

A **saga** is a group of phases with one story running through them. Phases one
to three are the Infinity Saga and end with *Avengers: Endgame*. Phases four to
six are the Multiverse Saga.

Both are useful for finding your place and neither tells you what to watch.
