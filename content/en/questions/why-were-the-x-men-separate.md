---
title: "Why were the X-Men and Spider-Man kept out for twenty years?"
order: 170
topic: story
---

Because Marvel had sold the film rights before it started making films. In the
1990s the company licensed the X-Men and mutants to 20th Century Fox and
Spider-Man to Sony, and when it later set up its own studio those were the
characters it could not use.

That single fact shapes the entire first decade. The connected run was built out
of Iron Man, Thor, Hulk and Captain America because they were what was left.

It went further than casting. Wanda and Pietro Maximoff sat on the line: both
studios claimed them, and the settlement was that both could use them provided
neither pointed at the other's property. The Marvel films could not say the word
mutant or name Magneto. The Fox films could not call them Avengers.
