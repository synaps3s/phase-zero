---
title: "Some of your dates and numbers differ from other sites. Why?"
order: 210
topic: catalogue
---

Usually because of a convention, and the convention is written down.

A release date here is the United States theatrical date. Many of these films
opened elsewhere first, sometimes by weeks, so first-release-anywhere would
reorder the run against how almost every source describes it. A few older films
never had a United States cinema release at all: those carry their first
release, and their page says which kind it was.

A series runtime is per episode, and where a source gives a range this records
the median. The total for a season is that figure times the episode count.

Where a source contradicts itself, this catalogue records what the source's own
data field says and puts the contradiction on the page.
