---
title: "Who is Thanos and why did it take ten years to get to him?"
order: 150
topic: story
---

A warlord from a dead world who believes the universe has too many people in it
and intends to remove half of them, at random, in a single act.

He appears for a few seconds at a time across six years, in films that have
nothing to do with him, before the film about him arrives. That is deliberate:
by the time he turns up properly the audience has been told to be afraid of him
by four different sets of characters.

What makes him work is that the films never give him a lie to hide behind. He
believes what he says, applies it to himself, and nobody delivers the speech
that proves him wrong. The story shows the result instead.
