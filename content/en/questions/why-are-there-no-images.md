---
title: "Why are there no posters or photographs on this site?"
order: 190
topic: catalogue
---

Because they are copyrighted, and hosting them would put the project at legal
risk and pass that risk to anybody who forked it.

Every mark, emblem and glyph you see here was drawn in this repository as vector
artwork and generated from the data. That is a constraint rather than a
preference, and it turned out to be the reason the site looks like itself
instead of like every other site about these films.

It is one of five rules that a change to this project cannot break, and it is
checked automatically.
