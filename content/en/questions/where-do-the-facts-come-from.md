Every entry carries its sources and the date somebody last opened them. Those
are on the page, at the bottom, as links you can follow.

The rule the project runs on is that a source is opened and read before a fact
is written, never recalled and given a plausible link afterwards. That rule
exists because it was broken here first: the earliest entries were written from
memory with real URLs attached that nobody had opened, and checking them found
two wrong runtimes out of six. Famous facts, complete confidence, still wrong.

Where two sources disagree, both are recorded and the disagreement is explained
rather than settled quietly. There are several of those in here, and they are
usually the most interesting thing on the page.
