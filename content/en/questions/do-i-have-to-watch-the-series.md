---
title: "Do I have to watch the series, or can I stay with the films?"
order: 60
topic: watching
---

Before 2021, films were the whole story and there is nothing to miss. From
*WandaVision* onward that stops being true.

The clearest case is *Loki*. It ends by changing the rules the rest of the story
runs on, and *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* proceeds as though
you were there. *WandaVision* is the same: the film treats a character's
position as established, and it was established across six hours of television
you did not watch.

You can stay with the films. You will follow the plots. You will keep meeting
people who have visibly been through something you did not see.
