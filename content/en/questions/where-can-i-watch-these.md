This catalogue does not say, on purpose.

Streaming rights move constantly and differ by country, so any answer written
here would be wrong somewhere in the world on the day it was written and wrong
everywhere within a year. A guide that ages that badly is worse than no guide,
because it looks authoritative while being out of date.

What this site can tell you is what exists, what order it goes in, how long it
takes and what depends on what. Where to find a given title is a question for a
service that updates daily, and there are several.
