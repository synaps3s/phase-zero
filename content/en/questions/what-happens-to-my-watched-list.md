---
title: "If I mark things as watched, where does that go?"
order: 220
topic: catalogue
---

Into your browser and nowhere else. No request carries it, there is no account,
and there is no server that could store it even if somebody wanted to.

That has a consequence worth knowing: clearing your site data, using a private
window, or switching to another device loses it. So the timeline has an export
button that writes a small file, and an import button that reads one back. That
is the only way progress moves between devices, and it moves because you moved
it.

Every page here works correctly with storage switched off entirely. It is an
enhancement, never a requirement.
