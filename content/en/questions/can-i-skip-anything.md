title: "Is there anything I can skip entirely?"
order: 80
topic: watching
---

Yes, and the catalogue will not pretend otherwise.

Nothing later depends on *Thor: The Dark World*, *Iron Man 2* or *The Incredible
Hulk* in a way you would notice, though all three set up things you will meet
elsewhere. The Marvel Television era is a self-contained body of work: skipping
it costs you nothing in the films. The One-Shots are four to fifteen minutes
each and are bonus material by design.

What you cannot skip without feeling it: the Avengers films, the Captain America
films, *Guardians of the Galaxy*, and from 2021 onward *WandaVision* and *Loki*.

The watch paths on this site are exactly this question answered several
different ways.
