---
title: The Defenders
oneLine: Four people from the same city who spent their own series avoiding each
  other, put in one building for eight episodes.
tagline: The team that did not want to be one
---

It is not an organisation so much as an occasion. A blind lawyer, a private
investigator, a bulletproof man and a billionaire who says he is a living weapon
all arrive at the same conspiracy from four directions, and the miniseries is
mostly about how badly they get on.

The name is used once, as a joke, by somebody who is not in it.

It is also the closest the Marvel Television era came to its own crossover, and
watching it requires all four of the series before it, which is a demand the
films rarely make.
