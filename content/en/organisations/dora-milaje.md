---
title: The Dora Milaje
oneLine: Wakanda's royal guard, sworn to the throne rather than to whoever is
  sitting on it, and the best soldiers in this catalogue without an enhancement
  between them.
tagline: Loyal to the seat, not the person
---

They are the special forces of a country that has spent centuries pretending to
have none, and they are recruited from every tribe, which is part of how the
throne holds a federation together.

The interesting thing about them is constitutional. Their oath is to the crown,
and the films put that under the worst possible pressure: when the throne is won
legitimately by a man they know is wrong, the oath does not have an exception.
What the general does about that is the most difficult decision anybody makes in
*Black Panther*.
