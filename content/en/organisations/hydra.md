---
title: Hydra
oneLine: A terrorist organisation that began inside the Nazi war machine and
  survived by growing inside the agency built to destroy it.
tagline: Cut off one head
---

Hydra starts as the science division of the Third Reich, led by a man who took
an early and imperfect version of the super soldier serum. The war ends, the
organisation does not, and the arrangement that lets it continue is that the
people hunting Nazi scientists were also recruiting them.

The two people in this catalogue attached to it were not recruits in any
ordinary sense. One was captured, wiped and used as a weapon for seventy years.
The other volunteered, along with her brother, because they wanted something
badly enough to survive an experiment.

Its motto, cut off one head and two more take its place, describes both its
structure and the reason it cannot be beaten by winning a fight.
