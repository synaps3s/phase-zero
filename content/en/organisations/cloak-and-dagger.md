---
title: Cloak and Dagger
oneLine: Two teenagers in New Orleans whose powers came from the same night,
  which is also the night that ruined both their families.
tagline: Light and dark
---

Tandy Bowen is a street thief who can emit light. Tyrone Johnson can engulf
people in darkness and move through it. They are connected by a shared
childhood tragedy, and the series is careful that the connection is the wound
rather than the gift.

Twenty episodes across two seasons on Freeform, set in New Orleans, and unlike
almost everything else in this catalogue it is a story about a city rather than
about a threat. Brigid O'Reilly, a detective who believes nobody is above the
law, is the third point of it.

:::detail{title="Where it sits"}
Part of the Marvel Television tier. It crosses over with *Runaways* and is
otherwise self-contained, which makes it one of the easier things here to watch
without preparation.
:::
