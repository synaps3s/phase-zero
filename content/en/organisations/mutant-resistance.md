---
title: The Resistance
oneLine: The last mutants alive, in a future with nothing left to defend,
  fighting a delaying action so that somebody can undo all of it.
tagline: The future of Days of Future Past
---

Blink, Warpath, Bishop, Sunspot, Colossus, Kitty Pryde, Storm, Wolverine and,
finally in the same room, Xavier and Magneto. They are not an army. They are
what is left after the Sentinels have finished, and every scene they are in is
an evacuation.

They fight better than any team in the Fox films, because they have to: Kitty
sends Bishop back a few days at a time to warn them, Blink puts everyone
exactly where they need to be, and each of them has done this hundreds of
times. The film opens with them losing anyway.

:::detail{title="What they are actually doing"}
The fighting is a rearguard. The plan is Kitty holding Wolverine's mind fifty
years in the past for as long as the others can keep the door shut, and every
person on this list is spending their life buying her minutes.
:::
