---
title: The Warriors Three
oneLine: Thor's oldest friends, three soldiers of Asgard who follow him into
  every bad idea he has, and who are gone before the good films start.
tagline: Fandral, Hogun, Volstagg
---

Fandral the Dashing, Hogun the Grim and Volstagg are Thor's companions from
before the first film. Fandral is a dashing warrior of Asgard, played first by
Joshua Dallas and then by Zachary Levi. Hogun, played by Tadanobu Asano, is a
grim Vanir warrior from Vanaheim. Volstagg, played by Ray Stevenson, is an
Asgardian who loves to eat. All three appear in *Thor*, *Thor: The Dark World*
and *Thor: Ragnarok*.

They exist to make Asgard feel like a place somebody grew up in rather than a
set, and they do that well in the first film. The trouble is that the series
never found anything else for them to do.

:::spoiler{title="How it ends for all three"}
Hela kills them in 2017, within minutes of arriving. *Ragnarok* clears the board
of everything the first two films built so that it can be a different kind of
film, and this is the clearest example of the cost of that decision.
:::
