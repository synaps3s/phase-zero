---
title: The Guardians of the Galaxy
oneLine: Five criminals who met in a prison and stayed together, which is the
  only origin story in this catalogue that nobody planned.
tagline: The family that was an accident
---

They are assembled by circumstance rather than by anybody's initiative: a thief,
an assassin, a bounty hunter, his tree, and a man whose family was murdered.
Every one of them is trying to leave for the first half of the film they meet
in.

What distinguishes them from every other team here is that the group is the
point. The Avengers exist to answer threats. The Guardians exist because none of
them has anywhere else, and the plots are mostly a pretext for putting that
under pressure.

Thor is on the roster below because he joins them for a while, which is a
sentence that would have made no sense in 2014.
