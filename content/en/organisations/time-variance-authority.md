---
title: The Time Variance Authority
oneLine: A bureaucracy that exists outside time and deletes any version of
  history that does not match the one it protects.
tagline: The organisation that files you
---

The TVA monitors the timeline, identifies anything that has diverged from the
approved version, and prunes it, which is a clerical word for an act of erasure
on a scale nothing else in this catalogue attempts.

What makes it work as an idea is the tone. It is not a fortress. It is an
office, with forms, queues, an orientation video and staff who have been doing
this so long that nobody remembers why. The most frightening organisation in
this material has a reception desk.

It is also the mechanism by which the multiverse arrives. Everything from Phase
Four onward passes through what happens to this agency.
