---
title: S.H.I.E.L.D.
oneLine: The agency that watches for the threats no ordinary government is
  equipped to see, and that turned out to have been hollow for seventy years.
tagline: The machinery underneath
---

For the first four years of this story S.H.I.E.L.D. is the infrastructure.
It has the aircraft, the files, the budget and the confidence to walk into
anybody's laboratory, and every character here is either working for it, being
watched by it, or being recruited by it.

Then *Captain America: The Winter Soldier* takes it apart, and not by defeating
it. It reveals that Hydra never went away after the war and simply grew inside
the organisation built to prevent exactly that, which means every good thing the
agency did was also cover.

It has never really come back. What replaces it is a series of smaller,
stranger bodies, and a director who fakes his own death and keeps working.
