---
title: The New Mutants
oneLine: Five young mutants held in a facility that says it is helping them,
  in the only horror film the X-Men series ever made.
tagline: The last one out
---

Rahne Sinclair turns into a wolf and cannot square it with her faith. Illyana
Rasputina has sorcery, a Soulsword and teleportation discs. Sam Guthrie can
propel himself into the air and is invulnerable while he does it. Dani Moonstar
creates illusions from other people's fears and desires. Roberto da Costa
manipulates solar energy. Dr Cecilia Reyes runs the facility and can generate
protective fields.

The film was shot in 2017 and released in August 2020 after repeated delays and
a studio changing hands, and it is the thirteenth and final entry in the Fox
X-Men series. It is the end of that continuity, and almost nobody saw it.

:::detail{title="A horror film, in name at least"}
Josh Boone directed and co-wrote it as a superhero horror film. What reached
cinemas is a much milder thing than the version that was shot, which is the
single most common thing said about it.
:::
