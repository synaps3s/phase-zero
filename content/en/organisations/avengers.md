---
title: The Avengers
oneLine: A team assembled by one man out of people who were never going to
  agree, which spends most of its existence arguing about whether it should
  exist.
tagline: Earth's mightiest, most of the time
---

Nick Fury created the Avengers Initiative in 1995 after meeting somebody who
could fly through a spaceship, and spent seventeen years quietly building a list
before he had a reason to use it. The team is a United States organisation with
no charter, no oversight and no legal standing, which is the argument that
eventually splits it.

What it actually does is get assembled and disassembled. It forms in 2012 for a
single afternoon, reforms with a different roster, breaks over the Sokovia
Accords, exists as two illegal halves for two years, comes back for one day, and
is formally restarted years later under somebody else's leadership.
