---
title: Stark Industries
oneLine: An arms manufacturer inherited by a twenty-one-year-old, run brilliantly
  for two decades, and then shut down in the middle of a press conference.
tagline: The company that started it
---

Howard Stark built it and shaped a century with it. His son inherited it at
twenty-one and ran it the way a prodigy runs anything handed to him: extremely
well, and without ever asking whether it should exist.

The decision that starts this entire story is a business decision. Tony Stark
cancels the weapons division, live, without telling the board, and the film that
follows is about the consequences of a company changing what it makes.

It is the only organisation in this catalogue whose most important act was to
stop doing the thing it was for.
