---
title: The X-Men
oneLine: A school for people who were born different, run by a man who believes
  they can be tolerated, in a world that keeps proving him optimistic.
tagline: The other answer, twenty years earlier
---

They existed on screen before any of this. The first X-Men film arrived in 2000,
eight years before *Iron Man*, and established most of what a modern comic book
film looks like while doing it.

The team is a school first. Charles Xavier takes in children whose abilities
have arrived without warning, teaches them to control what they can do, and
argues in public for a coexistence that his oldest friend has concluded is
impossible. Every film in the series is that argument with different people
standing in for each side.

They belong to a different universe from the rest of this catalogue, made by a
different studio under a separate licence, which is a production fact that
shaped twenty years of both.
