---
title: The Red Room
oneLine: A programme that took girls, trained them as assassins under the cover
  of a ballet academy, and sterilised them on graduation.
tagline: Where two people in this catalogue came from
---

It is described rather than shown for most of a decade. Natasha Romanoff refers
to a ledger written in red, and to a graduation ceremony, long before any film
puts the place on screen.

When one finally does, the detail that lands is not the training. It is the
administration: false families placed in suburban America for years, chemical
control that removes the possibility of refusing, and a man running it who has
never once been in danger.

Two people in this catalogue came out of it. One escaped and spent her life
paying for what she did inside. The other went back for everybody still there.
