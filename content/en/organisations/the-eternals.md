---
title: The Eternals
oneLine: Ten immortals sent to Earth seven thousand years ago with one
  instruction and a rule against ever using their power on people.
tagline: Seven thousand years, watching
---

Sersi can transform matter, Ikaris flies and projects cosmic energy, Kingo
fires energy projectiles and becomes a Bollywood star, Sprite makes illusions
and is stuck as a child, Phastos invents, Makkari runs, Druig controls minds,
Gilgamesh is the strongest of them, Ajak leads, and Thena fights. They have been
here since before recorded history and have not intervened in any of it.

That premise is the film's whole argument and the reason it divides people. If
they were here the entire time, then everything in every other entry in this
catalogue happened while they watched, and the film has to justify that. Some
viewers find the answer profound and some find it a retcon that costs more than
it pays for. This catalogue records both readings and does not settle it.

:::detail{title="Firsts"}
Makkari is the first deaf superhero in these films, played by Lauren Ridloff,
and Phastos, played by Brian Tyree Henry, is the first depicted as gay. Kit
Harington plays Dane Whitman, a human history professor at the Natural History
Museum in London who is seeing Sersi, and who is handed a cursed sword in the
last scene.
:::
