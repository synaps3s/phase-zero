title: The Masters of the Mystic Arts
oneLine: An order that has been defending the planet from things nobody else
  can see, out of three buildings, without telling anyone.
tagline: The people with the library
---

They are a discipline rather than an agency: a hierarchy with its own libraries,
its own three-century arguments about what is permitted, and a standing
responsibility that predates every other organisation here by a long way. Three
Sanctums, in New York, London and Hong Kong, hold a barrier that nothing in the
films had thought to ask about.

Their weakness is the one the story keeps using. The order is small, its
knowledge is written down in a finite number of books, and a sufficiently
motivated reader can get most of the way to catastrophe in an afternoon.
