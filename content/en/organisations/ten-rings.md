---
title: The Ten Rings
oneLine: A criminal organisation founded a thousand years ago by an immortal
  warlord, which spent three films being somebody else's cover story.
tagline: The oldest thing here
---

The Ten Rings are in the very first film. The people who take Tony Stark
hostage in Afghanistan work for them, and their name is said out loud. Then they
appear again, and again, and in *Iron Man 3* it turns out somebody has been
borrowing the organisation's reputation to run a completely different scheme.

Thirteen years after their first appearance, a film finally explains what they
actually are: a thousand-year-old organisation founded by Xu Wenwu, named after
the mystical rings that made him immortal.

It is the longest setup in this catalogue and almost certainly not one that
anybody planned in 2008.
