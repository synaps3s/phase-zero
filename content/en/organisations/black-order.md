title: The Black Order

tagline: The children of Thanos
---

Ebony Maw, Corvus Glaive, Proxima Midnight and Cull Obsidian are adopted
children of Thanos who join his search for the six Infinity Stones. They begin
by attacking the Statesman, the ship carrying Thor and the surviving
Asgardians, to take the Space Stone. Glaive and Midnight are sent after the
Mind Stone and ambush Vision; Maw and Obsidian go to New York for the Time
Stone.

They are the reason *Infinity War* can be in four places at once. Thanos does
not need to be in a scene for his pressure to be in it, and that is what the
Black Order is for.

:::detail{title="Where they are, and are not"}
Wikipedia records one project each for the four of them as of 2026:
*Avengers: Infinity War*. The versions in *Avengers: Endgame* are past versions
brought forward from 2014 by Thanos, which is why they are listed there as
alternate rather than as a return. Gamora and Nebula are also Thanos's adopted
children, and this catalogue keeps them with the Guardians, where they belong.
:::
