The royal family of Attilan is the centre of *Inhumans*, and the premise is
worth stating plainly because the series does not: every Inhuman goes through
Terrigenesis as a teenager, and what comes out of it decides their place for
life. Those who come out with nothing are sent to work underground.

Maximus, the king's brother, came out of it with nothing. He is the only person
in the series who says the arrangement out loud, and because he is the villain
the series never has to answer him.

:::detail{title="Why this entry is short"}
*Inhumans* ran for eight episodes in 2017 and is the least defended thing in
this catalogue. The royal family is recorded here because it is real and because
the series connects to *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*, not because there is much to
recommend.
:::
