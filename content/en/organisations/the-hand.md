---
title: The Hand
oneLine: An ancient order of assassins who cannot be killed permanently, which
  makes them the one enemy in New York that patience does not solve.
tagline: Five fingers, one hand
---

They run through the whole of the Netflix run and they are the reason those
four series are one story. Daredevil meets them in his second season, Iron Fist
was raised to fight them, Colleen Wing was raised inside them, and *The
Defenders* is about all four of the leads arriving at the same building for
different reasons.

The interesting thing about them as an antagonist is structural rather than
mystical. They are an organisation with money, lawyers, a construction project
and a board of directors, and the fighting is a small part of what they do. A
vigilante can punch a man in an alley. He cannot punch a corporation, and the
Hand is the point at which these series admit that.

:::detail{title="Five leaders, and the resurrection"}
The Hand is led by five, and what they want under New York is a substance that
extends their lives, which is why killing one of them tends not to be the end
of the conversation. Elektra is taken by them and made into the weapon they
call the Black Sky.
:::
