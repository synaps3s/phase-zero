---
title: The Runaways
oneLine: Six teenagers who find out what their parents actually do at those
  charity meetings, and leave.
tagline: Pride
---

Alex Wilder is the intelligent one who wants his childhood friends back
together and ends up leading. Nico Minoru is a Wiccan who keeps everyone at a
distance. Karolina Dean is a human and alien hybrid raised inside a religion.
Gertrude Yorkes is an activist with a telepathic link to a genetically
engineered dinosaur. Chase Stein is written off as a jock and is a brilliant
engineer. Molly Hayes Hernandez is the youngest and the strongest.

The series ran three seasons and thirty-three episodes on Hulu. Its idea is
simple and good: the villains are the parents, they meet as an organisation
called Pride, and the children have to decide what they owe people who love
them and are doing something monstrous.

:::detail{title="Where it sits"}
*Runaways* is part of the Marvel Television tier, which the films never
acknowledge. It crosses over with *Cloak & Dagger* and with nothing else.
:::
