---
title: The First Class
oneLine: The first team, assembled by the CIA out of teenagers in 1962, and
  split down the middle by the end of the same summer.
tagline: "1962"
---

Charles Xavier and Erik Lehnsherr recruit them together: Raven, Hank McCoy,
Alex Summers out of a military prison, Sean Cassidy, Armando Munoz and Angel
Salvadore. They train in a garden, they name themselves, and they stop the
Cuban Missile Crisis.

Then the film does the thing the whole series had been avoiding. The people
they saved fire on them, and the group divides on the spot into those who
think that changes everything and those who think it changes nothing. Nothing
in the later films is as clear about what the disagreement actually is.

:::spoiler{title="How many of them last"}
Darwin is killed during the recruitment. Angel leaves for the Hellfire Club
before the mission. Raven and Erik walk away at the end of it. Of the eight
people in this entry, three are still standing on Xavier's side when the credits
roll, and the school opens anyway.
:::
