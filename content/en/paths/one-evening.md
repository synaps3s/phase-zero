---
title: "One evening"
oneLine: "Two films, about four and a half hours, for somebody who wants to know what the fuss is and nothing more."
---

If you never watch anything else here, watch these two. The first is a
self-contained film about an arms manufacturer who changes his mind, made before
anybody knew whether this would work. The second is the one where the idea pays
off, and it is still the clearest demonstration of what the whole thing was for.

Nothing in between is required to understand the second. That gap is a real gap
and it is worth the shortcut.
