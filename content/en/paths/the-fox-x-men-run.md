---
title: "The Fox X-Men, in a watchable order"
oneLine: "Twenty years of films made in the wrong order by a studio that kept changing its mind, arranged so that they mostly make sense."
---

This series was not planned. It was made across two decades, in two casts, with
a time travel film in the middle that rewrites part of it, and watching it in
release order means watching the same characters get younger and then older
again.

The order here goes by the story instead: the prequel run first, in the decades
it is set in, then the original trilogy, then the two Wolverine films that close
his arc, then Deadpool, who is commenting on all of it. *The New Mutants* is
last because it is the last thing made and it needs nothing.

:::detail{title="It still will not fit perfectly"}
No order fixes this series. *X-Men: Days of Future Past* explicitly undoes part
of the original trilogy, so watching that trilogy after it means watching events
the later film has cancelled.

That is not a flaw in the route. It is the series, and there is no arrangement
that hides it.
:::
