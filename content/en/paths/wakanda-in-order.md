---
title: "Wakanda, in order"
oneLine: "A country that spent centuries hiding, told from its own history forward rather than from the outside in."
---

Most viewers meet Wakanda as a rumour in somebody else's film and then get the
whole argument at once. This route does it the other way: it starts inside,
centuries back, with the people sent out to bring stolen vibranium home, and
only then arrives at the week the king dies.

Six entries, and the shortest complete arc in the catalogue that is genuinely
about a place rather than a person.
