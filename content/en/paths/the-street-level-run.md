title: "The street-level run"
oneLine: "Eighteen seasons and one special about people who fight in corridors, from the Netflix era to the revival that finally settled whether it counted."
---

No spaceships. No gods. Everything here happens inside a few square miles of New
York, to people who get tired, and the through-line is not a threat to the
planet but a question about what a city owes the people who live in it.

It is a long watch and it is the most consistent body of work in this catalogue.
The order below interleaves the seasons the way they were released, because they
were written to be watched that way and because the crossover in the middle
assumes the four before it.

:::detail{title="Whether it counts"}
For years nobody would say whether these belonged to the same story as the
films. In January 2024 they were added to the official timeline, and the revival
brought back the same two lead performances.

Each entry's own page has the specific answer for that show, because it differs.
:::
