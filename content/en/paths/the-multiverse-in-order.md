title: "The multiverse, in order"
oneLine: "The entries that build the multiverse from a single timeline to a broken one, in the order the idea develops."
---

Before 2019 this story had one timeline and no interest in others. Six years
later it has a bureaucracy for policing them, three Spider-Men in one room, and
a Fantastic Four on a different Earth. This route is how that happened, with
nothing in it that does not move the idea forward.

It starts at the end of the previous saga, because the multiverse is a
consequence of what is done there. Then a grieving woman, an arrested god, a
spell that goes wrong, and a book that should not be read.

:::detail{title="Why WandaVision is second"}
It is the entry people skip, and it is load-bearing. *Doctor Strange in the
Multiverse of Madness* assumes all of it, emotionally rather than factually, and
plays as a sudden turn without it.
:::
