The multiverse is not introduced in a film. It arrives in a series, and every film that plays with it afterwards assumes you watched that series. This is the shortest route through the idea itself.

It is deliberately not a complete list of everything that touches the multiverse. It is the sequence where the concept is established, tested, and then taken seriously, which is a different and more useful thing.
