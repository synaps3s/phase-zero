---
title: "Before the next Avengers film"
oneLine: "What the Multiverse Saga has actually established, for somebody who wants to be ready for the film that closes it."
---

To be plain about what this is: *Avengers: Doomsday* is not out. Nobody outside
the production knows which of these it will actually need, and this catalogue
does not pretend otherwise.

What this route is instead is the load-bearing spine of the saga the film
closes: the entries where the multiverse is established, where the rules for it
are set, where somebody crosses between universes, and where the last of the
separated properties come home. If the film builds on anything, it builds on
these.

:::detail{title="What is deliberately not here"}
The rest of Phase Four and Five. Some of it is very good and none of it has been
shown to matter to this particular ending, so putting it in a route called
"before the next Avengers film" would be a guess wearing a recommendation's
clothes.

When the film is out, this route gets rewritten from what it actually used, and
the difference between the two versions will be worth reading.
:::
