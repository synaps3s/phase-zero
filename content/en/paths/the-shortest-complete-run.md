This is not the best way to watch it. It is the shortest way that still works, for somebody with limited evenings who wants the whole arc rather than a summary of it.

Everything cut from it is genuinely good and genuinely optional. What is kept is what the ending depends on: the man who starts it, the team forming, the film that breaks the world's trust in institutions, the half of the galaxy that turns out to matter, the argument that splits the team, and the two films that collect all of it.
