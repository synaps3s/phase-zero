---
title: "The cosmic run"
oneLine: "The Guardians from the first film to the last, with the two Avengers films they are actually in and the shorts that fill the gaps."
---

The Guardians films are the most self-contained sequence in the connected run
and the one with the clearest single arc: a group of people with nothing become
a family, badly, over nine years.

This route keeps that arc intact. The two Avengers films are here because the
team is in them and because what happens to two of its members there is the
whole engine of the third film. The shorts and the Christmas special are here
because they are where the family actually gets built, and because they are
five and forty minutes rather than two hours.
