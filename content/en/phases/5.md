---
title: Phase Five
oneLine: A phase with no single antagonist, released while the studio was
  publicly rethinking how much of this there should be.
---

The fifth phase runs through a period in which the production schedule itself
became a subject of discussion. Films moved, series were cancelled, and the
studio began saying out loud that it had been making too much.

What is in it is genuinely varied: a courtroom comedy, a heist across the
galaxy, a Quantum Realm epic, a team film about people nobody would call
heroes. What it does not have is the through-line that the first three phases
had, and that is the criticism it has attracted most.

The end of it is where the shape starts to reassemble, with a run of entries
that are visibly clearing the table for what comes next.
