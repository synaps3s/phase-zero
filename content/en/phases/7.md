---
title: Phase Seven
oneLine: Announced, undesignated, and mostly a set of dates in a schedule.
---

There is very little here yet, and this catalogue is deliberately honest about
that. What exists is a small number of release dates in 2028 attached to
projects that have not begun filming, plus one that will be the first X-Men
film made inside the connected run.

:::detail{title="The phase number is not official"}
Reference material describes these films as expected to be part of Phase Seven
rather than announced as part of it. Marvel has not designated a phase.

The number is recorded here because the source names it and files the entries
under that heading, and each of those pages says the same thing in its own
words. If Marvel designates something different, this is one line to change.
:::
