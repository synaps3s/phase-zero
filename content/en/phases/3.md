title: Phase Three
oneLine: The bill arrives, the cast doubles, and eleven years of setup is
  collected in two films.
---

*Captain America: Civil War* splits the team over the question the run had been
avoiding since the first Avengers film, which is who authorised any of it. From
there the phase spends three years widening: a surgeon, a king, a thief with a
spaceship, a teenager, and a woman who had been off the board since 1995.

Then it closes. *Avengers: Infinity War* is the rare blockbuster where the
antagonist wins outright and the film simply stops, and *Avengers: Endgame*
spends its first act on the year after that rather than on the fix.

It is the densest phase and the one that assumes the most. It is also the last
time the run was a single line that everybody was reading at the same time.

:::detail{title="The one that ends twice"}
*Avengers: Endgame* is the end of the story. *Spider-Man: Far From Home*,
released two months later, is the last film of the phase, and it exists to
answer the question the ending left open: what does a world do the morning
after.

Reference works put both in Phase Three for that reason, which is why this
catalogue does too even though almost everyone remembers the phase ending
earlier.
:::
