---
title: Phase Four
oneLine: The run comes back after a two-year gap, without its two leads, and
  spends the phase asking who is left.
---

There is a gap between the third phase and the fourth, and the phase is shaped
by it. It opens not with a film but with a television series about grief, and
much of what follows is about absence: the people who came back after five
years, the people who did not, and the ones who inherited a name they were not
sure they wanted.

It is also the phase where the shape of the thing changed. Television became
part of the main story rather than a parallel to it, which means a viewer who
watched only the films started missing things for the first time.

Reaction to it was mixed and remains so. What is not in dispute is that it
attempted more different kinds of story than any phase before it.
