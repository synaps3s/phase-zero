---
title: Phase Six
oneLine: The phase that reintroduces the Fantastic Four, brings the X-Men name
  back inside, and ends the Multiverse Saga.
---

This is the phase where the licences come home. The Fantastic Four arrive as a
Marvel Studios production for the first time, and the word mutant stops being
contractually unavailable. Two decades of separation between the largest bodies
of work in this catalogue end here.

It closes the Multiverse Saga with two Avengers films, in the same shape the
Infinity Saga used, which is a deliberate rhyme rather than a coincidence.

:::detail{title="Reading this phase while it is happening"}
Part of this phase is out and part of it is not, and this catalogue marks the
difference on every entry rather than smoothing it over. Anything not yet
released carries the window it was announced for, no runtime, and no way to
mark it as watched.

Dates in this phase have moved more than once. Where they have, the entry says
so.
:::
