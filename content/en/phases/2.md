---
title: Phase Two
oneLine: The consequences of the first phase, and the point at which the run
  stopped resembling itself.
---

Everything here starts from damage. *Iron Man 3* opens on a man who cannot
sleep after New York. *Captain America: The Winter Soldier* takes the agency
that assembled the team and reveals it was hollow. *Avengers: Age of Ultron*
is about the cost of a defence system built by someone who was frightened.

It is also the phase that gambles. *Guardians of the Galaxy* had no famous
characters, no connection to anything on screen so far, and a talking tree, and
it was the point at which the run proved it could go anywhere. *Ant-Man*
followed by being a heist film.

The pattern that holds the phase together is that the sequels got darker and
the new properties got stranger, and both worked.
