---
title: "Mark I"
oneLine: "Built in a cave from the parts of the weapons that wounded him, with the back and knees left open because there was no time."
---

It is made from a missile that was pointed at him, assembled over three months by a man with a car battery wired into his chest and a physicist who kept him alive long enough to finish it. It carries flamethrowers and one missile, and the flight is not flight so much as a controlled fall.

Everything about it is wrong on purpose. It is too heavy, it cannot turn, and its armour stops at the shoulder blades. It was never meant to be worn twice.

:::detail{title="Why it still matters"}
It is the only suit he did not design alone, and the only one built to solve a problem that was not engineering. Every later mark is an improvement on a machine; this one is an improvement on a situation.

He keeps the wreck. It is recovered from the desert and, years later, put on display, which is a small joke about the distance between where he started and what he became.
:::
