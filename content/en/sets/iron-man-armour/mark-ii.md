---
title: "Mark II"
oneLine: "The first one built at home, and the first one that can actually fly. It also freezes solid above forty thousand feet."
---

Bare metal, no paint, and the first heads-up display: the suit starts talking back. The repulsors that will define every later mark are worked out here, in a workshop, by trial and by falling over repeatedly.

Then he takes it high enough to find the flaw. Ice forms on the joints and the power cuts, and he falls most of the way back to the ground before restarting it.

:::detail{title="What happens to it"}
It does not survive the film as his. Stripped and rebuilt by somebody else, it becomes the first armoured suit worn by another person in this story, which sets up a relationship that runs for another decade.
:::
