---
title: "Mark L"
oneLine: "The bleeding edge: nanotechnology that pours out of the reactor and becomes whatever he needs, thrusters for deep space included."
---

This is the suit that stops being a suit. It has no fixed shape, it is stored inside him, and it makes weapons on demand: a shield, a blade, a cannon, a set of arms. The armour has become a material rather than an object.

It is also the first one built for somewhere other than Earth, with propulsion for open space, which he did not know he would need.

:::detail{title="The line it completes"}
Read the marks in order and this is where they were always going. Mark I had to be climbed into. Mark VII came when called. Mark L never leaves. Each step made him more ready and less separable from the machine, and this is the end of that road.
:::
