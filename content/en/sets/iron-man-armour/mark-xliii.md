Unmanned sentry mode is the quiet innovation here, and the one that matters most: the armour no longer needs him at all. He builds a suit that can do his job in his absence, in a film about building things that decide they do not need him.

It is destroyed in a fight against a version of himself, which is not subtle and does not need to be.

:::detail{title="Read it beside what he does next"}
Everything in this phase is Stark trying to make himself unnecessary and discovering what that produces. The sentry mode is the first draft of that idea. The film's antagonist is the second.
:::
