---
title: "Mark XLII"
oneLine: "Gold where the others are red, and built to fly to him one piece at a time from anywhere."
---

It is the prototype for remote assembly: each component travels independently and finds him. When it works it is the most impressive thing any suit does. It mostly does not work, and the film is honest about that.

It spends most of its screen time broken, dragged, or arriving one boot at a time. He fights a large part of the film out of the armour entirely, with it stumbling along behind him.

:::detail{title="The point of it failing"}
This is the film about what happens when the thing you built to feel safe stops being reliable. A suit that half arrives is a better image for that than one that works, and the choice to make his best engineering the least dependable is the film's argument, not a flaw in it.
:::
