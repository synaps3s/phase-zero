---
title: "Mark XLVI"
oneLine: "A retractable helmet and the first hybrid nanotechnology, built during the argument about whether he should be allowed to build anything."
---

It arrives in the film where the question stops being what he can make and becomes who gets to say no to him. The suit is quieter and more personal than the ones before it, which suits a story that is mostly two people arguing and one of them being right about the wrong thing.

The nanotechnology is partial here. It is the first draft of what the armour becomes two films later.

:::detail{title="The fight it ends in"}
It takes the most personal damage of any mark, in a fight that is not about ideology by the time it happens. The film leaves it on the floor of a bunker in Siberia, which is where the first eleven years of his engineering effectively stop.
:::
