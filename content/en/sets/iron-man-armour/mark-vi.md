---
title: "Mark VI"
oneLine: "The triangular reactor: a new element, synthesised in a basement, replacing the one that was poisoning him."
---

The shape is the tell. Every previous chest piece is a circle; this one is a triangle, because what is behind it is different. He built a particle accelerator across his own workshop to make it.

It is the suit that fights in New York, and the one he is wearing when he takes a missile through a portal.

:::detail{title="The last of the old kind"}
It is the final mark that has to be put on rather than summoned, and the last built around a power source he had to invent rather than refine. After it, the engineering stops being about survival and starts being about readiness.
:::
