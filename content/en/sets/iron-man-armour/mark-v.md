---
title: "Mark V"
oneLine: "A suit that folds into a suitcase and builds itself around him, made for the day he needs armour somewhere he cannot bring armour."
---

It is thinner, weaker and far less capable than anything in his workshop, and it exists entirely because he had already worked out that the dangerous moments would not be the ones he prepared for.

He uses it once, on a racetrack, in a suit and tie, against a man with electrified whips. It barely holds together and it is the reason he survives.

:::detail{title="The idea that outlasts it"}
The suitcase is a dead end but the principle is not. From here on every mark is measured by how fast it can reach him, and the line ends with armour stored inside his body.
:::
