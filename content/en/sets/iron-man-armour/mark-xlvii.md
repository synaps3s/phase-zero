---
title: "Mark XLVII"
oneLine: "A repainted Mark XLVI in silver, worn while he is being somebody's mentor rather than somebody's soldier."
---

The colour is the whole note. Underneath it is the same armour, presented differently, in a film about a teenager who wants the suit to mean he has arrived and an adult who knows perfectly well that it does not.

The design nods to a version of the character from another set of comics, which is the kind of reference this run usually keeps in the background.

:::detail{title="The suit that is not the point"}
It barely fights. Its function in the film is to stand next to a boy and be the thing he wants, so that taking it away from him means something. It is the only mark whose job is mostly to be desirable.
:::
