---
title: "Mark VII"
oneLine: "The first suit that comes to him instead of the other way round, called by a bracelet and assembled mid-fall."
---

Every mark before this needed him to be somewhere specific to put it on. This one removes that, and in doing so changes what kind of person he is allowed to be: he no longer has to decide in advance that today is a day for the armour.

The assembly is deliberately ungraceful. It catches him halfway down a building and finishes closing while he is already moving.

:::detail{title="What it costs him"}
Being always ready turns out to be the same thing as never being off duty, and the film after this one is about a man who has not slept since. The suit that solved the problem of getting to the armour created the problem of never being without it.
:::
