title: "Mark XLV"
oneLine: "Predominantly red, with a hexagonal reactor, and the first suit that answers to a new voice."
---

It is the suit he wears after losing the one before it, built quickly and used immediately, and the first with F.R.I.D.A.Y. running it. The change of assistant is the emotional detail: the previous one is gone, and this is the replacement he had to accept.

It also builds a second suit mid-fight, which is the largest thing any mark does and the least remarked upon.

:::detail{title="What the reactor shape tells you"}
The chest piece has changed shape three times: circle, triangle, hexagon. It is a small, consistent way of marking that the thing keeping him alive has been rebuilt from scratch more than once, and each time by a different desperation.
:::
