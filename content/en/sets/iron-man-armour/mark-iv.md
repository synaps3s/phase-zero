It arrives with a helmet that lifts away by hand, which sounds trivial and is the last time it is true. Every suit after this one either opens itself or does not open at all.

It gets the least screen time of any of them, appearing briefly at an exposition where he is using the armour as a stage costume, which is the film's whole argument about him in one image.

:::detail{title="Where it sits"}
This is the calm before the interesting engineering. The next two marks solve portability and power, and after that the line never returns to something this straightforward.
:::
