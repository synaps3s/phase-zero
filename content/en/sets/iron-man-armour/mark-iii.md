title: "Mark III"
oneLine: "The gold-titanium alloy that fixes the icing, and the first time the suit looks like the thing everybody pictures."
---

The problem was cold, and the answer was an alloy. The colour came second: he wanted the alloy and then decided he did not like how it looked, so the red went on afterwards. The most recognisable object in this run exists because of a material science fix and a passing aesthetic complaint.

It is also the first suit he uses on somebody else's behalf, flying to a village on the other side of the world without telling anyone he is going.

:::detail{title="The one that gets wrecked"}
It ends the film badly damaged, which becomes the pattern: almost every mark is destroyed in the film that introduces it. He does not repair suits. He builds the next one.
:::
