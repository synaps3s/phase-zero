---
title: "Mjolnir"
oneLine: "A hammer enchanted so that only those who are worthy may wield it, which makes it less a weapon than a standing verdict."
---

The enchantment is the entire design. Odin placed it there as a punishment
that could be undone, so the hammer is simultaneously a weapon, a sentence and
a way back. Thor spends his first film unable to lift it, which is the only
time in the run that the protagonist is powerless for a structural reason
rather than a plot one.

It is destroyed in his third film, by somebody with a better claim to it than
he has, and the loss is played as a relief rather than a tragedy.

:::detail{title="It comes back, and to somebody else"}
Reference material records that the hammer was remade, with visible cracks,
and that it is later wielded by Jane Foster, who is deemed worthy.

That is the enchantment doing exactly what it was written to do, and it takes
thirteen years for a film to use it on anyone but him.
:::
