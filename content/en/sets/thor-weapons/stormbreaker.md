---
title: "Stormbreaker"
oneLine: "An axe forged at a dying star to replace a hammer that no longer exists, by a man who had run out of other options."
---

It is made on Nidavellir, at a forge Thor has to restart with his own body,
which is the most physically punishing thing the character ever does and the
only time the films let him earn something through endurance rather than
worth.

What it can do is stated plainly: it summons lightning, it allows flight, and
it calls the Bifrost, which means it can put him anywhere in the Nine Realms.
Reference material goes further and says it has the power to defeat even the
Infinity Stones.

:::detail{title="Why it is the more frightening object"}
Mjolnir was limited by a rule. This is not limited by anything, and the film
that introduces it uses that: he arrives with it at exactly the right moment
and it is not enough, because the problem was never the weapon.

That is the point of the whole set. He spends years trying to be worthy of a
tool and then discovers that the better tool did not fix the thing he actually
needed to fix.
:::
