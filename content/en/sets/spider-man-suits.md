---
title: Spider-Man's suits
oneLine: Seven costumes over five films, and the question of who made each one
  is the whole arc of the character.
tagline: Who gave it to him, and who took it back
---

Read the set by its makers rather than its features. He builds the first one
himself because nobody offers him anything. Then a billionaire builds him two,
with the functions locked. Then an intelligence agency builds him one that is
trying to hide him. Then he builds one for himself with borrowed tools. Then
he builds one with nothing at all.

That sequence is the story: a boy who wants to be given permission, learning
that the permission was never the point.

:::detail{title="What the suits are doing in the plot"}
Twice, a film takes a suit away from him, and both times it is the emotional
centre rather than a setback. The suit is how the adults in his life express
approval, so removing it is how they express its withdrawal.

The last one is the only suit in the set that nobody can take, because nobody
knows he has it.
:::
