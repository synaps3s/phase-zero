---
title: The relics
oneLine: Objects that do the magic instead of the sorcerer, which is why a
  student who has learned almost nothing can already fly.
tagline: Borrowed power, and the terms it comes with
---

A relic is not a tool. The distinction matters and the films are consistent
about it: a sorcerer channels energy through their own discipline, and a relic
holds power that was put into it by somebody else, usually a long time ago.
That is why Stephen Strange can be dangerous within months. He is not strong.
He is carrying things that are.

The interesting consequence is that relics have opinions. The Cloak chooses
him. The Eye has rules he breaks. The two books at the end of this list are the
same object twice, written from opposite directions, and both of them are gone
by the time the credits roll on the film that introduces them.

:::detail{title="Why this list is short"}
Kamar-Taj has a room full of these, and most are never named on screen. This
collection records the ones a source identifies, which is five, rather than the
ones a viewer can half remember seeing on a shelf.

If you can point at a source that names another, it takes one file to add it.
:::
