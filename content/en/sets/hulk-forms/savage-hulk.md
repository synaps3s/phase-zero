---
title: "Savage Hulk"
oneLine: "The original arrangement: Banner is gone while it is here, and it wants to be left alone."
---

It speaks in single words or not at all, it does not recognise people it should, and it treats every attempt to contain it as an attack, which it usually is. Banner has no memory of what it does and spends years arranging his life so it never has to appear.

The films are careful never to make it a superpower. It is presented as an illness with a body count, and the running joke about the other Avengers wanting to deploy it is played as tasteless on purpose.

:::detail{title="The one exception"}
There is a single moment where the arrangement works: a fight in New York where somebody asks him to do it deliberately and he does. It is the only time in years that the transformation is a choice rather than a failure, and the films spend the next decade unable to repeat it.
:::
