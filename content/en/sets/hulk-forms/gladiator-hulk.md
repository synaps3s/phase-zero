title: "Gladiator Hulk"
oneLine: "Two years in one body without Banner surfacing at all, on a planet that adores him for winning fights."
---

This is the longest he has ever been the one in charge, and the result is not a monster but somebody with a room, a job and opinions. He has learned to talk in short sentences. He does not want to go back.

The armour is the tell: it was made for him, by people who like him, which has never happened before. For the first time the body is somewhere it is welcome.

:::detail{title="Why he does not want Banner back"}
The film treats this as the emotional centre and mostly hides it under jokes. Being Hulk is the only life in which he is not a problem to be managed, and being asked to give it up is being asked to disappear again.

It is the closest this character comes to arguing his own case.
:::
