title: "Smart Hulk"
oneLine: "Banner's mind and voice in the Hulk's body, permanently, after eighteen months of deliberately putting them together."
---

It is the resolution of the whole set, arrived at off screen between films: he stops treating the two as opponents and works out how to be both at once. He is calmer, funnier, and visibly happier than either half ever was.

It also costs him something the films do not dwell on. The rage went somewhere, and what is left is a man who is very good with people and no longer frightening at all.

:::detail{title="The quiet loss inside it"}
Every earlier form is somebody fighting for control of a body. This one is a truce, and truces end arguments by giving both sides less than they wanted.

Later material has the arrangement break down again, which is the honest ending for a set that was never really about transformation.
:::
