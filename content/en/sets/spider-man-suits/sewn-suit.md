---
title: "Sewn Suit"
oneLine: "Made by hand, from nothing, in a small apartment, by somebody nobody remembers."
---

It is a return to the first suit with none of the amateurishness: no borrowed technology, no assistant, no benefactor. The classic red and blue, sewn.

The set begins with a boy who made his own costume because nobody would give him one, and ends with a man who makes his own because there is nobody left to ask. That is the whole story of the character told in clothing.
