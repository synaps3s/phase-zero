---
title: "Homemade Suit"
oneLine: "A red and blue hoodie with goggles and a pair of web-shooters he built in his bedroom."
---

It is a costume in the literal sense: fabric, not armour, made by a fifteen-year-old with no money and a working knowledge of chemistry. The web-shooters are the only part that is genuinely advanced, and he invented those himself.

It matters because everything that comes after is given to him. This is the only suit that is entirely his, and the film that takes it away is doing something on purpose.
