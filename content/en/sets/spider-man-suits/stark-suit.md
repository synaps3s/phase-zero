---
title: "Homecoming Suit"
oneLine: "The first one somebody else made: a full Stark suit with an assistant inside it and hundreds of web settings he is not allowed to use."
---

It arrives with training wheels. Most of its functions are locked, there is an AI watching him, and the film's central conflict is whether he deserves to have the restrictions removed.

The suit is the relationship. When it is taken back from him it reads as a parent grounding a child, which is exactly how the film frames it.
