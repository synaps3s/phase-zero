---
title: "Upgraded Suit"
oneLine: "Red and black, built by Peter himself using Stark's fabricator, in the sequence where he decides what kind of hero to be."
---

It is the turning point of the set. Everything before it was inherited or issued; this one he designs, in a montage that is explicitly about him choosing rather than being chosen.

The colours are his own and the technology is borrowed, which is a fair description of where the character is at that point.
