---
title: "Integrated Suit"
oneLine: "The Upgraded Suit with the Iron Spider's nanites folded into it, made in the middle of the worst night of his life."
---

It is the last suit built with any of Stark's technology, assembled in a hurry for a fight he did not want, and it is visually the most complicated thing he ever wears.

The set's whole trajectory is legible in it: every gift he has been given, combined into one object, immediately before he loses all of them.
