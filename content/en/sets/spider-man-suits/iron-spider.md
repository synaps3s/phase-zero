---
title: "Iron Spider"
oneLine: "Nanotechnology in a red and gold shell, with four mechanical legs that unfold from the back."
---

This is the one he is offered as an Avenger and initially refuses, which is the closest the character comes to a defining choice: he turns down the promotion because he wants to stay local.

He ends up in it anyway, in space, and the legs turn out to be the most useful thing on it. It is the only Spider-Man suit built to survive somewhere without air.
