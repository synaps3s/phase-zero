---
title: "Stealth Suit"
oneLine: "Black and grey, made by somebody who is not Stark, for a job Peter did not ask for."
---

It comes from an intelligence agency rather than a workshop, and it looks it: no red, no blue, nothing that says Spider-Man at all. The design nods to a version of the character from a different set of comics.

It is the only suit in the set that is trying to hide him, and the film is faintly embarrassed about it in a way that is clearly deliberate.
