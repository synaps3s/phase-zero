---
title: The forms Hulk takes
oneLine: The same body, three different people inside it, arriving in the order
  that turns a curse into a person.
tagline: Not transformations, negotiations
---

Every other set here is made of objects somebody built. This one is made of
states a person is in, which makes it the strangest collection on the site and
the one that says the most about its subject.

Read in order it is an argument being settled. The first form is somebody else
wearing his body. The second is that somebody being liked for it. The third is
the two of them agreeing to stop fighting, which the films present as a
recovery and which is also, quietly, a loss.

:::detail{title="A note on the names"}
These names are not official. Reference material uses them descriptively
rather than establishing them as formal designations, and the films themselves
mostly do not name the states at all.

They are recorded here because they are what everybody calls them and because
the distinction they describe is real. Where a name is a convention rather
than a fact, this project says so rather than presenting the convention as
though it were the thing.
:::
