---
title: The Infinity Stones
oneLine: Six objects older than the universe, each one controlling a single
  aspect of existence, scattered and hidden until somebody tried to collect
  them all.
tagline: Six things, one plan, eleven years
---

The Stones are the spine of the first twenty-two films. They arrive one at a
time, usually disguised as something else, and for most of that run nobody on
screen understands what they are looking at.

Their real function is structural. They gave a decade of separate films a
reason to be one story, and they let a villain who appears for thirty seconds
in 2012 remain a credible threat until 2018 without doing anything.

:::detail{title="Why the vessels matter more than the stones"}
Almost none of them are found as a bare stone. They turn up as a cube, a
sceptre, a substance, an orb, an amulet. That disguise is doing two jobs: it
lets a film use one as a plot device without explaining the whole system, and
it means a rewatch shows you a Stone in a film you thought had nothing to do
with any of this.
:::
