---
title: The Infinity Gauntlet
oneLine: A left-handed glove forged out of star metal, built to hold all six
  stones at once.
---

It is owned by Thanos and forged from uru by Eitri and the Dwarves of
Nidavellir. It can harness the power of all six Infinity Stones together, which
makes the wearer able to do whatever they can imagine.

The scene where it is made is the reason it works as an object. Thanos does not
find it. He commissions it, from a workforce he then kills.
