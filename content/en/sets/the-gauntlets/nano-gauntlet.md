---
title: The Nano Gauntlet
oneLine: The one the Avengers build themselves, right-handed, out of the
  technology they already had.
---

Also called the Iron Gauntlet or the Power Gauntlet, it is made by Tony Stark,
Bruce Banner and Rocket using Stark nanotechnology, and it is designed to do
what Thanos's gauntlet did in order to undo it.

Bruce Banner is the one who uses it, because the stones give off radiation that
almost everybody in the room would not survive. During the battle that follows
it is passed from person to person, which is the only sequence in these films
where the object matters more than whoever is holding it.
