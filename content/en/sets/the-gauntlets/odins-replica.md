---
title: The replica in Odin's vault
oneLine: A gauntlet that sat in the background of a 2011 film for six years
  before anybody had to decide what it was.
---

A replica of the Infinity Gauntlet is kept in Odin's vault on Asgard. It first
appears in *Thor* as an Easter egg.

Marvel Studios later worked out that it could not be the real one, and formed
an internal position that the Asgardian gauntlet was a fake, which produced a
line in *Thor: Ragnarok* where Hela looks at it and dismisses it.
