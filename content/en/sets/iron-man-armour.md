title: The Iron Man armours
oneLine: Every suit Tony Stark builds is an answer to the last thing that
  nearly killed him, which is why there are so many of them.
tagline: Numbered, because he kept going
---

The marks are not upgrades in the way a phone is an upgrade. Each one fixes a
specific failure: the first cannot fly straight, the second freezes at
altitude, the third solves that and becomes the shape everyone remembers.

Reading them in order is a decent way to read the character. They get faster to
put on, harder to take off, and closer to his body, until the last few are
simply part of him. That is either the arc of a man solving a problem or the
arc of a man who cannot stop, and the films are careful never to settle it.

:::detail{title="Why the numbers jump"}
Between the ones you see there are dozens you do not. The films establish that
he builds constantly and only shows the ones a plot needs, so the run goes III,
then XLII, then L, with the gaps standing for years of work happening off
screen.

The entries here are the ones that appear and matter. A complete list of every
mark is a different document, and a less interesting one.
:::
