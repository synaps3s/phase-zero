---
title: "The astronaut telling stories to the Watchers"
oneLine: "Sitting on an asteroid in a spacesuit, telling a group of Watchers about his life, including his time as a delivery man."
---

This is the scene that turned a running gag into a theory. If the same man is on Xandar, in a New York street and on an asteroid recounting all of it to cosmic observers, then the cameos are one character rather than forty.

:::detail{title="They leave without him"}
He comes back after the credits, still talking, while the Watchers walk away tired of him. He points out that they are his only ride home. The Watchers, and Ego, are among the few characters in these films he did co-create.
:::
