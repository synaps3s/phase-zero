---
title: "The man who sells Miles the suit"
oneLine: "A shopkeeper who sells Miles Morales a Spider-Man costume and refuses to take it back, telling him it always fits, eventually."
---

Released a month after his death. The directors said they wanted him to have a real moment here rather than a passing one, because he was so much a part of what the film was about.

:::detail{title="The other places he is in it"}
He turns up again in a few frames of a passing train, and his glasses appear after the credits in a tribute to him and to Steve Ditko.
:::
