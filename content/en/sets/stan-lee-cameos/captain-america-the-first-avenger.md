He says he thought he would be taller, which is a joke about a soldier and also about a reader who has only ever seen the character drawn.

:::detail{title="What he did and did not invent"}
Lee did not create Captain America. He did create the idea of the shield thrown as a weapon, and he brought the character back in the Silver Age and wrote most of his stories in that period.
:::
