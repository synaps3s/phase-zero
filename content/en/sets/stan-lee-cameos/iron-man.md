It is the first one, and it sets the shape of all the others: he is placed where the hero is already looking, given a line that costs the scene nothing, and left there.

:::detail{title="The take that was not used"}
A second version was filmed in which Stark realises his mistake and Lee answers, that is okay, I get this all the time. The theatrical cut keeps the shorter version. In 2008 Lee named this his favourite of his cameos to that point.
:::
