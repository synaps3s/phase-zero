---
title: "The man on the train, reading his own lines"
oneLine: "Riding a Los Angeles train in 1995, rehearsing his lines from the screenplay of Mallrats, he catches Carol Danvers looking and smiles at her."
---

Released four months after his death, it is the first of the posthumous appearances and the only one where he plays himself doing his own job.

:::detail{title="The logo that year"}
The Marvel Studios logo at the head of the film was rebuilt out of his cameos and ends on the words Thank You Stan. Lee did not create Carol Danvers, though he did co-create the first character to carry the name Captain Marvel.
:::
