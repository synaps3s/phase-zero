It is the first cameo set outside Earth, and the film treats it as proof he gets everywhere. His catchphrase, Excelsior, appears on the scanner rendered in Kree.

:::detail{title="How much of this he made"}
Almost none of it. Apart from Groot, Ronan the Accuser and the Collector, the characters in this film are not his.
:::
