---
title: "The officer on the wall of the precinct"
oneLine: "A photograph of him in police uniform, hung on a commendation plaque in a New York precinct, and repeated across six Netflix series."
---

He never appears in person in any of them. The plaque turns up in the middle of the final episode of Daredevil's first season, and the same conceit recurs in Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist, The Defenders and The Punisher.

:::detail{title="He is given a name, eventually"}
Iron Fist identifies the officer as Captain Irving Forbush, a name Marvel had used since 1955 for its own in-house joke. Jessica Jones then makes Forbush a lawyer with a firm called Forbush and Associates, and Luke Cage puts his advertising on a billboard: call Forbush, get what you deserve.
:::

:::detail{title="What the sources do and do not say"}
The catalogue's sources name the six series and the season one placement in Daredevil. They do not give an episode for each of the later series, so this entry does not claim one.
:::
