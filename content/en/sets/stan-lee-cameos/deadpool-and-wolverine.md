title: "The face on the Stanlee Steamer advert"
oneLine: "His photograph on an advert for a carpet cleaning firm called Stanlee Steamer, promising a friendly neighbourhood cleaner."
---

Five years after the last real cameo the joke has become a piece of set dressing: a pun on a cleaning company, his own birth year printed on the advert, and archival footage of him in the closing montage.
