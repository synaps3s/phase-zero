title: "The same rescue, two years later"
oneLine: "During Spider-Man's first fight with Doctor Octopus, he repeats the rescue he performed in the previous film, this time with a young woman."
---

Doing it twice is what made it a habit rather than an accident. The gag from here on is that he is always in the crowd, in every crowd.

:::detail{title="The line that went to somebody else"}
A blooper on the home release has him say that Spider-Man stole that kid's sneakers. The scripted line was about a stolen pizza, and it stayed in the film, spoken by Donnell Rawlings instead.
:::
