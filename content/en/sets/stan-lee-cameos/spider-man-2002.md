No line, no look at the camera. Of all of them this is the one that behaves most like an ordinary extra, which is why it took years for most viewers to notice it was there.

:::detail{title="The scene that was cut"}
A deleted scene has him as a street vendor trying to sell Peter Parker sunglasses, saying they wore them in The X-Men. It was most likely cut because those films belonged to a different studio.
:::
