---
title: "The man with the pickup truck"
oneLine: "He ties a chain to Thor's hammer, drives off, and takes the bed of his own truck with him."
---

Everyone in the crater is trying to lift the thing. He is the only one who brings equipment, and the only one whose equipment loses. Sitting in the wreckage he asks the crowd, did it work.

:::detail{title="How he is credited"}
The credit reads Stan the Man, the nickname he had taken during the Silver Age of comics.
:::
