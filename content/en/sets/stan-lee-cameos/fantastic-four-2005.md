---
title: "Willie Lumpkin, the postman"
oneLine: "The mail carrier who greets the four as they walk into the Baxter Building, and the first time he plays a character he created himself."
---

Everywhere else he is himself, or an unnamed man in a crowd. Here he is playing somebody out of his own comics, a postman he and Jack Kirby had put in the building in 1961.
