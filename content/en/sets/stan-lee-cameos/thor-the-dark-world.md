---
title: "The patient who lends his shoe"
oneLine: "A patient in a psychiatric ward who hands Erik Selvig a shoe so he can demonstrate the Convergence, then wants it back."
---

Selvig is the only man in the film who understands what is happening and the only one everybody has decided is mad. The cameo sits inside that joke rather than beside it: the audience in the ward is taking the lecture seriously.
