oneLine: "A suburban neighbour watering his garden in a scene set twenty years earlier, when the water from his hose stops obeying him."

Jean Grey is a child in this scene, and the water going up instead of down is the first thing anyone sees her do. Chris Claremont, who wrote the comics run this film draws on, is the neighbour standing beside him.
