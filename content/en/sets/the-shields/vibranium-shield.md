---
title: The vibranium shield
oneLine: The original, made from a metal Howard Stark had exactly enough of to
  make one thing.
---

It is created by Howard Stark and given to Steve Rogers during the Second World
War, and it is vibranium: it absorbs what hits it instead of passing the shock
through to the arm behind it. Stark had one supply and no more, which is the
scene that quietly establishes how rare the metal is nine films before Wakanda
appears.

It is destroyed during the Battle of Earth, seventy-eight years after it was
made, by somebody strong enough that absorbing the impact stops being relevant.
