---
title: John Walker's shield
oneLine: Scrap metal and a medal, welded into a copy by a man who was told the
  real one was not his.
---

After he is stripped of the title, John Walker builds a shield of his own out
of scrap metal and his Medal of Honor. He abandons it later.

It is the least impressive object in this collection and the most revealing.
Two of these shields are about who is worthy of a symbol. This one is about
somebody who could not let go of it.
