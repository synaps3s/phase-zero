---
title: The alternate shield
oneLine: The one that gets handed over at the end, which is not the one that was
  broken twenty minutes earlier.
---

Reference works describe this as an alternate version of the shield. It is the
one an elderly Steve Rogers is holding when he gives the job to Sam Wilson,
which is possible because the original was destroyed in the same film.

Wilson donates it to the Smithsonian. The government takes it back out and
gives it to John Walker, and the argument of *The Falcon and the Winter
Soldier* is what an object means when it can be reassigned.
