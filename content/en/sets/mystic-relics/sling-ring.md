---
title: "Sling ring"
oneLine: "A two-finger ring that opens a doorway anywhere the wearer can picture
  clearly enough."
---

It is the least dramatic relic and the one that changes the most. Sorcerers do
not travel, they arrive, and every fight in this part of the catalogue is
shaped by the fact that anyone with a ring and a clear mental image can leave
or bring an army.

The films understand its value best when they take it away. In *No Way Home*,
Peter Parker steals Strange's ring and strands him in the Mirror Dimension for
twelve hours, which is the entire reason the plot is allowed to go wrong.
