---
title: "The Darkhold"
oneLine: "A book of corrupted magic that gives its reader everything they ask
  for and takes them apart while doing it."
---

The Darkhold is the Book of Vishanti's opposite and it is the one that works.
It teaches dreamwalking, possession across universes, and a great deal more, and
its price is not paid in energy. It is paid in the reader.

By the time it appears in a cinema, Wanda Maximoff has already read it. The film
treats that as settled backstory and spends its length on the consequences.

:::detail{title="Where it comes from, and where it ends"}
It is a copy. The source of its power is a temple on Mount Wundagore built for
the being who wrote it, and Wanda goes there when the copy she has is destroyed.

At the end of the film she turns the book's own magic against it and brings the
mountain down, which destroys every copy of the Darkhold across the multiverse,
and herself with them.
:::
