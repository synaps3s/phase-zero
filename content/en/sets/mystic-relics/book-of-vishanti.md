---
title: "Book of Vishanti"
oneLine: "A book of pure magic that is supposed to give a sorcerer whatever they
  need to defeat their enemy, and that most sorcerers believe is a myth."
---

Strange says plainly that it is not real. He is wrong, and the film opens on a
version of him dying while trying to reach it, in the space between universes
where it is kept.

It is written as the answer to the whole plot, and then it is destroyed within
seconds of being found. That is a deliberately cruel piece of construction, and
it is what forces the film's ending to be a choice rather than a weapon.
