---
title: "Eye of Agamotto"
oneLine: "A relic worn at the throat that can run time forwards and backwards,
  because there is an Infinity Stone inside it."
---

Strange finds it, reads the book it is described in, and teaches himself to use
it in an afternoon, which is precisely what Mordo and Wong tell him not to do.
The Eye can rewind an object, loop a moment, or age something into dust, and
the film is careful to show all three before it explains any of the danger.

It is also the join between two parts of this story that had nothing to do with
each other. The magic and the Infinity Stones arrive in the same object.

:::detail{title="What it actually is"}
The Eye is the housing. The power is the Time Stone, one of the six, which
means a relic in a temple in Nepal had been holding a piece of the thing the
next two Avengers films are entirely about.

That is why Strange is at the centre of those films rather than an addition to
them, and it is why the object stops being available after them.
:::
