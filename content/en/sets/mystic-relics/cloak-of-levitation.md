---
title: "Cloak of Levitation"
oneLine: "A red cloak that flies its wearer, fights on its own initiative, and
  has more presence in a scene than most of the people in it."
---

It is introduced doing the job of a sidekick. It carries Strange into the air,
blocks for him, and in the attack on the New York Sanctum it is the reason he
holds the zealots off at all, which is not a sentence you can write about most
pieces of costume.

The design choice underneath is a good one. The character's whole problem is
that he wants to be the best at everything immediately, and one of the first
things the story gives him is equipment with better instincts than he has. Even
reviewers who disliked the film singled it out: it has a personality, and it
uses it.
