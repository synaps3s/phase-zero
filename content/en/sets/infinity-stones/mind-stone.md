---
title: Mind Stone
oneLine: A yellow stone that can reach into a mind and change what it wants, hidden inside the sceptre its first owner is given rather than in his hands.
---

It is the only Stone that spends part of its life as a person. Set into a sceptre it turns people into willing servants; set into a synthetic body it produces someone with a conscience, which the films never fully account for.

:::detail{title="Where you first see it"}
It arrives in The Avengers in 2012, in the weapon Loki carries. Nobody names it as a Stone for another three years.
:::
