---
title: Reality Stone
oneLine: A red stone that does not obey the shape of a stone, appearing instead as a liquid that rewrites whatever it touches.
---

The most abstract of the six and the least used, which is probably related. Its power is stated rather than shown: it makes what is true stop being true, and a film has to work hard to dramatise that.

:::detail{title="Where you first see it"}
It is the substance at the centre of Thor: The Dark World in 2013, where it is called the Aether and treated as a weapon rather than as one of a set.
:::
