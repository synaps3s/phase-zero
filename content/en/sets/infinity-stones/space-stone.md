---
title: Space Stone
oneLine: A blue stone that can open a door between any two places, carried for decades inside a cube nobody realises is a container.
---

It spends most of its screen life as the Tesseract, an object that several organisations fight over while understanding only that it produces enormous amounts of energy. What it actually does is remove distance.

:::detail{title="Where you first see it"}
It appears at the end of Thor in 2011, months before the film that makes it a plot device. That is typical of how these were introduced: as set dressing first, and as a system much later.
:::
