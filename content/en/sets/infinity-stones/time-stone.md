---
title: Time Stone
oneLine: A green stone that can run time backwards, forwards, or in a circle, kept in an amulet by people who understand exactly how dangerous that is.
---

The only Stone whose keepers know what they have. That changes how it is used: it comes with rules, warnings and a discipline, and the film that introduces it is largely about earning the right to hold it.

:::detail{title="Where you first see it"}
It is in the Eye of Agamotto in Doctor Strange in 2016, and unusually it is named and explained on screen in the same film.
:::
