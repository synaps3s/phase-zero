---
title: Soul Stone
oneLine: An orange stone that cannot be taken, only paid for.
---

The strangest of the six, because its power is barely used and its cost is the entire point. It is guarded by a rule rather than by a person, and the rule is what makes the film containing it work.

:::detail{title="Where you first see it"}
It is found on Vormir in Avengers: Infinity War in 2018, the last of the six to appear and the only one whose location was a mystery inside the story rather than only to the audience.
:::
