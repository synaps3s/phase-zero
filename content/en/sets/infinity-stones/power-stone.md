---
title: Power Stone
oneLine: A purple stone holding enough raw force to destroy a planet, and enough to kill anyone who touches it without protection.
---

The simplest of the six and the most immediately frightening, because the film that introduces it demonstrates what it does to a person rather than describing it. Everything after that scene is played with the memory of it.

:::detail{title="Where you first see it"}
It is inside the Orb that the plot of Guardians of the Galaxy is built around in 2014, wanted by four different parties who mostly do not know what it is.
:::
