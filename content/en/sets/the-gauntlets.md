---
title: The gauntlets
oneLine: Three metal gloves, one of which the studio had to decide, years later,
  was a fake.
tagline: Left hand, right hand, and a prop
---

The Infinity Gauntlet is the object the first ten years of this catalogue are
pointed at. There are three of them.

The one Thanos wears is forged from uru by the Dwarves of Nidavellir and is
left-handed. The one the Avengers build to undo his work is right-handed, made
by Tony Stark, Bruce Banner and Rocket out of Stark nanotechnology. And the
third has been sitting in Odin's vault since 2011, when it was put there as a
joke.

:::detail{title="The joke that had to be retconned"}
The gauntlet in Odin's treasure room appeared in *Thor* as an Easter egg.
Marvel Studios later realised it could not be the real one, since the real one
had not been made yet, and settled internally on the idea that the Asgardian
gauntlet was a fake. That decision is then written into *Thor: Ragnarok*, where
Hela walks past it and says so out loud.

It is a rare case of a franchise correcting its own background detail in the
text, in front of the audience, seven years later.
:::
