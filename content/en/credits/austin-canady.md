He started with three names. Danny Rand was not here, and neither were Hector
Ayala or Angela del Toro, and he said so. That was the first sign that the
character coverage was not merely unfinished but thin in a way nobody inside
the project had noticed.

Then he sent a list of about ninety more. Working through it took this
catalogue from a hundred and twenty-three people to well over two hundred, and
not one entry on that list turned out to be a matter of taste. The Netflix
street level, the Fox mutants, the Asgardian court, the cosmic tier, the
monsters, the agents: every group he named was a real gap.

He is also the reason several things here are organisations rather than
people. He noticed that some of what he had listed were groups, which is why
the Hand, the Warriors Three, the Black Order, the Eternals, the Runaways,
Cloak and Dagger, the New Mutants and the Inhuman Royal Family have entries of
their own.

The Earth numbers on the character index are his idea as well. He pointed out
that somebody who meets Cyclops or the Green Goblin in a list with no mark on
it will assume there is a version of them in the shared story, and asked for
the universe to sit next to the name of everyone who is not from it. It does
now.

:::detail{title="The two he was most right about"}
He asked why the Spider-Verse films were catalogued and none of their people
were, and made the argument rather than just the request: Miguel O'Hara naming
Earth-199999, and Donald Glover reprising his role from
*Spider-Man: Homecoming* inside an animated film. Both are recorded here now,
with sources.

And he said the emblems were too plain and that too many looked alike. Checking
that turned up a real bug, where two parts of the design system were writing to
the same custom property and knocking the generated marks out to black, and
then a rebuild of the generator so that a character without an authored glyph
still gets a mark that belongs to them.

The mark above is not from that generator. It is drawn by hand in
`src/lib/sigils.ts`, on the same terms as every character glyph in this
project: a web of twelve strands and three rings computed around the centre,
with the eyes of the mask he wears as an avatar over it, in one colour, in the
same frame. Nothing in it is traced from anything.
:::
