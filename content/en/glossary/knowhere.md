title: Knowhere
aliases: ["Knowhere"]
oneLine: A city built inside the severed head of a dead giant, which is a real
  address in these films and not a figure of speech.
---

Knowhere is described as the enormous severed head of an ancient celestial
being, used as an interdimensional crossroads and a scientific observatory. It
appears in *Thor: The Dark World*, *Guardians of the Galaxy*, *Avengers:
Infinity War*, the Holiday Special and *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3*.

It is worth having in a glossary because it is the clearest example of what the
cosmic side of this catalogue is doing. The mining town is inside the skull, the
skull belongs to a Celestial, and nobody in the scene finds any of that
remarkable.
