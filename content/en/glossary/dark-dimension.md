---
title: Dark Dimension
aliases: ["Dark Dimension"]
oneLine: A timeless place that has swallowed every other dimension it has
  conquered, and the home of the thing that wants this one.
---

The Dark Dimension is described as timeless, and as an amalgamation of itself
and every other dimension its ruler has absorbed. Its ruler is Dormammu, and
Kaecilius contacts it in order to hand over the Earth.

Because there is no time inside it, Stephen Strange can bargain there by making
a loop and dying in it repeatedly until the other party finds the arrangement
tiresome. It is the only fight in these films won by being annoying on purpose.
