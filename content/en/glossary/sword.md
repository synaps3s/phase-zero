S.W.O.R.D. stands for Sentient World Observation and Response Department. In the
comics it is a counterterrorism and intelligence agency dealing with
extraterrestrial threats to world security, described as the space-based
counterpart of S.H.I.E.L.D.

In these films it arrives with *WandaVision*, where it is the organisation
parked outside the town with the anomaly, and where its acting director is doing
something with Vision's body that he has not told anyone about.
