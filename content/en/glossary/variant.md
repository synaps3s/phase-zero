---
title: Variant
aliases: ["variants"]
oneLine: Another version of a person from another timeline, who is genuinely
  them and genuinely not the one you have been following.
---

The word does a lot of work. It means the person is recognisably the same
character, with the same name and often the same face, and that nothing they
have done in their own life is shared with the version you know.

It is also an accusation. The organisation that coined it uses it to mean
somebody who has stepped off the path they were supposed to be on, which is a
crime in its system.

:::detail{title="A word with two owners"}
The audience uses it to mean an alternate version. The bureaucracy inside the
story uses it to mean an offender. Both readings are live at once, and a lot of
the tension in the series that introduces it comes from that gap.
:::
