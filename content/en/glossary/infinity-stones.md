---
title: Infinity Stones
aliases: ["Infinity Stone"]
oneLine: Six objects that each control one fundamental part of how the universe
  works, and that together let their holder do anything at all.
---

Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time and Soul. Six of them, one each, and any one
is enough to make its holder the most dangerous thing in a solar system. The
story spends ten years introducing them one at a time in films that appear to
be about something else, which is why the audience is ahead of the characters
for most of a decade.

Their function in the writing is to be the reason a villain would bother. Any
one Stone is a weapon; all six together are a single decision, taken by one
person, that nobody can appeal.

:::detail{title="Called something else in the comics"}
They are based on the Infinity Gems of the comics, and the films rename them.
Reference works describe the six as embodying and controlling essential aspects
of existence, which is a larger claim than any single film makes about any
single Stone.
:::
