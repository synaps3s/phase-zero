---
title: The Destroyer
aliases: ["Destroyer"]
oneLine: An Asgardian automaton kept for threats the guards cannot handle, and
  turned on Thor by his own brother.
---

The Destroyer is an automaton used by Odin to stop threats such as the Frost
Giants. Loki later sends it to Earth to attack Thor, in the small New Mexico
town where Thor is living as a mortal, and Thor destroys it once his powers
are returned to him.

It is a suit of armour with nothing inside it, which is why it is listed here
rather than among the characters: it has no will of its own and does whatever
the person holding Asgard tells it to do. That is also the only reason it
matters. The Destroyer is the first thing in these films to make the point that
Asgard's weapons are only ever as reasonable as whoever is on the throne.
