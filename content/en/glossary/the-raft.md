The Raft is a maximum security prison designed to hold super-powered people,
located underwater in the Atlantic Ocean, with Thaddeus Ross as its warden.

It exists in this catalogue for one scene. Sam Wilson, Wanda Maximoff, Clint
Barton and Scott Lang are sent there for helping Steve Rogers and Bucky Barnes
evade capture, in violation of the Sokovia Accords. *Civil War* ends with half
its heroes in cells built for them by the other half.
