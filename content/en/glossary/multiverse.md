---
title: Multiverse
aliases: ["multiverses"]
oneLine: The idea that this is not the only version of reality, and that other
  versions of the same people are living slightly different lives elsewhere.
---

Until 2021 there was one reality and the story happened in it. After 2021 there
are many, and the films start crossing between them.

What that buys the storytellers is a way to bring back characters from separate
productions, to undo things without cheating, and to raise a threat that cannot
be punched. What it costs the audience is certainty: once anything can be from
somewhere else, "who is this" becomes a question the films have to keep
answering.

:::detail{title="Why it appeared when it did"}
It solves a practical problem as much as a narrative one. Different studios had
been making films about the same characters for twenty years with no way to
connect them, and this is the device that made those two decades usable.
:::
