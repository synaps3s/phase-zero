---
title: The Nine Realms
aliases: ["Nine Realms"]
oneLine: The nine worlds Asgardian cosmology counts, one of which is ours.
---

The Nine Realms are the set of worlds the Thor films use as their map. Among
them: Asgard; Jotunheim, an icy planet and the home of the Frost Giants, where
Loki was born; Muspelheim, a fiery realm and the home of Surtur; Nidavellir, a
disk built around a dying star where the Dwarves forge weapons; Svartalfheim,
wreathed in permanent darkness and ruled by the Dark Elves; Niflheim, which
contains the realm of the dead; and Midgard, which is another name for Earth.

Knowing that last one is enough for most purposes. When a character in these
films says Midgard, they mean here.
