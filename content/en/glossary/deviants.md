---

The Deviants are the product of a series of experiments carried out by the
Celestials, alongside the line that produced the Eternals. In the comics they
are described as largely hostile and distrustful, of other species and of each
other.

In *Eternals* they are the assignment. The Eternals are placed on Earth to
remove them, which they do over several thousand years, and the film's first
turn is the discovery that the assignment was not what they were told.
