---
title: Kree
aliases: ["Kree Empire"]
oneLine: A militarised alien civilisation from the planet Hala, technologically
  far ahead of Earth and at war with a species it drove off its own world.
---

The Kree arrive in the story as a warrior culture with a fleet, an artificial
intelligence in charge, and an absolute certainty that they are the ones being
attacked. They are introduced through the person of a soldier who believes all
of that and turns out to have been given the wrong file.

They matter beyond their own films because they are the story's first
demonstration that an interstellar power can be simply wrong, at scale, for
centuries, and still be winning.

:::detail{title="Where they appear"}
Reference works record the Kree across the television series
*Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* and the films *Guardians of the Galaxy*,
*Captain Marvel* and *The Marvels*. They are native to Hala, in the Large
Magellanic Cloud.
:::
