The Sentinels are mutant-hunting robots, usually depicted as the X-Men's
antagonists. On film they appear briefly in *X-Men: The Last Stand* and *X-Men:
Apocalypse*, and prominently in *X-Men: Days of Future Past*.

That film is the reason they matter here. The future half of it is a war the
mutants have already lost, and the machines that won it were funded because one
person was killed in 1973. The whole plot is an argument about where a policy
starts.
