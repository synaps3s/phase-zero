---
title: Pym particles
aliases: ["Pym particles", "Pym particle"]
oneLine: Particles that change the distance between the atoms in a thing, which
  is how anything in these films gets smaller or larger.
---

Pym particles are described as extradimensional subatomic particles that reduce
or increase the distance between atoms, allowing whatever they are applied to
to shrink or grow. Hank Pym created the formula. They appear as a liquid in
vials, red for shrinking and blue for growing.

They are worth knowing because they are load bearing far outside the Ant-Man
films. They are how the Avengers get to the Quantum Realm, which is how they get
to 2012, which is how *Endgame* happens.
