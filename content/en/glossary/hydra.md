---
title: Hydra
aliases: ["HYDRA"]
oneLine: A terrorist organisation that began inside the Nazi war machine and
  survived it by growing inside the agency that was built to stop it.
---

Hydra starts as the science division of the Third Reich, run by a man who took
an early and imperfect version of the super soldier serum. That would be enough
for one film. What the story does instead is keep it alive, quietly, for
seventy years, inside S.H.I.E.L.D.

The motto is the design brief. Reference works record it as "cut off one head,
two more shall take its place", which describes both the organisation's
structure and the reason it cannot be beaten by winning a fight.

:::detail{title="The name"}
It is borrowed from the Lernaean Hydra of Greek myth, the creature that grew
two heads for each one cut off. In the comics the organisation was taken over
and turned into a neo-fascist crime syndicate by Baron Wolfgang von Strucker,
who appears in these films as well.
:::
