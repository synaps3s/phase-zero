---
title: The Snap
aliases: ["the Snap", "Snap"]
oneLine: The moment half of all life in the universe was erased, and the five
  years the survivors spent living with it before it was undone.
---

It is the hinge of the whole first run. One antagonist succeeds completely, the
story does not undo it before the credits, and the next film opens with everyone
still living in the aftermath.

The five years matter more than the event. Films set afterwards keep meeting
people who were gone and came back to a world that had moved on, and that gap is
where most of the emotional consequences live.

:::detail{title="The dates"}
Reference material places the erasure in 2018 within the story and the
restoration five years later in 2023. That gap is why some characters visibly
age between films and others do not.
:::
