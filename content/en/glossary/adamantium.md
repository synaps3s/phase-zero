---
title: Adamantium
aliases: ["adamantium"]
oneLine: A metal that cannot be broken, which is what one of these films bonded
  to a man's skeleton.
---

Adamantium is described as an extremely strong metallic element, found in
Celestials, and present as an alloy in Wolverine's skeleton, Deadpool's katanas,
X-23's claws and Lady Deathstrike's nails. Reference works place it above
vibranium: more indestructible.

It is the Fox films' central object in the way vibranium is Wakanda's. Logan's
claws are bone until a programme fuses the metal to him, and the operation is
shown as torture rather than as an upgrade, which is the only reason the
character works.
