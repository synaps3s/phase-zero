title: The Avengers Initiative
oneLine: A plan to keep a list of remarkable people and call them if the world
  needs it, which is the reason any of these films connect.
aliases: ["Avengers Initiative"]
---

The Avengers Initiative, originally called the Protector Initiative, is the plan
to gather superheroes from various backgrounds, described as a group of
remarkable people, to protect Earth. Nick Fury started it in the 1990s and
renamed it after Carol Danvers's call sign, Avenger.

It is the mechanism this whole catalogue is built on. In 2011 Fury sends Natasha
Romanoff to assess Tony Stark for it, which is why she is in *Iron Man 2*. Every
post-credits scene in the first phase is somebody being told the initiative
exists.
