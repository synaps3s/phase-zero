---
title: Incursion
aliases: ["incursion", "incursions"]
oneLine: What happens when the wall between two universes wears through and
  they hit each other, which ends at least one of them.
---

An incursion is defined as a universe-ending event: the barriers between two
universes erode, the universes collide, and one or both are destroyed. It is
usually caused by travelling between universes, or by tampering with them.

This is the single most important term in the current run of films. It is the
reason moving between universes is not a free adventure, and it is the bill
attached to every multiverse story since. When a film shows you two skies at
once, this is what it is showing you.
