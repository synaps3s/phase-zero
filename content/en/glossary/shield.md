---
title: S.H.I.E.L.D.
aliases: ["SHIELD"]
oneLine: The international agency that watches for threats no ordinary
  government is equipped to see, and that assembled the Avengers before anyone
  agreed it should.
---

S.H.I.E.L.D. is the machinery underneath the first several films. It has the
budget, the aircraft, the files and the authority to walk into a private
laboratory, and it uses all four constantly. The Avengers exist because a
director of this agency decided they should, without asking permission from
anyone he answered to.

The story then spends a film taking it apart. What looks like an
over-powerful institution turns out to have been hollowed out from the inside
for seventy years, which is a more interesting problem than the one the
audience thought it was watching.

:::detail{title="What the letters stand for"}
The acronym has been expanded differently over the years. In the comics it
originally stood for Supreme Headquarters, International Espionage and
Law-Enforcement Division. In the films and the live-action series it is
Strategic Homeland Intervention, Enforcement and Logistics Division.
:::
