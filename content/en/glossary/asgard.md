---

Asgard is described as a small, flat planetary body and the home of the
Asgardians, one of the Nine Realms. It is destroyed by Surtur.

That last sentence is unusual for this kind of story and it is the point of the
third Thor film. Asgard is presented for two films as a golden place worth
defending, and then the film asks what it was actually built on, and answers by
letting it burn. What survives is a boat of refugees, and a line that says
Asgard is not a place.

:::detail{title="Where the survivors go"}
They settle in a fishing village in Norway, previously called Tonsberg, which
is renamed New Asgard and is later recognised as a city-state. It is where
*Endgame* finds Thor, and where *Love and Thunder* leaves Valkyrie doing
paperwork.
:::
