---
title: Sacred Timeline
aliases: ["Sacred Timeline"]
oneLine: The single approved version of history that an organisation outside
  time was keeping, by deleting every version that tried to branch off it.
---

For most of the run there was one timeline and nobody said so, because nobody
knew there was an alternative. This is the name given to it once the alternative
turns up.

Calling it sacred is doing a lot of work: it frames the deletion of every other
possible history as protection rather than as murder, which the series that
introduces the term spends six episodes taking apart.

:::detail{title="What the sources actually say"}
The term appears in reference material about the series in quotation marks and
without a definition, which is worth knowing. What is documented is what the
organisation does, not a formal account of what the timeline is. That gap is in
the material, not in this entry.
:::
