---
title: Phase
oneLine: A numbered block of releases, the way a long-running series is divided
  into seasons.
---

Phases are a publishing convention rather than something anyone inside the story
would recognise. They tell you roughly when a film belongs and which other films
were made around it, and they group into larger arcs.

They are useful for orientation and misleading for viewing order: a phase is not
a self-contained story and starting at the beginning of one will not spare you
the previous one.

:::detail{title="How they nest"}
Reference material describes releases as grouped into Phases, with three phases
making up a Saga. That is why you will see both words used about the same films,
and why "Phase Four" and "the Multiverse Saga" overlap without meaning the same
thing.
:::
