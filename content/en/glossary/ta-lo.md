---
title: Ta Lo
aliases: ["Ta Lo"]
oneLine: A hidden realm reachable from Earth only at certain times, inhabited by
  creatures from Chinese mythology.
---

Ta Lo is a mystical realm inhabited by Chinese mythological creatures, among
them a dragon known as the Great Protector, the fenghuang, the shishi, the
hundun and the qilin. Thousands of years ago the Dweller-in-Darkness attacked
it and was sealed away behind the Dark Gate by the people of Ta Lo and the Great
Protector.

It is the place Xu Wenwu believes is holding his dead wife, and the voice he
hears from behind the gate is the film's whole trap.
