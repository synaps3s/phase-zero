---
title: Mutant
aliases: ["mutants"]
oneLine: A person born with a genetic difference that gives them abilities,
  usually surfacing at puberty, and usually noticed by other people before
  they notice it themselves.
---

The mutant premise is the oldest political idea in this material and the most
direct. Nobody chooses it, nobody earns it, and it arrives in adolescence in
front of an audience. Every X-Men film is built on the difference between that
and the way other people in this catalogue acquire what they can do, which is
by accident, purchase or ambition.

Reference works describe the trait as the X-gene, and the population as
sometimes called the next stage of human evolution, a claim the material itself
treats as contested rather than settled.

:::detail{title="A word that was contractually unavailable"}
For years the two studios splitting these characters agreed that neither would
point at the other's property. The films made by Marvel Studios could not use
the word mutant or name Magneto; the Fox films could not call the same
characters Avengers.

That is why some entries in this catalogue are careful about a word that other
entries use freely.
:::
