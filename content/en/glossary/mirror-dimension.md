---
title: Mirror Dimension
aliases: ["mirror dimension"]
oneLine: A copy of wherever you are standing, which sorcerers can fold and
  break without anything happening to the real place.
---

The Mirror Dimension reflects the surroundings in different directions without
affecting the real world, which is why sorcerers use it for training and for
containing threats. It is the reason a chase through New York can bend a whole
street sideways and leave the traffic beneath it untouched.

The Ancient One uses it during the Battle of New York. Stephen Strange uses it
against Kaecilius, against Thanos, against Peter Parker, and against Wanda
Maximoff, which is a fair summary of how his decade goes.
