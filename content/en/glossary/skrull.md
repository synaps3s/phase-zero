---
title: Skrull
aliases: ["Skrulls"]
oneLine: An alien species that can take the exact shape of any person it has
  seen, introduced as an invading force and revealed to be refugees.
---

The shapeshifting is the obvious thing about them and the least interesting.
What the films actually do with the Skrulls is use them to make a point about
who gets described as an invader: they are introduced through the eyes of the
people who burned their planet, and the reversal is the whole structure of the
film they first appear in.

Once you know, everything about their first scenes reads differently, which is
the definition of a twist worth having.

:::detail{title="Where they appear"}
Reference works place their MCU debut in *Captain Marvel* in 2019, and record
later appearances in *Spider-Man: Far from Home*, the series *WandaVision*,
*What If...?* and *Secret Invasion*, and the film *The Marvels*.
:::
