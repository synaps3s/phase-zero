---
title: Wakanda
aliases: ["Wakandan", "Kingdom of Wakanda"]
oneLine: An East African country that has been the most technologically
  advanced nation on Earth for centuries, and has spent that time convincing
  everyone it is one of the poorest.
---

Wakanda's whole position rests on a deception maintained across generations.
The country sits on the world's only significant supply of vibranium, has used
it to build medicine and transport and defence that nothing else on the planet
can match, and has hidden all of it behind a projection of subsistence
farming.

The films treat the decision to hide as a live argument rather than settled
wisdom. It kept Wakanda from being colonised. It also meant watching the rest
of the continent be taken apart from behind a wall, which is the accusation
that the story cannot answer.

:::detail{title="The comics version has gone further"}
In the comics the country has at times been styled the Intergalactic Empire of
Wakanda, with territory outside Earth. Nothing in these films goes there.
:::
