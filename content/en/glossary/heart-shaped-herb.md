---
title: Heart-shaped herb
aliases: ["heart-shaped herb"]
oneLine: A Wakandan plant changed by vibranium, eaten in a ceremony by whoever
  becomes Black Panther, and the reason the title is not just a job.
---

The herb is a Wakandan plant enriched by exposure to vibranium, which gives it
a glowing purple colour. It is taken in a ceremony by each new Black Panther and
grants them enhanced abilities. It also allows the person who takes it to speak
with the dead, on the Ancestral Plane.

That second property is what makes it more than a power source. Every Black
Panther film uses it as a conversation with a parent, and the plot of the first
turns on the fact that a new king can destroy the supply.

:::spoiler{title="What happens to it"}
On becoming king, N'Jadaka orders the remaining stock burned. One is saved by
Nakia and used to heal T'Challa. By the second film the herb is gone, which is
why the succession there is a scientific problem rather than a ceremonial one.
:::
