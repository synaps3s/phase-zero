---
title: Kamar-Taj
aliases: ["Kamar-Taj"]
oneLine: The place in Kathmandu where sorcerers are trained, which Stephen
  Strange reaches because he was looking for a doctor.
---

Kamar-Taj is the headquarters of the Masters of the Mystic Arts, in Kathmandu,
Nepal. Stephen Strange goes there looking for something that will fix his hands
after a car accident, which is not what the place is for.

Its library is where most of the trouble in these films starts. It holds the
Book of Cagliostro among many others, and both *Doctor Strange* films begin with
somebody taking pages out of a book they were told to leave alone.
