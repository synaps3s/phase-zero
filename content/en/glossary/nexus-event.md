---
title: Nexus event
aliases: ["nexus event", "nexus events"]
oneLine: The moment a person does something they were not supposed to do, which
  starts a second version of history running alongside the first.
---

A nexus event is a significant moment where somebody deviates from their
predetermined path on the Sacred Timeline, creating a new branching timeline.

It is the unit the Time Variance Authority works in. Every case they open is
one of these, and the series is built on the discomfort of the idea: if there
is a path you are supposed to be on, then somebody wrote it, and the agency
that enforces it has never asked who.
