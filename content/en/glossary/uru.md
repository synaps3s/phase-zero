---
title: Uru
aliases: ["uru"]
oneLine: The metal Asgardian weapons are made of, which can only be melted by
  the heat of a dying star.
---

Uru is described as a metal with magical properties, used by the Dwarves to
forge Mjolnir, Stormbreaker and the Infinity Gauntlet. It is forged in the
furnaces of Nidavellir because only the heat of a dying star is enough to melt
it.

It is a small term that explains a large amount. It is why Thor's hammer is not
simply a hammer, why the same forge made the glove Thanos needs, and why a
sequence in *Infinity War* consists of holding a star open by hand.
