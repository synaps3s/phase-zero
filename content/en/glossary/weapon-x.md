title: Weapon X
aliases: ["Weapon X"]
oneLine: The programme that turned captured people into weapons, and the reason
  Logan does not remember most of his life.
---

Weapon X is a government genetic research project that turned willing and
unwilling beings into living weapons for covert work. It captured mutants and
experimented on them to enhance their abilities, and it also worked on ordinary
humans.

The part that matters on film is the adamantium bonding process, written by
Barry Windsor-Smith in 1991, in which the metal is fused to a skeleton. It is
where Logan's claws stop being bone, and both *X-Men Origins: Wolverine* and
*Deadpool* are stories about people who came out of that building.
