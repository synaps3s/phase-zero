---
title: Pruning
aliases: ["pruning", "pruned"]
oneLine: The word the time agency uses for deleting a person or a timeline,
  which is not quite the same as destroying it.
---

Pruning is the Time Variance Authority's method of erasing branched timelines,
objects or variants judged to be a threat to the Sacred Timeline. It is done
with reset charges and time sticks.

The detail that matters is what it is not. Pruned targets are not destroyed:
they are sent to the Void, at the end of time, where they are meant to be eaten
by a creature called Alioth. The agency's clean administrative verb has a place
attached to it, and the series eventually goes there.
