The Winter Soldier programme was a top secret Hydra project started by the Nazi
scientist Arnim Zola in the 1940s. It took soldiers, brainwashed them, and
enhanced them with a recreation of the super soldier serum, producing assassins
who were kept in cryostasis between missions. Each had a set of codewords,
recorded in a book, that could be used to activate him.

The book, and the words, are what *Civil War* turns on. They are also what
*The Falcon and the Winter Soldier* has to undo, slowly, in a language Bucky
Barnes does not get to choose.
