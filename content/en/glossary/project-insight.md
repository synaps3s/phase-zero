---
title: Project Insight
aliases: ["Project Insight"]
oneLine: Three flying ships and an algorithm that decides who is going to be a
  threat later, and shoots them now.
---

Project Insight was a secret S.H.I.E.L.D. operation begun in response to the
Battle of New York. Three advanced Helicarriers would patrol the Earth, using an
algorithm to evaluate people's behaviour, identify future threats and eliminate
them with satellite-guided guns. It was led by Alexander Pierce.

It is the best premise these films have used, and the reason *The Winter
Soldier* still reads well: the plan is pre-emptive killing at scale, and the
argument the film has about it happens before anyone finds out who is really
running it.
