---
title: Sokovia Accords
aliases: ["the Accords", "Accords"]
oneLine: A United Nations agreement placing the Avengers under a panel that
  decides when they are allowed to act, which splits the team in half.
---

The Accords are the story's answer to a question it had been avoiding for four
films: who authorised any of this. A group of people with the ability to level
a city has been deciding for itself where to go, and after Sokovia the United
Nations proposes a panel that decides instead.

What makes the argument work is that both sides are right about something. One
side has seen what happens when nobody is accountable. The other has seen what
happens when the people signing the orders have an agenda, which they have,
twice, in the previous two films.

:::detail{title="What the document actually does"}
Reference material describes the Accords as establishing a United Nations panel
to oversee and control the team. Not to disband it, and not to punish it: to
decide when it deploys.

That distinction is the whole disagreement. Nobody in the film objects to being
answerable. The objection is to somebody else holding the decision about when
to go.
:::
