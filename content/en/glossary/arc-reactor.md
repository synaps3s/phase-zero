---
title: Arc reactor
aliases: ["arc reactor"]
oneLine: The power source in Tony Stark's chest, designed by his father, and the
  object every Iron Man film is really about.
---

The arc reactor was originally designed by Howard Stark and Anton Vanko as part
of an attempt to reproduce the Tesseract's energy. Their sons each built it
again independently. Tony Stark made two: a large industrial one to power the
Stark Industries building, and a miniature one in his chest.

The small one starts as a magnet keeping shrapnel out of his heart and becomes
the thing that powers the suit. It is the clearest object in these films: a
weapons manufacturer's son, keeping himself alive with his father's unfinished
idea.
