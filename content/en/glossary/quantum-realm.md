---
title: Quantum Realm
aliases: ["quantum realm"]
oneLine: A universe underneath our own, smaller than atoms, where the usual
  rules about space and time do not apply.
---

The Quantum Realm is what you reach if you keep shrinking past the point where
shrinking should stop. Reference works describe it as a subatomic universe that
exists outside space and time, reachable only through subatomic particles or
through magic.

Two things in these films depend on it. Janet van Dyne went subatomic in 1987
and was stranded there for thirty years, which is the plot of two Ant-Man
films. And time behaves differently inside it, which is the reason the Avengers
can use it to reach 2012 in *Endgame*.

:::detail{title="It is also somebody's address"}
The Council of Kang exiled Kang the Conqueror to the realm, which is where he
is when the van Dyne family arrive. So the same place is a lost mother, a time
machine and a prison, depending on which film you are watching.
:::
