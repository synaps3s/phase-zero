Oscorp, or Oscorp Industries, is a multinational corporation founded by Norman
Osborn. It first appeared in *The Amazing Spider-Man* number 37 in 1966.

It is the single most useful term for anyone watching the Sony Spider-Man films
in any order, because it is the connective tissue. Norman Osborn runs it. Otto
Octavius works on its behalf. Curt Connors is its head scientist. Max Dillon is
its electrical engineer. Harry Osborn inherits it. Four different villains in
two different film series all come out of the same building.
