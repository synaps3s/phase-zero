---
title: Vibranium
aliases: ["vibranium"]
oneLine: A metal that absorbs impact instead of transmitting it, found in one
  country and responsible for most of what makes that country unusual.
---

It is first named on screen by Howard Stark, who describes it as stronger than
steel, a third of the weight, and completely vibration absorbent. He has enough
of it to make one shield and no more, which is the point of the scene: the
metal is scarce because there is exactly one place on Earth it comes from.

That place is Wakanda, and everything the country is able to do rests on
having a supply of it that nobody else knows about. The shield, the suits, the
medicine and the trains are all the same fact seen from different angles.

:::detail{title="Where it comes from"}
Reference works describe vibranium as mined only in Wakanda and associated with
two characters above all others: Black Panther, whose suit is made of it, and
Captain America, whose shield is an alloy of vibranium and steel.

A second form, called Anti-Metal, exists in the comics and does not appear in
these films.
:::
