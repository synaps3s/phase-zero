The Chitauri are an alien race created for the Ultimate Marvel comics in 2002,
taking the role the Skrulls play in the main continuity. They were adapted for
the films, and the film version was successful enough that it was written back
into the comics.

In these films they are the invasion of *The Avengers*, and they matter mostly
for what they leave behind. Their technology, scavenged from the streets
afterwards, is what Adrian Toomes builds his flight suit and his business out of
in *Spider-Man: Homecoming*. The Battle of New York is the event the next ten
years of this catalogue keep referring back to.
