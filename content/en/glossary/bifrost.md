---
title: Bifrost
aliases: ["Bifrost", "Rainbow Bridge"]
oneLine: The bridge Asgardians travel on, which reaches from their own world to
  any of the others and leaves a burn mark where it lands.
---

The Rainbow Bridge runs from the centre of Asgard to its edge, connecting the
royal palace to Himinbjorg, where the Bifrost is generated. Thor destroys it in
2011, and Heimdall later repairs it using the power of the Tesseract.

Its practical function in the films is transport between the Nine Realms, which
is why the first Thor film can strand its lead on Earth simply by breaking it.
The circular pattern it scorches into the ground is one of the few visual
signatures these films reuse for twelve years without changing.
