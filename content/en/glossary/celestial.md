---
title: Celestial
aliases: ["Celestials"]
oneLine: Cosmic beings on a scale where a planet is a component rather than a
  place, and where creating life is a manufacturing step.
---

Celestials are the largest thing this material has. They predate the story,
they operate on timescales that make the Infinity Stones look recent, and their
interest in inhabited worlds is not hostility. It is process.

They are also the point where the films stop being about people who can punch
harder than each other. A Celestial cannot be fought in the ordinary sense, so
the films that involve them have to find something else for their characters to
do, and what they usually find is a decision.

:::detail{title="Where they appear"}
Reference works record Celestials in the live-action films
*Guardians of the Galaxy*, *Eternals* and *Thor: Love and Thunder*. They also
note a change from the comics: the MCU turns Ego the Living Planet into an
alleged Celestial, which he is not in the source material.
:::
