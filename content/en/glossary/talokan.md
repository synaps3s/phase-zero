title: Talokan

oneLine: An underwater kingdom in the Gulf of Mexico, hidden for four hundred
  years, whose people are called the Talokanil.
---

Talokan is an underwater kingdom ruled by Namor and populated by the Talokanil.
Its people were Yucatec, and they went under the water in the sixteenth century
after a vibranium-enriched herb saved them from smallpox and made the surface
unliveable for them.

It exists in this catalogue as the mirror of Wakanda: a second nation built on
the same metal, which made the same decision to hide, and which has now watched
the other one stop hiding.
