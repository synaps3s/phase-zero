title: The Void
aliases: ["the Void"]
oneLine: The place at the end of time where everything the time agency deleted
  actually went, and where most of it is still lying around.
---

The Void, also called the Void at the End of Time, holds the remains of every
alternate universe the Time Variance Authority pruned. It is patrolled by
Alioth, a storm-shaped entity that consumes what arrives.

It is also the series being honest about its own premise. An agency that
protects one timeline by deleting the others has to put the others somewhere,
and the show goes and films it: a landfill of possible worlds, with the
survivors living in the wreckage.
