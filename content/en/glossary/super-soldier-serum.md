---
title: Super Soldier Serum
aliases: ["super soldier serum", "the serum"]
oneLine: The formula that turns a person into the strongest version of a person,
  which worked twice and has been going wrong ever since.
---

The serum was developed by Doctor Abraham Erskine to enhance a human being to
the peak of human capability. It was given to Johann Schmidt, who became the
Red Skull, and to Steve Rogers, who became Captain America. After Erskine's
death, numerous other versions were made with varying degrees of success.

That last clause is the whole story. Hydra used a version on Bucky Barnes.
Bruce Banner's attempt to recreate it produced the Hulk. The Weapon Plus
programme produced Emil Blonsky. John Walker takes a vial of it in a stairwell.
Every one of those is a film or a series arguing about the same thing: the
serum amplifies whoever takes it, and nobody has ever been able to agree on how
to pick that person.
