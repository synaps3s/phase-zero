---
title: Time Variance Authority
aliases: ["TVA", "Time Variance Authority"]
oneLine: A bureaucracy that exists outside of time and monitors the timeline,
  deleting anything that departs from the version it protects.
---

Its people process the erasure of entire lives as paperwork, in an office with
a queueing system. That tone is deliberate: the horror is not that they are
cruel but that they are bored.

Understanding what they are for is the single most useful thing a newcomer can
carry into the films that follow, because those films assume it.

:::detail{title="Where the name comes from"}
It is usually shortened to three letters, and the material rarely expands it.
The full name is stated in reference material as the Time Variance Authority.
:::
