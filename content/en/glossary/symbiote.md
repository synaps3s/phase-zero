---
title: Symbiote
aliases: ["symbiotes", "symbiotic"]
oneLine: A living extraterrestrial substance that bonds to a host, gives them
  abilities, and edits their personality while it is there.
---

A symbiote is not a suit and not a possession. Reference works describe the
bond as forming a single entity out of two, one that amplifies whatever the
host already is and pushes at whatever they already want. That is why the same
creature makes one host into a reluctant vigilante and another into a serial
killer with better equipment.

The species has a name, Klyntar, which the films almost never use.

:::detail{title="Where they appear"}
The first symbiote reaches cinema in *Spider-Man 3*, in the Raimi continuity.
Venom becomes the lead of his own film in 2018, in a separate one. Carnage
follows in 2021 and further symbiotes in 2024.

Three of those films are in three different universes, which makes the symbiote
the piece of this material that has travelled furthest without a shared story
carrying it.
:::
