# Credits

Not every contribution arrives as a pull request. Some of the most useful work
on this project has been somebody reading a page carefully and saying what is
missing from it, which is harder than it sounds and easier to overlook when the
commit log is the only record.

This file is that record. It is for people whose work is in the catalogue
without their name being on a commit.

If you are here and would rather not be, or would rather be listed differently,
open an issue or say so wherever you said the rest of it, and it will be changed
without discussion.

---

## Austin Canady

Discord: `austinc2006`

Austin has read this catalogue more closely than anyone outside the project,
and a large amount of what is in it now is there because he asked where it was.

- Noticed that Danny Rand, Hector Ayala and Angela del Toro were missing
  entirely, which was the first sign that the character coverage was thin
  rather than merely incomplete.
- Sent a list of roughly ninety characters the catalogue did not have. Working
  through it took the roster from 123 people to 236, and every group in it
  turned out to be a real hole rather than a matter of taste: the Netflix street
  level, the Fox mutants, the Asgardian court, the cosmic tier, the monsters,
  the Marvel Television agents.
- Pointed out that several entries on that list were groups rather than people,
  which is why the Hand, the Warriors Three, the Black Order, the Eternals, the
  Runaways, Cloak and Dagger, the New Mutants and the Inhuman Royal Family are
  now recorded as organisations.
- Asked why the Spider-Verse films had no characters when the films themselves
  were already catalogued, and made the argument for why they belong: Miguel
  O'Hara naming Earth-199999, and Donald Glover reprising his role from
  *Spider-Man: Homecoming* in *Across the Spider-Verse*. Both are now recorded,
  with sources.
- Said the emblems were too basic and that too many of them looked the same.
  He was right. It led to finding a real bug, where two systems were writing to
  the same custom property and knocking every generated mark out to black, and
  then to rebuilding the generator so that a character without an authored glyph
  gets a mark that is actually their own.
- Kept going: Emma Frost, the First Class, the Wesley Snipes Blade, the
  resistance from *Days of Future Past*, the rest of the Inhuman Royal Family,
  and the rest of the agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.

None of this arrived as a complaint. All of it arrived as a list, which is the
most useful thing a reader can send.

## How to end up in this file

Report something that is wrong or missing, anywhere: an issue, a discussion, a
message. If it changes the catalogue, it belongs here. Contributions that come
in as pull requests are already credited by the commit history and do not need
a second entry, though nobody will be turned away for wanting one.
